/* The 10 body trait blocks of the nutrition mod (Plan 13 Task 16, spec section 3.1, ruling 13-25; ruling 13-46's thin pairs lifted in Task 10c): each a
   same-name restatement of vanilla's character_trait_definition, replacing it. The creation costs are Angus's choice
   (spec section 11): Overweight -6, Obese -10, Underweight -6, Very Underweight -10; Weak, Feeble, Stout, Strong,
   Weight Gain and Weight Loss keep vanilla's. The four muscle blocks keep vanilla's Strength XPBoosts (Plan 13 Task 17
   fix 1): applyTraits then lands the native level in the picked band, and the creation handler infers the pick from
   that level. No other block carries XPBoosts, no block carries GrantedTraits, no block is a profession trait, and
   the description keys are the mod's own. A thin pick is fat-only, so Weak and Feeble may be taken with Underweight
   and Very Underweight (Plan 13 Task 10c, Angus 2026-10-15: "Lift the exclusions"). Underweight, Very Underweight
   and Overweight show the mod's names, Overly Lean, Gaunt and Overfat (the ids stay vanilla's: the axes read body
   fat, not weight).
   Weight Gain and Weight Loss keep the exclusions their removed grants implied (Weight Gain: Underweight, Very
   Underweight, Emaciated, Athletic, Fit; Weight Loss: Overweight, Obese, Very Underweight), written two-way on the
   restated blocks; Emaciated, Athletic and Fit are not restated, so those three pairs sit on Weight Gain alone (the
   server validation checks exclusions in both directions). */
