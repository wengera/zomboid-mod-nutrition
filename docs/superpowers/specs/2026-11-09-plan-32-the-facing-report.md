## 1. What the record said before any code

Two research reads in Plan 31's workspace and one offline read of F17's frames settled the mechanism before the probe was built. The jar read (`facing-jar-research.md`, 29 claims): the engine writes the aimed player's facing three times per update after `OnPlayerUpdate` (the movement facing when unaimed with a non-zero input vector, the aim facing from the mouse whenever `isAiming()` is true and an aim key counts, the force-aim included, and the post-update handing the forward direction to the animation), no Lua event sits between the aim write and the post-update, so `faceThisObject` under the held stance is overwritten every update and the body faces wherever the OS cursor sits; unaimed and standing the write holds and the body turns at about 3.4 degrees an update; `setTargetAndCurrentDirection(x, y)` is the one public call that snaps the body angle itself; a melee press never reads `isAiming`. The workshop read (`facing-mods-research.md`): no mod in the corpus drives a local player's melee; the Horse Mod's input holds are the only precedent. F17's frames (PZA/1.10 carried the float `bodyAngle` since Plan 31 Task 2, which no grade read): on the 363 aiming frames with a zombie within 1.5 tiles the body sat within 22.5 degrees of its bearing under the meet's swings (88 of 88) and shoves (154 of 161) and broke on the ladder's stance legs (53 of 102, the body held at one world angle while the bearing moved) and at point-blank flee turns, where the wounds fell.
