# Plan 32 — the facing (design)

Date: 2026-11-08. Owner: Angus; written by the controller under the standing direction of memories `combat-facing-first`, `finish-actions-gain-distance`, `window-broken-glass`, `fast-exploratory-trials` and `drive-toward-full-survival` (Claude decides, prototypes and iterates; the harness rules bind every boot; a trial is not evidence, P31-T).

## 1. The problem

Angus, watching F17 live (2026-11-08): the character keeps scampering away from combat, and in the combat stance it still does not face the zombie it fights; the facing is the main priority, solved by exploring and experimenting; then the coward fix; and a window is checked for broken glass before a climb.

What the record shows (Plan 31's workspace: `facing-jar-research.md`, `facing-mods-research.md`, `visual-review-f17.md`, the scratch read of F17's frames):

- **The engine writes the aimed player's facing from the mouse on every update.** Three writers run after `OnPlayerUpdate` and before the post-update that hands the facing to the animation: the movement facing (unaimed, a non-zero input vector, the harness's auto-walk included), the aim facing (`isAiming()` with an aim key counting, the force-aim included: the mouse direction), and the post-update itself. No Lua event fires between the aim write and the post-update. So while the stance is held, `faceThisObject` is overwritten every update and the body faces wherever the OS cursor sits; the cursor is never driven (P28-F).
- **F17's frames bear it out.** On the 363 aiming frames with a zombie within 1.5 tiles, the engine's own `bodyAngle` sits within 22.5° of the zombie's bearing under the meet reflex's swing step (88 of 88) and shove step (154 of 161), and breaks on the ladder's own stance legs (53 of 102 within 22.5°, 37 over 45°, the body held at one world angle while the bearing moved) and on flee turns at point blank (the first wound, seq 1613-1621: an `attack`, then a `flee:many` steer at 0.18 tiles). The visual pass read the character squared on the zombie in none of its 13 close shots. The frames' `dir` string (45° steps) hid all of it, and the grade's facing reading (0 points off on every press) was wrong by construction.
- **What the engine lets Lua do.** Unaimed and standing, `faceThisObject` holds and the body turns at about 3.4° an update (`setTurnDelta` scales it). `setTargetAndCurrentDirection(x, y)` is the one public call that snaps the body angle itself; from `OnTick` it holds for that frame's render and for a press sent in that `OnTick`. `DoAttack` and `pressedAttack` never read `isAiming`: an unaimed press swings on the last facing written. One unproven aimed-without-mouse posture exists: `setBlockMovement(true)` with `setIsAiming(true)` and the force-aim off. The joypad and NPC routes are closed; `setIgnoreAimingInput` turns the aim off; `setIgnoreInputsForDirection` has no facing reader.
- **The coward.** F17 fled on 389 lines, 51.8 of 222 game minutes (`strain` 158, `held` 80, `unreachable` 46, `pair` 36, `many` 29), fled at point blank, and ran care actions in a start-flee-restart loop (15 rips sent, 1 done).

## 2. Goal and success criteria

The goal: the character faces the zombie it fights, by the engine's own body angle, at every press and through the stance, and the wounds that followed a facing break stop; then fewer flees and finished actions; then no laceration at a window.

Success, read in trials and then in one evidence session: (1) the facing error (`bodyAngle` against the target's bearing) at every press under 15° at the median and under 30° at the 90th percentile, and through a held stance under 30° at the 90th percentile, against F17's stance legs (37 of 102 over 45°); (2) the two metrics (P24-Q kills, P28-M hits per kill: the frames' reaction runs and the union) no worse than F17's 10 kills and 0.4 landed attacks a kill on the arena and the street; (3) flee minutes under half of F17's 51.8 of 222 with no flee at a zombie within `KEEP_R`; (4) a care action started with a zombie near ends done or is replaced by one distance leg and then done, never fled and restarted; (5) no climb through a window with glass.

## 3. Approaches considered

- **A. Probe first, then adopt (chosen).** A harness probe act runs each candidate facing mechanism for N ticks against a world point or a zombie, logging the engine's facing getters every tick; a zombie-free trial driver runs the six experiments of the jar read in one or two boots; a score tool ranks the mechanisms; the winner goes into the meet reflex and the stance skill; combat trials follow. Cost: one harness change (a schema bump) and one driver before any behaviour changes. Why: Angus asked for experiments, the mechanisms differ in what they break (the aim posture, the walk), and a probe settles which engine write wins per tick, which no combat trial can read cleanly.
- **B. Adopt the snap straight away.** Put `setTargetAndCurrentDirection` into the reflex and the stance and trial it in combat. Faster by a day; but if the snap does not hold through the render (the jar read's open item: `ModelInstance.Update` may turn the angle again on the render path), a combat trial cannot tell a facing failure from a planner one.
- **C. Drop the aimed stance.** Fight unaimed: `faceThisObject` holds, the press swings on it, and the stance's cost (the mouse facing) is gone with it; but the aim posture is what makes the walk a strafe and the shove ready, and its loss is a behaviour change to measure, not assume. It is one of the probe's modes, not the plan.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The facing ring (PZA/1.11) and the probe act

The frame's `me` gains the engine's facing getters at float resolution, read in the same tick as `bodyAngle` and `lookAngle`: `fwdAngle` (`getDirectionAngleRadians`, the forward direction as the update left it), `twist` (`getTwist`, degrees), `aimAngle` (`getAimVector(Vector2)`'s direction when its length is above 0, else null) and `autoWalk` (`isAutoWalk`). The schema bumps to 1.11 (`pza1.json` edited, `python tools/pza_schema.py --write`; the generated Lua, the Python tables and `tools.json` never hand-edited); `grade_x1` and every frozen reader keep working on 1.10 logs (the fields null before 1.11).

A new act type `probe` (`args_probe`: `mode`, `targetId` or `x`,`y`, `ticks`, `k`, `vx`,`vy` for a back-pedal, each optional but one target), run by a Lua reflex skill `PZAgent_FacingProbe.lua` in `OnTick`: for `ticks` ticks it applies the mode's calls toward the target and emits one `progress` line a tick carrying the ring (`fwdAngle`, `bodyAngle`, `lookAngle`, `twist`, `aimAngle`, `isAiming`, `isForceAim`, `autoWalk`, the bearing `b` to the target, and the step), then `done` with the summary (ticks, the error's median and 90th percentile for body and look, the hold share under 30°). The modes, the jar read's experiments: `read` (the current stance: force-aim held, `faceThisObject` each tick), `snap_aimed` (the stance plus `setTargetAndCurrentDirection(cos b, sin b)` each `OnTick`), `unaimed_face` (force-aim off, `faceThisObject` each tick), `unaimed_snap` (force-aim off, the snap), `block_aim` (force-aim off, `setBlockMovement(true)`, `setIsAiming(true)`, `faceThisObject`; released with `setBlockMovement(false)` and `nullifyAiming()`), `ignore_aim` (the stance with `setIgnoreAimingInput(true)` for the leg) and `turn_k` (`unaimed_face` with `setTurnDelta(k)`, the read value restored at the end). A mode with `vx`,`vy` adds the steer skill's auto-walk along the vector at the stance's walk, so the back-pedalling stance is probed too. Every member is index-checked on the first tick (not loaded rejects the act); a zero vector is never passed to `setForwardDirection` (the jar read's claim 16); every flag the mode sets is restored on every exit path, the melee's `finally` form. Kahlua rules (CLAUDE.md § 5) apply; `luabalance`, `kahlua_lint`, `bus_inventory --check`, `pza_schema --check` and `hotpath_lint` gate the commit.

### 4.2 The facing trial

`testing/agent/drivers/x_facing.py` with `testing/agent/driverlib/facing.py` (its own lib started from `trial.py`'s): the arena fixture (`arena-east`), no waves, the character teleported to the arena's square; the aim vector read from the first frames gives the cursor's bearing, and the target point is set 6 tiles from the character at 90° off it (so the mouse facing and the target facing differ by a quarter turn, the error Angus sees); each mode in the list runs `ticks` (default 60) standing and again with a back-pedal vector, in a fixed order, 2 s of rest between; a `--zombie` flag spawns one zombie (`createhorde2`, count 1) at the target bearing for the press modes (`snap_aimed`, `unaimed_snap` with a `DoAttack` on the last tick, the press leaf read); the run ends at the list's end or the wall cap (P31-T's 568 s default). The driver's pre-boot is the trial loop's (doctor, the memory read, no game Java, the staged profile, the arguments refused statically), and it imports `xlib` and starts its own lib. `tools/facing_score.py` scores a run into `testing/trials/facing.md` (one row per mode and movement: ticks, body error median and p90, look error median and p90, hold share under 30°, `isAiming` share, the press outcome if any, three words of what was seen), under the trials log's rules (a row names the booted commit, never cited). Tests: the lib's mode table and the schema's `args_probe` agree (a static test), the scorer on a recorded fixture, the driver's argument refusals static.

### 4.3 Adoption

The ruling after round one names the mechanism (or the pair: one for the stance, one for the press). It goes into `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua` (the `face`, `faceAt` and `holdStance` writes) and `PZAgent_Stance.lua` (`faceOf`), each tick in `OnTick` as the probe did, with the leaf `look_off` kept and `body_off` (the body angle against the bearing at the press) added to the press leaf; the planner's crowd and chaser rules unchanged. `grade_x2` (`agent.base.grades.x2`, `grade_x1`'s readings under its base plus the facing readings: the error at every press and through every stance leg from `bodyAngle`, by act step, the hold share, the breaks as runs with the action before each) replaces the frozen `facing` block's 45° points. The offline read of F17 by `grade_x2` is the baseline row of the trials log's facing table. Then combat trials under `x_trial.py` (lone, pair, street) with the scorecard, two rounds, the ladder with the adopted facing against the ladder at `066c4336`.

### 4.4 The coward fix

Angus's rules, each a planner rule with a replay test on F17's lines and a trial: (a) **no flee at point blank**: a zombie within `KEEP_R` is fought, whatever the flee reason (`many`, `pair`, `strain`, `held` included; the refuge ruling of Plan 31 stands for a named structure beyond it); (b) **the pair**: with two zombies within reach the nearer is faced and shoved first, the stance never leaves the nearer for the farther; (c) **one leg, then finish**: a care action (bandage, rip, wash, unwear) wanted with a zombie within `CARE_HOLD_R` plans one distance leg (a walk away to `CARE_HOLD_R` plus a margin, bounded in time), then finishes the action with the threat branch yielding to it unless a zombie closes within `KEEP_R`; a rip or an unwear the engine allows while moving is sent on the move (the `moving` rejection in `PZAgent_Tend.lua` read and lifted where the engine allows it); (d) **the `strain` flee** (158 lines in F17): its condition read against the endurance and the pain, and bounded to a real exhaustion (the pain-divided swing is the meet's business, not a flee's). Each rule lands behind a switch on by default, the goldens re-recorded under the named change, and the trials' flee minutes and hits per kill decide.

### 4.5 The window glass

A jar read of `IsoWindow` (the smashed and glass-removed getters, the laceration in the climb state) minted as rows; the harness's window reading gains the glass state (PZA/1.11's `window` object or the climb's refusal: `glass`); the planner's climb (`windows.py`, `door_held`'s `leave` at a window) refuses a window with glass and prefers the remove-glass action when the engine offers it to the player, else another way; a replay test and one trial through a smashed window.

One evidence session (F18: `f_goals_18.py` copied from `f_goals_17.py` and re-based; E25 stays unbooted unless Angus asks) under the full CLAUDE.md § 5, its grade `grade_x2`, the artifact flow, the visual pass with the facing table, the write-up with rows (the facing mechanism's first evidence; the coward rules' flee minutes; the window rule), the close.

## 5. Out of scope

The LLM goal source, the Dress maneuver's internals (beyond the care commitment of 4.4c), the HEAD_AIM calibration (it stays off until the facing holds), any OS cursor or virtual controller, Angus's own client.

## 6. Risks

The snap may not survive the render path (the jar read's open item): the probe's `read` mode against `snap_aimed` settles it before anything is adopted. The block-aim posture may fall out of aim on a state enter: the probe reads `isAiming` every tick. A mode may park the `-debug` client on a nil member: every member is index-checked first, and a probe act is sent only through the trial driver. The coward rules may raise the hits per kill: the trials read both metrics, and a rule that raises them is tuned, never kept for its flee minutes alone.
