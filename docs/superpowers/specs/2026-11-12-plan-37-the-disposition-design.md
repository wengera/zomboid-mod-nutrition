# Plan 37 — the disposition: design

Date 2026-11-12. Owner Angus; controller Fable. Opens on Plan 36's close (`2026-11-11-plan-36-the-engagement-report.md` § 5) and Angus's design conversation of 2026-11-12: what is a reflex and what is a decision, how a decision may overrule or interrupt the reflex loop, and his proposal that the reflexes take different priorities depending on a current disposition, which changes at a slower cadence and takes more information into account. Three layers, each with one job: execute (the Lua reflex), decide (the planner picks the disposition), intend (the model sets the objective).

## 1. Why

One-on-one through one-on-three fights are solved: Plan 36's round one read 0 hits a kill across the agent fixture, the street and five arena waves. Both evidence sessions since then died the same way, under a group of eight to eleven on the town walk that no trial ever drew. F22 (`#4769`–`#4777`): a near-unhurt character (health 95.9) with 13 kills met seven to eleven in a lane; over the last 23.9 s the planner sent six melees under the picker's pulls and six steers of the `many` run, the branch passing between them each time the standing count crossed `RETREAT_MAX_N` and back (four crossings in eleven decision lines), every send a supersede that reset the reflex's footwork, the run steering into a pocket the ring read five-eighths walled, and nine dragged the character down. F21 died under eight at a fence edge after the same alternation.

The planner was not making bad decisions. It was making reasonable decisions at reflex cadence and delivering each one as an interruption: today a decision reaches the reflex by killing the running skill and starting another, and the one owner of contact changes with every threshold crossing. The reflexes themselves (facing, the press on the moving back step, the step keyed to the next landing hit, the pile step-out, the crawler buffer, the ring turn) read the last few hundred milliseconds of physical state and are right; what is wrong is the seam between them and the layer above.

Angus's framing (2026-11-12): a thing is a reflex when its right answer is a function of the last few hundred milliseconds of positions, speeds, reach and the walls within three tiles; a thing is a decision when it needs what the reflex cannot see (the map beyond the ring, the body's health, the count behind a building, the weapon, a goal). The reflex layer never decides: it executes the table it was handed. Deciding is picking the table, and nothing else: which disposition, on what event, after what dwell, toward what heading. The model never touches the reflexes and never picks the disposition: it sets the objective. Neither layer reaches down past its neighbour.

The second thing F22 teaches is quantitative. Off F21's and F22's frames (a design read, not evidence) the character walks at about 2.1 tiles a second, runs at 3.6, and backpedals in the stance at 1.0; zombies walk at 0.55 (fast shamblers 1.45) and lunge at 1.3 to 1.6. The fighting retreat loses ground to every lunging zombie and holds three only because each one that lunges is pressed; against eight the backpedal is overtaken from the flanks whatever the cadence. A walk away outpaces every shambler and a run outpaces every zombie, and the group spreads by speed as it follows: the real technique against a crowd in this game is to walk away, let them string out, turn and kill the one or two who arrive first, and walk again. It is not running away, and it is what Angus's fighting retreat becomes when the count passes what a backpedal can hold.

## 2. Criteria (read on the trial rounds, P31-T; proven when the reading repeats across the crowd waves and the evidence fixture; one evidence session F23 at the end for the record)

1. **One owner in contact.** On every arm the scorer's `restarts` cell reads 0: no melee carrying `work` is superseded by a non-melee command while a standing zombie stands within `KEEP_R`; every change of disposition reaches the running reflex as a re-send that keeps its footwork state; every hand-over to the Flee goes through the reflex's `released` hand-back (or the `floor` one).
2. **The crowd waves** (§ 3.6: `horde8`, `horde10`, `town`, and the pocket arm): alive at the cap on every arm, hits a kill (the union) not above 0.1 on `horde8`, not above 0.2 on the others; the baseline on the Plan 36 build is run first and written as read (its deaths are the plan's control).
3. **The disposition cadence.** Disposition changes per minute in contact not above 3 on any arm, every change after its dwell, the sequence on the scored line.
4. **The string.** On `horde8` and `horde10` every turn-and-press episode read with the second-nearest zombie beyond `STRING_GAP` at the turn on at least 80 % of turns; no swing into the air; the kills on the horde arms not below 6 in 5 minutes.
5. **The ground.** No contact frame on the pocket arm with the ring reading five or more of eight headings closed while the disposition is `string` or `hold` and the heading sent was into a closed one; the string's heading the widest open line the map and the ring read, logged.
6. **The regression guards.** `trio`, `flank`, `crawler`, `pair` alive at 0 hits as before; `street-1` two trials alive; the `agent` fixture with the model two trials, 0 deaths, kills median not below 10, hits a kill not above 0.1.
7. Every wound in F23 traced to its cause in the write-up; the kills and the two hit counts in its table.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The contract (one skill in contact; parameters, never restarts; the safe release; the reflex's report)

- In contact (a standing zombie within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`, the character armed and off a room square, the cases the engagement owns today) one Lua skill runs: the melee with `work`. The planner changes its arguments by re-send, and the Lua keeps the footwork's whole state across any re-send carrying `work` while one with `work` runs, whatever the target (today only a same-target re-send keeps it: `keptOnResend` and `keepState` widen to every work re-send). A change of disposition is a re-send; a change of target inside a disposition is the reflex's own re-pin or a re-send that keeps the state.
- No planner row supersedes the engaged melee with a steer, a walk, an aim or any other command while a standing zombie stands within `KEEP_R`. The hand-over to the Flee (the `break` disposition) is asked for, never cut: the planner re-sends the melee with `release` true, the reflex finishes the press in flight and hands back `done handback why released` at the first safe frame (no press in flight and the nearest standing zombie beyond `K.RELEASE_SAFE_R` 1.3 tiles or the target knocked down), or after `K.RELEASE_MAX_TICKS` 90 whatever the frame, `safe` on the result; only then does the Flee's first leg go. The `floor` hand-back (the body's health at `FLEE_HEALTH`) stays immediate, as today.
- The reflex reports upward on its progress lines, which the planner reads as events rather than re-deriving the situation from the frame: the work leaf gains `disposition` (the one running), `pinned` (two or more standing inside `K.WORK_POINT_BLANK_R`), and keeps `cornered`, `n`, `gap`, `contact`, `clear`; the selector's transitions read these and the frame's slow facts (the count within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`, the health, the ring, the map).

### 3.2 The dispositions

A disposition is a table over a fixed set of primitives (face the attacker, hold the gap, press when the swing lands, step in on a stagger, shove as a knock-down, step out of a pile, buffer the crawler, turn along the ring, walk a heading): which are on, what wins when two fire, and their parameters. Two primitives sit above every table and are never off: the crawler buffer and facing a zombie already on the body (a test asserts both in every table). Plan 37 builds the three contact dispositions and the hand-over to the fourth; `tend` (the care chain riding the walk), `work` (a timed action under the clock) and `travel` (the objective's walk, Plan 38) are named in the log by the selector and keep today's execution.

| disposition | the reflex runs | enters (the selector) | leaves (the selector) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hunt` | the meet as today: the stance, the facing, the band step, the stagger in, the pile step-out, the chosen knock-down, no withdraw | one or two standing within `GROUP_ENTRY_R` | three or more closing or on (to `hold`) |
| `hold` | `hunt` plus the fighting retreat: the press on the moving back step along the withdraw heading (the picker's spread, retreat or cone heading, clamped by the ring), the step only as far as the next swing lands | `GROUP_LEAVE_N` (3) or more standing within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`, closing or on the character (Plan 33's leave entry) | under 3 within `GROUP_ENTRY_R + RETREAT_HOLD_R` for `DISPOSITION_DWELL_S` (to `hunt`); or `string`'s entry |
| `string` | the walk away along the string heading at a walk (`MO.want walk`, the stance released), at a run when the leader's recent speed exceeds `STRING_RUN_V` 1.6 tiles a second; the turn and the press when the leader's distance at the swing's landing is inside the reach and the second-nearest stands beyond `STRING_GAP` 2.5 tiles (else the walk goes on); after the press the walk again; the shove as a chosen knock-down; the crawler buffer; the stagger in off; the pile step-out when pinned; the target re-pinned to the nearest standing every `K.WORK_TICKS`; the melee's release radius lifted to `STRING_RELEASE_R` 8.0 tiles (a `lost` out of range only past it) | `STRING_N` (5) or more standing within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`; or `hold` with the leaf reading `pinned` for `STRING_PIN_S` 3 s with the count within `GROUP_ENTRY_R` not falling; or `hold` with the ring reading the withdraw heading and both neighbours closed | at most 3 standing within `GROUP_ENTRY_R` for `DISPOSITION_DWELL_S` (to `hold`); the health under `FLEE_HEALTH` (the `floor` hand-back, to `break`); no open walk heading in the ring or the map for `BREAK_CORNERED_S` 2 s (to `break`); the leader's recent speed above `STRING_RUN_V` while the gap to it shrinks on three reads at a run (to `break`: a sprinter cannot be strung) |
| `break` | the Flee as today (the steer along the ring's and the map's open heading, the door, the shelter, the window), entered only through `released` or `floor` | the health floor; the cornered read; the sprinter read; the Flee's own reasons off the engagement (unarmed, a breach, the sheltered rows) | the Flee's own ends; a standing count of at most `GROUP_LEAVE_N` with the health above `FLEE_HEALTH` for `DISPOSITION_DWELL_S` (to `hold` or `hunt` by the count) |

`DISPOSITION_DWELL_S` 2.0 s is the least time any disposition holds before the selector changes it, except the `floor` hand-back. Entry and exit thresholds differ on purpose (the hysteresis); every transition is logged `disposition {from, to, why, dwell}` on the decision line and read by the grade.

### 3.3 The Lua (PZA/1.16)

- The melee's `work` gains `disposition` ("hunt" | "hold" | "string", the schema's enum; absent reads `hunt`, the Plan 36 behaviour byte for byte, `test_lua_footwork.py`) and `release` (boolean, absent false); `K.WORK_*` constants for the string (`K.STRING_GAP`, `K.STRING_TURN_R` 0.5 tiles beyond the reach at which the walk turns, `K.STRING_RUN_V`, `K.RELEASE_SAFE_R`, `K.RELEASE_MAX_TICKS`), each with its source comment.
- `keptOnResend` and `keepState` keep the stance and the footwork's state on any melee re-send carrying `work` while one with `work` runs; the string's walk is a new step kind `string` of `walkAlong` with the stance released (`loosen`) and the gait latched by `MO.want`; the turn is `holdStance` plus the meet's press; the leaf carries `step_for string`, `turn_for leader {gap2}` on the turn's press, `disposition`, `pinned`.
- The release: `release` true ends the melee `done handback {why "released", safe}` by § 3.1's rule; the planner's `_contact_rule` lets `released` through to the ladder (as `floor`), every other end in contact re-dispatching the melee as today.
- The speeds: the character's own walk and run and the zombies' gaits above are read off the committed frames before the constants are written (the Plan 36 Task 4 method: a scratch read over F21, F22 and the trial logs; the numbers in the task's report and in the constants' comments; a probe trial confirms the string's gap grows at a walk against the `horde8` wave before the planner rule depends on it).

### 3.4 The planner (the selector; the deletions)

- A new module `planner/disposition.py`: the mixin `Disposer` on `Threat` (its state `Survive`'s, as `ThreatGroup` is) with `select(held)` returning the disposition for the tick by § 3.2's table, the dwell and the hysteresis, the transitions on the reflex's report (the engaged melee's last progress leaf) and the frame's slow facts, logged `disposition`; the constants in `policies.py`. The threat branch's open-ground rows in contact become: the selector's disposition, then the Fight's melee with `work` carrying `disposition`, the withdraw heading (`hold`: the picker's `_pull`; `string`: the string heading of § 3.5) and `release` (on a `break` transition), nothing else; `_engage_holds` holds every row in contact with no lapse (`_group_lapse` deleted); the `break` transition sends `release` and takes the Flee only after the `released` or `floor` hand-back.
- Deleted (CLAUDE.md § 3's reset rule: each test named in the task report): the leave's `run` leg above `RETREAT_MAX_N` and the constant, `SPENT_LEAVE`'s lowered threshold and `RUN_HOLD` (the string owns the spent and the many), `_group_lapse` and `engage_yield many`, `_pull_retreat`'s separate hysteresis (the hold's dwell is the selector's), the `many` run's branch for the engaged open-ground Fight (`Flee.open_run` stays as the `break` steer's ring read). The switches `GROUP_LEAVE`, `SPENT_LEAVE`, `RUN_HOLD` go with them; the T19 tuple shrinks and `T22_SWITCHES = ("DISPOSITION",)` names the one new switch, on by default, its off arm the Plan 36 branch for a golden that must keep its lines.
- Kept as today: the drag guard held under the engaged melee, the cornered Fight, the door fight, the doorway stand, the sheltered rows, the action clock, the care chain and its ride, the leave's door leg (now a `break` reason `leave`, taken only from `hunt` or `hold` through the release).

### 3.5 The ground (the string heading; the pocket)

- The string heading is the planner's: `World.open_line(heading_deg, tiles)` reads the map's known squares and blocked edges along a line (an unknown square open), and `Disposer._string_heading` picks, among the headings every 15 degrees within `STRING_TURN_DEG` 120 either side of the way away from the group's centroid, the one with the longest open line over `STRING_LOOK_R` 10 tiles, ties to the nearest to straight away, then clamps it to the ring's open headings as the pull is clamped (`_pull_room`); re-read every decision line, the heading re-sent only when it turns past `WITHDRAW_RESEND_DEG` (the existing sector rule). The map knows what the character has scanned; the ring knows three tiles; the pocket F22 died in was both in the map lines (the fence edge, the walls) and in the ring at its end, and neither was read past 45 degrees of the run's own heading.
- The `break` steer keeps `Flee._steer`'s turns but its ring read (`room_open_heading`) widens from `ROOM_OPEN_DEG` 45 to the whole circle less the half toward the group's centroid (Plan 36's thread (b)), and reads `World.open_line` first over `STRING_LOOK_R`.

### 3.6 The waves, the fixture, the scorer and the grade

- New waves in `driverlib/trial.py` (a per-spawn radius element, default `WAVE_RADIUS`): `horde8` (8 at 10 tiles, bearing 0, radius 2), `horde10` (10 at 10 tiles, radius 2), `town` (3 at 8 tiles bearing 0; 4 at 10 tiles bearing 90 after 10 s; 4 at 10 tiles bearing 200 after 20 s: the crowd that grows while the fight is on, F22's shape).
- The pocket arm: the `agent` fixture with the character teleported into F22's death lane (`--teleport 10691,10100`: the death square x 10691.56, y 10097.05 off the artifact's last frames, three tiles south of it, clear of the fixture's dormant zombie lying at 10689.5, 10095.5) and the `pocket` wave spawning five at each open end of the lane (headings 90 and 270, the lane's north-south axis read off the ring at the lane square: open to 3 tiles there, a wall within 2.5 on every other heading); no snapshot fixture: an idle 15-second snapshot loop there ends wounded, and `pzt snapshot` refuses a wounded end.
- The scorer's new cells (`CROWD_COLUMNS`, the era widening as before): `restarts` (melee act lines carrying `work` that ended `superseded` by a non-melee command while the leaf's last `contact` read true or the nearest standing stood within `KEEP_R`, per minute in contact), `disp` (changes per minute in contact, the dwell median, the sequence's counts by name), `string` (episodes, turns, turns with the second beyond `STRING_GAP`, kills inside, the gap to the second at the turn median), `release` (asked, handed back safe, handed back at the bound, taken by the Flee after neither).
- `grade_x7` (x6 under `base` plus the four readings above and `speeds` (the character's and the zombies' tiles a second by gait off the session's frames)), `f_grade_19.py`, `e_grade_28.py`; the F23 regrade test at the artifact commit.

## 4. Out of scope

`tend`, `work` and `travel` as executed dispositions (Plan 38 takes `travel` with Angus's `always-an-objective`, and `tend` and `work` move under the selector then); the model's goal choice against the group size the map lists; the pair's long street contact (at 0 hits a kill; a thread, not a defect); the door and shelter decisions inside a building; the bare-handed melee.

## 5. Risks

The selector can thrash as the branch did if its transitions are polled against thresholds; the dwell, the split thresholds and the event reads are the answer, and the `disp` cell is the test. The string's walk may be caught by fast shamblers at a walk (1.45 against 2.1 leaves 0.65 tiles a second); the run gait is the answer and the sprinter read the exit. A string that walks into a second group is the map's and the ring's to refuse: the heading reads every listed zombie, not the centroid alone. Widening `keptOnResend` keeps a stale footwork state across a target change; the state kept is the heading, the cadence marks and the clear count, every per-target read re-made on the first tick. The regression guards are the arena waves and the street; a reading the waves never covered is written as unproven, never as a regression.
