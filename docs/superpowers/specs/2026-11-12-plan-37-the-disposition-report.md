# Plan 37 — the disposition: close report (closing 2026-11-12)

The plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-plan-37-the-disposition.md`, spec `2026-11-12-plan-37-the-disposition-design.md`) opened on 2026-11-12 from Plan 36's close and Angus's design conversation of the morning: what is a reflex and what is a decision, how a decision may overrule or interrupt the reflex loop, and his proposal that the reflexes take different priorities depending on a current disposition chosen at a slower cadence with more information. ## 4. The rulings

P37-A (2026-11-12): in contact a decision changes the running reflex's parameters by a re-send, never by a restart; the hand-over to the Flee goes through the reflex's `released` or `floor` hand-back; the scorer's `restarts` cell is the test and read 0 on every row.
