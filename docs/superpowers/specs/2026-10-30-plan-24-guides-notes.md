## 1. Combat

### Stance and timing

- **Backpedal while attacking; hit at the weapon's edge of reach while backing away.** Page 1 ("Step backward to reset swing timing when additional threats close in"); forum 18 ("hit zombies at maximum range while backing away"; "walk backward while fighting rather than sprinting"); forum 21 ("keep backing up and swinging"); forum 22 ("walk backward while aiming"). *For the agent:* **agrees with** P24-N (the stance opens the fight, the backpedal inside KEEP_R 2.5, the swing in reach). Register: the backpedal is the force-aim plus the auto-walk vector, walking only (#4332, #4334).
- **A miss is not chased: back off at once, or shove.** Page 1 and the search digest of it ("If you miss, don't try to recover - backpedal immediately"); forum 18 (shove "when you miss attacks and need to reset positioning"). *For the agent:* **new** in part: the Fight reads no miss; after a done `attack` whose target's health did not fall, the next leg could be the backpedal (or the P24-AH shove) rather than a second swing at point blank. The no-progress stall (STALL_SWINGS) reads the same signal over several swings only.
- **Don't fight hungry, thirsty or with moodles stacked; "if they start piling up ... call it a day".** Page 1 (hunger and thirst "stack penalties on swing timing"; "Eat before multi-house clearing"); forum 20 ("If you are tired or exhausted. You will hit like a wet noodle"); forum 22 ("Avoid fighting while tired, hungry, or debuffed"). *For the agent:* **agrees with** the order threat → care → need, but the P24-X seek reads health and the weapon only. **New:** the seek's `_takes_on` could also refuse with a need over its threshold or the tired moodle (below).

### Tiredness, endurance and strain

- **Never fight tired; "treat orange Tired as a stop sign".** Page 4 ("Ridiculously Tired turns a shove into a funeral"); page 13 (Tired level 1: "Melee damage ... begin[s] to fall"); the fandom snippet (Tired above 70 % fatigue: 80 % less melee damage, above 80 %: 90 %, above 90 %: 95 %). Register: a melee swing's damage is × 0.5, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05 at tired moodle levels 1 to 4, and again at endurance levels 1 to 4, compounding (#4371); the tired moodle fires above 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9 (#2279); the stomp takes the same ladder (#2280). The snippet's ladder is the jar's shifted by one level (its "Tired" is the jar's level 2, × 0.2); the jar is right: the first tired level, above 0.6, already halves the swing. *For the agent:* **new.** P24-AG disengages at endurance level 1 but reads no fatigue; the night wants its sleep at TIRED_T 0.6, the same line, yet a fight sought at fatigue 0.65 swings at half damage. Rule it: no seek and no standing fight above TIRED_T, the night first.
- **Never fight to exhaustion; keep endurance for the retreat.** Page 2 ("Never fight to complete exhaustion"; "Fighting to exhaustion rather than preserving retreat endurance" among the common mistakes; its per-swing endurance percentages are unverified); page 13 (endurance level 1: melee damage, attack speed, climbing fall and trip risk rises). Register #4371 (× 0.5 at endurance level 1; −0.07 combat speed per level). *For the agent:* **agrees with** P24-AG's disengage at endurance level 1 (ruled, Task 27).
- **Muscle strain: weapons strain the arms, heavier weapons more, high Strength and skill less; rest relieves it; painkillers restore damage but not speed; alternate swings with stomps to spread strain.** Page 6; page 7 (strain × 2 for two targets hit and × 3 for three; "60% of the weapon's weight plus 120% of its endurance modifier"; shove 0.15, stomp 0.3; level 0 Strength 150 % to 50 % at 10). Register: #4368 (the shove's 0.15 and the stomp's 0.3 × (15 − Strength) / 10, the stomp to the right leg), #2140 (the (15 − Strength) / 10 factor: page 7's 150 % at 0 and 50 % at 10 match it), #4368-#4371 (pain over 10 divides the swing's damage). *For the agent:* **agrees with** P24-AG (stomp and shove preferred at arm pain ≥ 10, disengage at ≥ 20). **New:** the guides' remedy is rest (sitting) and painkillers; the goal layer's `Rest` could sit (no PZA action sits), and a painkiller is no `use`. Page 7's "the stomp's strain lands on the legs" is the jar's (#4368), so P24-AG's stomp preference does move the strain off the arms.
- **Weight hurts the fight: reduce load before combat.** The fandom snippet (heavy load: attack speed −0.14, trip chance up, speed down; unverified numbers); page 13 (heavy load level 1: "Movement, attacks, climbing ... begin to worsen; trip risk rises"); page 1 ("Overweight inventory kills more early players than zombie damage"). Register: −0.07 combat speed per heavy-load level (#4371), −5 knock-down chance per heavy-load level on a shove (#4373), the climb's fail chance −8 per heavy-load level (#4297). The snippet's −0.14 at "Heavy Load" is the jar's −0.07 per level at level 2, consistent if its first named level is the jar's 2; the jar is right. *For the agent:* **contradicts** in emphasis: the load branch drops only at DROP_AT 3 and LootHouse's cap is `load.max` × 1.0, so the character routinely fights at level 1 or 2. **New:** the seek drops spare weight to level 0 before it engages, or the loot caps under level 1.

### The shove and the stomp

- **The safe default loop is shove → pin → stomp; a downed zombie is easy to kill; stand on its head so it cannot rise.** Page 1 ("Stomping downed targets preserves weapon durability"); page 2 ("Stomp ... deals full damage with zero weapon durability loss"); page 16 (target "the chest or head rather than its feet, as the zombie can get up again"; at Strength 5 to 10 "only need to stomp once"); forums 19, 20, 22 ("push the others while standing on the downed zombies to keep them down"); page 17 ("If a survivor ... stands on a downed zombie ... [it] will be rendered unable to stand up", Build 41 wiki text). Register: the shove's knock-down chance 35 + 2 × Strength − 5 per endurance and heavy-load level − 1.3 per panic level (#4373), the stomp's damage (0.7-1.0 + 0.2 × Strength) × the shoes' stomp power, × 0.5 barefoot (#4374, #2196); a weapon's own floor attack multiplies its damage by max(5, its critical multiplier) (#4374). Standing on a fallen zombie keeping it down is not read in the jar. *For the agent:* **agrees with** P24-AH (stomp a fallen pin within 0.5 tiles; shove a second zombie inside KEEP_R). **New:** (a) a fallen pin anywhere within FIGHT_REACH is closed on and stomped, or struck with the weapon's floor attack (#4374's × 5 or more), never left to stand; (b) with a pair, shove the second down and keep to the first, stomping each in turn (forum 19's two-zombie recipe), which needs a fallen read on the zombie record (Task 27's brief names `isOnFloor`; the working tree's `pza1.json` read for these notes carries no such key).
- **Shove when a zombie closes inside the weapon's reach rather than panic-swinging.** The search digest of page 1 ("If zombies get too close, shove instead of panic-swinging"); forum 18. Register: a swing at a standing zombie nearer than the weapon's `MinRange` is turned into a shove by the engine anyway (#4372). *For the agent:* **agrees with** P24-AH; the guide adds that the shove is the *first* answer to a zombie at point blank, which today's cornered Fight answers with the swing.
- **Multiple targets: kill the standing ones before stomping the downed (unless one blocks the retreat).** Page 1 ("Target standing threats before stomping downed zombies"). *For the agent:* **contradicts** P24-AH's reading only where a standing second zombie is near: P24-AH already refuses the stomp "while another standing zombie is within the weapon's MinRange"; the guide widens that to any standing zombie closing. Keep P24-AH's guard, add the guide's: no stomp with a standing zombie inside KEEP_R.

### Doorways, fences and windows

- **Fight through doorframes; zombies queue poorly in narrow spaces; stand offset from the doorway.** Page 1 ("Fight through doorframes and stair bottlenecks"; "Stand slightly offset during doorway combat"; never fight "in open lawns"); forum 22 ("Window fighting provides safety advantages over open combat"). *For the agent:* **agrees with** the sheltered door fight (P23-AG: the reachable held threats in the doorway or inside), `_chokepoint`'s hold on a room or door square, and the flee to shelter. **New:** today a `many` reason flees whatever the ground; with three or four held and a known building's exterior door within reach, the guide's answer is to back into the doorway and fight the queue, one at a time, which the door fight then does. The drag-down guard (#4290: three standing attackers within 0.9 tiles) is exactly what a doorway prevents.
- **The fence trick: hop a low fence, let the zombies follow; they tumble over it and are killed on the ground; hop back.** Forums 18, 19 ("Lure them, let them hop over, stamp on head, jump back over yourself"), 20 ("Lure zombies over and through obstacles because they will trip, and that's a downed zombie"), 21 ("jump over fence, whack 3-4 of them while they are down"), 22 (not with more than six); page 17 (a fence "will take damage and eventually collapse"); the pzwiki snippet (a zombie vaulting a fence can lunge and knock the player down: stay off the fence line). Register: the player's climb at a walk never falls and costs nothing (#4296); a zombie's fall at a fence is not read. Build 41 forum advice, unverified on 42. *For the agent:* **new**, and the register's sessions call for it: the chain's first fights met one zombie across the same fence row or on its locked gate in E1-E4 and later (#4235, #4242, #4248, #4258, #4273, #4282, #4316), each marked unreachable after a reposition walk. Rule: a chasing zombie across a known hoppable edge is waited for in the stance one to two tiles back from the fence (out of the lunge), and struck or stomped as it lands; `seek_allowed` already keeps the seek from crossing.
- **Windows: open one at each end of a building, lure the zombies through, kill each as it climbs in.** Forum 21; page 17. *For the agent:* **new**, a base-building-free version of the doorway rule: a zombie climbing a window is a zombie at a chokepoint. Not worth building before the doorway rule.

### Groups

- **Fight one at a time; never more than one (or four) at once; split groups by drawing a few away.** Forum 20 ("never fight more than one zombie at a time"; "draw out a single zombie from a group by letting the first one get close enough to run towards you, and walk away from it"); forum 22 ("Never engage more than 4"; "Split larger groups by drawing only a few zombies at a time"); forum 21 ("separate them at least a bit to be 1 on 1"); page 1. *For the agent:* **agrees with** MAX_FIGHT 2, the pair rules and the `many` reason at four listed within 12 (P24-A (a)), and P24-AD's idle-zombie rule. **New:** the pull: with one chasing and idle ones beyond, walk away from the group so only the chaser follows, then turn and fight it out of the others' sight (P24-X engages one or two, but it closes on the target, pulling the fight toward the idle group).
- **The conga line: backpedal so the group follows single file.** Forums 18, 21. *For the agent:* **new**; a backpedal leg aimed so the held threats fall into a line behind the nearest (away along the nearest's bearing rather than the 1/distance sum) would be the stance's version. Lower value than the doorway, which makes the line for free.
- **Attacks from behind injure more.** Page 17 ("a higher chance of injuring them if the attack is from the rear", Build 41 wiki text). Not read in the jar. *For the agent:* bears on P24-G 5 (no look-around: the frames list every zombie whatever the facing, #4302) and the backpedal's facing; a jar read of the rear-attack rule would say whether the stance's facing matters to the defence roll.

### Weapon choice and condition

- **Blunt first: the crowbar is the best early-to-late keeper (1 condition per 70 hits), the bat moderate (1 per 20), spears fragile; carry a backup weapon; stomp to save durability.** Page 15 (its loss rates unverified); page 1 (blunt "high knockdown with good durability; recommended for learning"); page 2 ("Carrying backup weapons prevents mid-combat equipment failure"); forum 18 (blunt over knives). *For the agent:* **new.** The loot's `weapon` want and `spare()`'s protection read `items.WEAPON_RX` (`\.(BaseballBat|Hammer|Axe|Crowbar|Knife|Pan|Pistol|Shotgun|Rifle)\b`), which names guns the agent never fights with and misses the kitchen and tool weapons the guides name first (page 11: knives, pans, rolling pins; a `Base.HandAxe`, `Base.KitchenKnife`, `Base.FryingPan`, `Base.Pipe`, `Base.LeadPipe`, `Base.Shovel` or `Base.Machete` fails the regex's `\.` anchor or its list). Nothing ranks two weapons, reads condition, or keeps a second. A rank (blunt and reach first, condition read off the item) and one backup held would keep the character armed, which the fight's every rule needs (`unarmed` always flees).
- **Avoid guns early; one shot pulls the neighbourhood.** Pages 1, 2, 5; forum 18. *For the agent:* **agrees with** the melee-only planner; the regex's gun names are dead weight.

## 2. Movement

- **Walk, don't run; you can out-walk default zombies; run only when the street fills.** Forum 18 ("Your walking speed is faster than their default speed"; "Avoid sprinting entirely"); forum 19 ("walking is faster than the zombies speed ... break line of sight somehow and they lose attention"); forum 20 ("Walk, you can outwalk them, only run if the street is about to be filled"); forum 22 ("Never run or jog except briefly to escape"); page 5 (sprint footsteps carry "two or three blocks"; unverified). Register: walking drains no endurance below heavy-load level 3 (#2256), running drains at 5.2e-5 and sprinting at 4.55e-4 per update (#2251); zombie walk speeds are not read. *For the agent:* **agrees with** P24-O (never sprint). **Contradicts** in part: every flee leg runs (FLEE_GAIT); the guides would walk away from shamblers and run only from a group closing. A flee from a single chaser at a walk spends no endurance and keeps the stance's damage; unverified which zombie speeds the server spawns (the sandbox's `Speed`), so a live reading of a chaser's speed against the walk comes first.
- **Break line of sight to lose pursuers; corners, trees, buildings.** Forum 18 ("Break visual contact for 3+ seconds"; "Use building corners and trees"); forum 19; page 3 (leave the zone "10-15 in-game minutes before returning"; unverified); page 8 (cover behind cars, fences and walls "genuinely improves visual stealth now" in 42). Register: within its hearing range a zombie counts the player spotted without line of sight (#2310). *For the agent:* **new**, as the MrAtomicDuck notes found too: the flee steers by distance-weighted away vectors only. A leg whose end puts a wall or a building's corner across the line from the nearest chaser (`world.line_clear` over the kept map) would act on it; #2310 says it works only beyond the zombie's hearing range, so the leg must also gain distance. The met-ground memory (`World.met`, MET_TTL_MIN) is the "leave the zone" half and **agrees**.
- **Sneak (crouch) when undetected; stand up and walk once spotted.** Forum 19; pages 3, 8 (crouch "cuts movement noise by roughly 60%"; unverified). *For the agent:* **new**; no PZA action crouches. Low value against the kill metric (P24-Q): the agent seeks fights.
- **Fences: hop them at a walk; vaulting at a run trips.** Not in the written guides beyond the fence trick; the register settles it (#4296: no fall roll at a walk). **Agrees with** P24-G 4 and P24-O.

## 3. First aid

- **Treat the worst first: heavy bleeding, then lodged glass, then deep wounds, then routine bandaging.** Page 10. *For the agent:* **agrees with** the care branch's order (a deep wound first, then the longest bleeding time). **New:** glass ("Remove shards ... Leaving glass in place keeps damage and bleeding active") and the stitch for a deep wound have no PZA action; the window route (Task 24) can cut the hands, so glass becomes reachable.
- **Bandage immediately; any dressing stops the bleeding; a sterilised one lowers the infection risk.** Pages 1, 10. Register: any bandage stops a part's bleeding drains (#4298); a clean one halves the infection odds against none, a dirty one doubles them (#4299). **Agrees with** P24-G 2, P24-P (bandage beyond CARE_R under a threat) and P24-R (bandage while walking above the legs).
- **Change dirty bandages early.** Page 10 ("Change dirty bandages early; dirty dressings raise wound-infection risk"); page 1 ("Wash or change clothes after messy combat"). **Agrees with** the care branch's `replace` (a spent or dirty bandage replaced with a clean one).
- **Wash a dirty rag with water to reuse it; sterilise with alcohol, vodka or whiskey.** Page 10. Register: a dirty rag washes clean at any water source, tainted or not (#4301). **Agrees with** the `wash` step. **New:** disinfecting (a `Disinfectant`, alcohol wipes, liquor applied to the wound or the rag) has no action; given F2 drank a `Base.Disinfectant` 68 times (P24-F), a use for it is overdue.
- **A bite is fatal on default settings; bandaging it only stops the bleeding.** Pages 1, 10 (default "2-3 day mortality"). *For the agent:* **agrees with** treating a bite as a bleed; it means a bite's real cost is the session, so the guides' whole combat advice is bite avoidance. Infection rates quoted by the search digest (scratch 7 %, laceration 25 %, bite 100 %) are unverified.

Head and neck items (a hard hat at 70 bite) are the largest single gains the guide names.

## 7. Zombies' senses and behaviour

- **Sight first, then sound; zombies move toward noise by the direct route; hordes form from sustained noise.** Pages 5, 17.
