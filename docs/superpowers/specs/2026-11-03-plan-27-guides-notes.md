## 1. A lone zombie with a one-handed blunt weapon (a bat)

- **Rhythm, the common answer: shove it down, then stomp.** The B41 Steam threads agree that a single zombie is pushed to the ground and its head stepped on: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1608274347724974532 [pre-B41, Oct 2019] (push it down repeatedly, then a head attack once aligned); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3493131640467957685 [B41, Nov 2022] (push until it falls, stand on it and stomp); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1620599015870308177 [pre-B41, Dec 2017] (isolate, push, stomp).
- **Some guides swing first to weaken, then shove down.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5617721963182127526 [B41, May 2023] says to land strikes first to weaken it, then focus on knocking it down with shoves, and finish it on the ground. The unofficial https://projectzomboid.wiki/guides/combat-basics/ [unofficial, build unstated] gives a shove, a weapon hit, then a stomp on the prone body. https://lootlore-d2ce2.web.app/en/project-zomboid/guides/zombie-combat-guide [build unstated, May 2026] names a shove, swing and sidestep rhythm for a 1v1. **The guides disagree** on whether the swing comes before or after the knockdown; none says to swing on approach before any shove.
- **Several shoves may be needed before one falls.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623 [B41, Jul 2023]. The pzwiki Bare Hands page (snippet, https://pzwiki.net/wiki/Bare_Hands, build not shown) says a shove does no damage but can knock a zombie over, the knockdown chance and the prone damage being modified by strength and fatigue.
- **Hits a bat needs: no guide gives a number for B42.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3389534247553698666 [B41, May 2022] reports a shovel needing about 12 hits on a downed zombie against 1-3 unarmed stomps, explained by the stomp aiming at the head; the same thread says fatigue, exhaustion, hunger and arm wounds cut weapon damage by 50-95 % and slow the swing.
- **Stomp versus swing at a fallen zombie: stomp.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1620599015870308177 [pre-B41] (the space-bar stomp saves weapon durability); https://projectzomboid.wiki/guides/combat-basics/ [unofficial] (the boot on a fallen enemy kills as fast as or faster than a low-skill swing). pzwiki Bare Hands (snippet) says the stomp also keeps a downed zombie from getting up and knocks over one caught early in standing up. The only argument for the swing is weapon XP (a search summary, no page fetched).
- **The stomp's cost in B42 is muscle strain, not stated as endurance.** https://build42guide.wiki/survival/build-42-combat [B42, says verified against 42.21] gives a shove 0.15 strain and a stomp 0.3, and a swing's strain from the weapon's weight and endurance modifier, falling with weapon skill and strength. No guide read says how much endurance a stomp takes.
- **Distance and the lunge.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623 [B41] says to keep distance by backing away while aiming and to alternate attacks with pushes. https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3109141414213278310 [B41 beta, Jan 2021] says a zombie lunges after climbing a fence or a window and that the moment it lands is a chance to kill it in one hit. No page read gives a lunge distance in tiles.
- **The backpedal's role: always moving backwards while fighting.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3109141414213278310 [B41] (fight several at once by never planting your feet and choosing when to swing and when to backpedal); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787 [B41, Apr 2023] (shove, melee, back away; never stand still). https://projectzomboid.wiki/guides/combat-basics/ warns that backing into a fence is a fatal mistake.

## 2. Endurance and exhaustion

- **What drains it.** Running, sprinting and continuous fighting: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3821906444738708739 [B41, Mar 2023]; in B42, players report reaching High Exertion after about eight zombies, heavier weapons draining faster: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/595135824845105737 [B42, Dec 2024]. Fighting, running and pushing all tire the character: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787 [B41].
- **B42 adds muscle strain beside endurance.** Swings strain the arms, running the legs, over-encumbrance the back: https://gameluster.com/project-zomboid-build-42-guide-muscle-strain/ [B42, Jan 2025] (attack speed falls with strain; rest on the ground or furniture relieves it); https://pzfans.com/ProjectZomboidB42MuscleStrain/ [B42, names 42.20.4] (recovery by stopping the source, scaled by the sandbox Muscle Strain Factor; fight in bursts with recovery); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/596288191849433797 [B42, Oct 2025] (strain lasts longer than exhaustion; players alternate leg and arm attacks).
- **Recovery.** Standing still recovers slowly, sitting faster, furniture fastest: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3821906444738708739 [B41]. A B42 player reports about 100 real seconds of sitting per 300 of play: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/595135824845105737 [B42]. **No page read gives a rate for standing or walking.**
- **What the moodles do.** https://pzfans.com/moodles/ [B42, names 42.19] gives the levels Out of Breath (endurance at or under 75 %), High Exertion (50 %), Excessive Exertion (25 %), Exhausted (10 %), each lowering melee damage, attack speed, movement, blocking and climbing and raising the trip chance, with no percentages. https://pindrop.gg/project-zomboid/moodles [B42] words them as hard, barely, barely, barely able to swing. The numbers come only from a search summary of the fandom Moodles page (snippet, https://projectzomboid.fandom.com/wiki/Moodles, a B41-era wiki): melee damage -50 % at the first level, -80 %, -90 % and -95 % at Exhausted, attack speed down 7 % rising to 28 %. **Read these as B41 at best.**
- **What to do when exhausted mid-fight: disengage and rest, re-engage only 1v1.** https://pzfans.com/ProjectZomboidB42Moodles/ [B42, 42.19] says to break line of sight, sit or rest, and come back only to a one-on-one once you can run and swing again. https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623 [B41] says not to attack while fatigued and to retreat when the debuffs show. https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787 [B41] says a character too tired to fight gets grabbed. https://lootlore-d2ce2.web.app/en/project-zomboid/guides/zombie-combat-guide says to be above 50 % endurance before re-engaging. **No guide says to keep fighting a lone zombie while exhausted**; none says to keep moving rather than rest once nothing is near.

## 3. Groups

- **The threshold: one at a time; two to four at most while learning; more than three is a run.** https://lootlore-d2ce2.web.app/en/project-zomboid/guides/zombie-combat-guide (never two at once unless skilled); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787 [B41] (try not to fight more than one at a time); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623 [B41] (no more than 2-4 when learning); https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3109141414213278310 [B41] (a skilled player handles five by backpedalling). In B42, swinging into a line costs double strain for two hit and triple for three: https://build42guide.wiki/survival/build-42-combat [B42]. **The guides disagree on the number**; they agree that the count that matters is how many are in reach at once.
- **Splitting a group.** Walk backwards so they queue single file (the "conga line"): https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787 [B41]. Draw one out by walking away with it just in range while its companions fall behind: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5617721963182127526 [B41]. Retreat backwards when one notices you so it separates: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1608274347724974532 [pre-B41]. Run around obstacles to separate two: same thread. Doorways, fence gaps and windows as chokepoints: https://projectzomboid.wiki/guides/combat-basics/ [unofficial]. Fences and windows make a climbing zombie fall, a window being safer than a fence since nothing flanks: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623 [B41]. B42: pull small groups of 1-3 and finish with stomps: https://pzfans.com/ProjectZomboidB42MuscleStrain/ [B42].
- **When to turn and fight after a flee.** When the one drawn away is alone and its companions have fallen behind; disengage again if more arrive before it is dead: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5617721963182127526 [B41]. No guide gives a distance.
- **How far a walk-away must go.** No guide gives tiles. Zombies hold about a six-second memory after losing sight and sound: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3198117849826832802 [B41, Dec 2021] (break line of sight, turn about 45 degrees, keep walking, run only when about to be grabbed). Walking outpaces a shambler: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3493131640467957685 [B41]; https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3722819643270951762 [B41, Dec 2022] (lure them far at a walk, then lose them out of sight).

## 4. Bleeding and wounds

- **How fast.** No guide gives a bleed-out time for a bite. A neck wound is the fastest: one player lost half their health in about 30 seconds from a neck scratch: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5696507684082056624 [B41, Jul 2022]. https://pindrop.gg/project-zomboid/moodles [B42] gives the bleeding moodle by bleeding parts: one part, two, three, and four or the neck as "death imminent". https://pindrop.gg/project-zomboid/injuries [B42] gives bleed multipliers by part (x0.2-1.5) for scratch, laceration and bite.
- **Rags.** The pzwiki Rag page (snippet, https://pzwiki.net/wiki/Rag) says a rag stops the bleeding of a skin-breaking wound (scratch, bite, laceration, deep wound, glass and others) entirely. https://pzfans.com/how-to-get-and-make-sheets-in-project-zomboid/ [B42, 42.19] says cotton clothes rip into ripped sheets, a sheet into ten, and a dirty rag is washed back clean with water. **Which garments, how many rags, and whether a worn garment must come off first: no page read says.** https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/598518433314820035 [B42, Feb 2025] says B42 rips one garment at a time, slower than B41, with the crafting menu ripping several of one type at once.
- **Bandage, rag, dirty rag, sterilized.** The pzwiki Bandage page (snippet, https://pzwiki.net/wiki/Bandage) says a bandage turns dirty after a time set by the wound and First Aid, that **a dirty bandage left on still stops the bleeding but gives the wound the highest infection chance**, and that a sterilized bandage dirties at the same rate but heals faster. https://pzfans.com/first-aid/ [B42, 42.19] says to change dirty dressings early because they raise infection risk, and to sterilize a bandage with alcohol, vodka, whiskey or hot water. https://allthings.how/project-zomboid-how-to-deal-with-infections-wound-vs-knox-virus/ [build unstated, Aug 2026] says dirty dressings are a main cause of infection and should be changed as soon as they are soiled.
- **When to re-dress: the guides disagree with the players' testimony.** The guides say change when dirty (above). The players say each change of a bleeding wound costs blood: one survived a neck laceration at 1 hp by changing it again and again, and another left wounds in dirty bandages and let them infect because changing them all at once cost too much pain and blood: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5696507684082056624 [B41].
- **Treatment order.** Heavy bleeding first, then lodged glass or bullets, then deep wounds, then routine bandaging: https://pzfans.com/first-aid/ [B42]. A deep wound wants a suture before the wrap, glass wants removal first: https://gamerempire.net/project-zomboid-how-to-stop-bleeding/ [build unstated, Aug 2023, B41-era].
- **Disinfectant.** Alcohol wipes are the weakest disinfectant but multi-use; a disinfectant bottle is stronger; bourbon works worst and hurts: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3596716756124513370 [B41, Nov 2022] (and a search summary of the same claims). Rubbing alcohol, vodka, whiskey and wipes sterilize a bandage or clean a wound: https://pzfans.com/first-aid/ [B42]. Liquor works as well as medical alcohol: https://allthings.how/project-zomboid-how-to-deal-with-infections-wound-vs-knox-virus/.
- **Is a bite survivable?** The bleeding is; the Knox infection is not, at default settings. https://pindrop.gg/project-zomboid/injuries [B42] gives the default transmission chances as bite 100 %, laceration 25 %, scratch 7 %, and death in 48-72 game hours. https://pzfans.com/first-aid/ [B42] says bandaging a bite does not stop a fatal infection once transmitted. https://allthings.how/project-zomboid-how-to-deal-with-infections-wound-vs-knox-virus/ calls a bite a death sentence in 2-3 days. A wound infection (not Knox) is curable with care: same page.

## 5. Night

- **Hide, do not loot.** Night one is for inventory and planning, sleep if tired, not mandatory loot hours: https://pzfans.com/what-should-i-do-first-in-project-zomboid/ [B42, 42.20.4] (one closed room with water and food for the night; sleep over stimulants, since fatigue's melee and escape penalties turn a loot run fatal).
- **Visibility.** Lights through windows draw zombies: keep lights off and curtains closed: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3198115500361811671 [B41, Dec 2021]; https://pzfans.com/what-should-i-do-first-in-project-zomboid/ [B42]. No page read gives the night sight ranges.
- **Sleeping.** Upstairs, in a bedroom behind a closed door, windows boarded rather than just covered; a broken window's noise wakes slower than door banging: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3198115500361811671 [B41].
- **A first night with zombies outside.** No guide read addresses that case directly beyond the above: silence the house, close curtains, keep to one closed room, sleep if tired. **No guide says to fight outside at night.**

## 6. Windows and doors as escape routes

- **Leave by the far side.** Enter a building and leave by an exit on the opposite side, door or window; the zombies keep to the entrance they followed you to: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3722819643270951762 [B41].
- **Smashing versus opening.** Smashing a window and climbing through the glass can cut you; clear the glass with a weapon first: pzwiki Window and Broken Glass pages (snippet, https://pzwiki.net/wiki/Window, https://pzwiki.net/wiki/Broken_Glass), which also say glass shards on the ground cut a shoeless character and crunch loudly enough to alert zombies. A smashed window without cleared glass can give a deep wound (search summary of the same pages). Players break windows on purpose to get cut and train First Aid, which shows how reliably the glass cuts: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/2248929785831712839 [pre-B41, May 2020].
- **Noise.** A broken window lets the house's noise out and draws zombies from further: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3722820118824561532 [B41, Dec 2022]; zombies enter silently through an open, unbarricaded window: same thread.
- **Barricading.** Board windows for real protection; leave one exit window lightly barricaded or keep a sheet rope from an upper floor: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3722820118824561532 [B41]; https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1710690448229808961 [pre-B41, Jul 2018]. In B42 any climber can fall from a sheet rope after a grace period (search summary of pzfans sheet pages, not fetched).
- **The window as a fighting position.** A zombie climbing through a window falls and lunges after; the landing is a one-hit chance: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3109141414213278310 [B41]; window fighting is safer than fence fighting: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623 [B41].

## 7. Looting priorities for a day-one character on foot

- https://pzfans.com/what-should-i-do-first-in-project-zomboid/ [B42, 42.20.4], in its order: noise control and exits; a weapon and shoes; water (fill bottles and pots while the taps run); food; the first bag found; rags, bandages or ripped clothing; one closed room for the night.
- https://projectzomboid.wiki/items/early-loot/ [unofficial, B42, Aug 2026]: day zero a bag, water containers and one melee weapon; day one medical first (bandages, disinfectant, painkillers, tweezers), then shelf-stable food and a can opener.
- **The guides agree** on weapon, bag, water and food first and medical within the first day; **they disagree** on whether medical comes before food. Both name disinfectant or bandages explicitly; neither names protective clothing on day one.

## 8. Where the agent's rules and the guides part

- **The endurance hold keeps fleeing with nothing near.** The guides say to break line of sight and then *stop and rest* (sit for the fastest recovery), re-engaging only 1v1: https://pzfans.com/ProjectZomboidB42Moodles/, https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3821906444738708739. A flee that runs on with no zombie within 6 tiles spends the movement the rest needs.
- **The `many` flee runs to a building at two close.** The guides fight two or three by backpedalling into a single file and turn on the lead zombie once it is separated: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787, https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5617721963182127526. A flee kept running with one zombie or none near is the opposite of the draw-out tactic.
- **A fallen zombie is left to a flee's steer.** Every guide read finishes a downed zombie with the stomp, the downed one being the safe one: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1620599015870308177, pzwiki Bare Hands (snippet).
- **The agent re-dresses a spent dressing.** pzwiki Bandage (snippet) says a dirty dressing still stops the bleeding, and the Steam testimony says each change of a bleeding wound costs blood (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5696507684082056624); the guides' "change when dirty" is for infection, and they pair it with a disinfectant.
- **Swinging at low endurance.** The B41-era numbers (fandom snippet) put melee damage at half or less from the first exertion level, and the B41 thread https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3389534247553698666 at 50-95 % off when tired or wounded; a divided swing in that state is the swing the guides say not to make (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623: do not attack while fatigued).
- **Fences only climbed, never fought at.** The guides use a fence or a window as a trip: the climbing zombie falls and is stomped or one-shot on landing (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623, https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3109141414213278310). The agent's climb is correct as an escape; it does not wait on the far side for the fall.
- **Agreements, for balance.** No sprinting, combat in the stance with a backpedal, shove-then-stomp at a lone zombie, rags from cotton clothing when no bandage is held, shelter at night, and leaving a building by a far window all match the guides cited above.

## What iteration 19 should take

Ranked by the guides' weight behind each and the deaths and kills the write-ups tie to it; each is a candidate, not a ruling.

1. **Under the endurance hold, stand (or sit) still to recover once no zombie is within `DANGER_R`, and fight a lone one 1v1 when one comes**, since the guides say to break sight, rest, and re-engage only one-on-one (https://pzfans.com/ProjectZomboidB42Moodles/; standing recovers, sitting faster: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3821906444738708739).
2. **Stomp a fallen zombie within reach before any flee's steer when no standing zombie is nearer**, the finish every guide uses and the one that keeps it from rising (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/1620599015870308177; pzwiki Bare Hands, snippet), at a B42 cost of 0.3 strain (https://build42guide.wiki/survival/build-42-combat).
3. **End the `many` flee when one zombie or none stands within `DANGER_R`, and turn on the lead one**, the draw-out the guides describe (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5617721963182127526; the conga line: https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6790907198738264787).
4. **Leave a spent dressing on while its part reads no bleeding, and re-dress only out of a fight with a disinfectant in hand**, since a dirty dressing still stops the bleeding and each change costs blood (pzwiki Bandage, snippet; https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5696507684082056624; disinfect: https://pzfans.com/first-aid/).
5. **Put bandages, a disinfectant (wipes, a bottle, or liquor) and tweezers in the loot wants on the first day**, as both day-one lists do (https://projectzomboid.wiki/items/early-loot/, https://pzfans.com/what-should-i-do-first-in-project-zomboid/).
6. **Make no swing below the first exertion level (endurance under about 75 %); use the shove and the stomp there**, since the guides put the tired swing's damage at half or less and say not to attack while fatigued (thresholds: https://pzfans.com/moodles/; damage: fandom snippet, B41; https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3389534247553698666, B41), which also reads P26-L1's divided swing as the swing to drop.
7. **After a window or fence way out, hold one step back from it and stomp the zombie that falls through**, the window being the guides' safest fighting position (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/3109141414213278310; https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/6458775074330221623).
8. **After three failed shoves at a lone zombie, swing once at full reach and backpedal, then shove again**, the swing-to-weaken-then-knock-down rhythm some guides give (https://steamcommunity.com/app/108600/discussions/0/5617721963182127526; https://projectzomboid.wiki/guides/combat-basics/), and only above the exertion line of item 6.
