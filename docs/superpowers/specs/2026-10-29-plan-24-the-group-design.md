# Plan 24: the group — design

Angus (2026-10-28, standing): the design strives for a character that fully plays and survives the game, scripted instincts for its reflexes and LLM-driven actions for its broader decisions; no design-approval round; the minutiae are Claude's to explore, prototype, iterate and improve; every boot under CLAUDE.md § 5. Angus (2026-10-29): "keep going, boot F1 when ready" — read as the next LLM session (F2) on the next planner base.

## 1. Goal

A chain that lives past one session. Plan 23's close report (`2026-10-28-plan-23-survivor-report.md` § 7) names why none did: four of six survival deaths came with a group gathering on the character while a fight or a corner held it in place, and the first limit is perception — the group was listed at the 12-tile edge while the threat branch counted within 6 (#4268). The close review (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-28-plan-23-survivor/review-close.md`) adds four planner findings from F1's log: a `walk` corner overrides the `health` flee, the point-blank re-pin alternates between two zombies, an approach walk is sent at point blank, and the group steer runs on a room square with a way out.

## 2. Principles (Plan 23's hold)

Instincts before goals; the planner host-side, no per-tick server work from the agent; every grade versioned per pass; a driver frozen from its run, the next pass a new file; every live reading a row with its artifact; a brief's trace of a live log is a hypothesis the writer verifies (CLAUDE.md § 6). New here: **a decision golden** — before any rule or refactor of the threat plan, `Survive`'s decisions over the recorded inputs of the E5 and F1 artifacts are recorded as a golden (`testing/tests/pzagent/golden/survive-decisions-*.json`), and every later change to the plan re-records it under a named change with each moved decision attributed (CLAUDE.md § 6's golden-trace rule applied to the planner).

## 6. Amendment (2026-10-29, Angus after F2): the stance, the climb, wound care, the look-around

Angus, having watched F2's shots: the character should use the combat stance more, so it swings while backpedalling; it need not run when combat-ready against a non-overwhelming number, it can stand and fight when appropriate, backpedalling in stance; it should climb fences short or tall and not vault a short one unless necessary (a vault is dangerous); it should prioritise bandaging wounds, which needs a couple of clean rags, got by ripping clothes and washing the rags; it should occasionally spin to check behind, even while backpedalling in stance when its targets are not yet in range. Added work (ruling P24-G):

9. **The jar reads for the skills** (Task 6b, research): the combat stance (aiming: `IsoPlayer.setAiming`/the aim key, movement while aiming, the swing while backpedalling, the stance's speed), the explicit climb (`climbOverFence` for a low fence against the walk's own climb; the vault's trip and the danger Angus names; what `ISClimbOverFence` does), the bandage (`ISBandageAction`-equivalent: the bandage items `Base.Bandage`, `Base.RippedSheets`, `Base.RippedSheetsDirty`; `BodyDamage` parts' bleeding and the bandage's effect; the infection chance of a dirty rag), the rip (`ISRipClothing` on clothing: which items yield rags), the wash (`ISWashClothing`/`ISWashYourself`: a water source, the rag's dirtiness), and the look (the facing: `setDir`/`faceLocation`, how a turn reveals zombies behind: the client's own perception radius and the listing). Members, lines, rules, the proposed PZA/1.5 actions.
10. **PZA/1.5** (the Lua): `aim {targetId or bearing, vx, vy, seconds}` (the stance held facing the target or bearing while moving along the vector at the stance's walk, the attack landing while aiming: the swing on contact), `climb {x, y}` (an explicit climb at a hoppable edge, never the vault), `bandage {part or worst}`, `rip {itemId}`, `wash {itemId}` (at a water source within reach), `look {seconds}` (a spin through the four bearings); the frames' `me` gains `aiming`, `bleedingParts` (already `body.bleeding`), `rags {clean, dirty}`; each a skill with the harness's `accepted/progress/done/failed` shapes and tests.
11. **The planner:** the Fight stands against one or two with health above `FLEE_HEALTH` and the stance on, backpedalling in stance as a zombie closes (the `aim` action with the away vector), swinging in reach; the flee from groups as today; a `care` branch between `threat` and `need`: bleeding → `bandage` with a clean rag or bandage, no rag → `rip` a spare clothing item (never the worn essentials), dirty rags and a water source near → `wash`; the look-around: every `LOOK_EVERY_S` 20 s of idle or goal walking, and while backpedalling when no target is within reach, one `look`; the route planner prefers a climb over a detour and never a vault.
12. **The sessions:** F3 and the E6 chain read them live; the write-ups mint the rows.
13. **Water** (Angus, the same day): water is found as a container somewhere and filled at a sink, a toilet or the like in a home. The map's square record gains a water source flag (`water`: a sink, toilet, bath or well the character can take water from, read as the jar's `IsoObject` water members say); PZA/1.5 adds `fill {itemId}` at a water source within reach; the need branch's chain: drink a held filled container, else fill a held empty one at the nearest known source (a walk to it), else loot a container (`use` `water` only for a drinkable filled container; an empty one `container`); the `use` table of P24-F reads these.
14. **The sixth chain's first session** (E6, `e_day_6-20261029-102942`, 2026-10-29): the character died at game minute 605 after three fights; 11 stance legs of which 8 stood facing with a zero vector (the backpedal band of P24-M, within one tile of reach, almost never opened), the fights went to approach-and-swing, every flee ran at a sprint (13 sprint steers) and the health fell 100 to 17 before the first bandage, which the care branch could not send while a zombie was held. Angus, watching: "The character doesnt initiate combat in combat stance, doesnt backpeddle so zombies eventually get too close. Then he tries to sprint away and trips, you should avoid sprinting, running is plenty." Rulings (P24-N, P24-O, P24-P, superseding P24-M): a fight opens in the stance and keeps it; the backpedal is the stance's default whenever the target is inside `KEEP_R` 2.5 tiles (the swing lands in reach), standing still faces only a farther target; the planner never sprints (walk and run only, every leg); the care branch bandages a bleeding part whenever the nearest zombie is beyond `CARE_R` 4 tiles, a threat held or not, the bandage superseded if one closes inside `DANGER_R`. Task 12 builds them; the E6 write-up follows.
15. **The metric** (Angus, the same day): "time to death isnt a good metric right now, lets use zombies killed instead to iterate and improve as this has more bearing on actual survivability, you could just run away forever until you starve to death and that would be your best count otherwise." Ruling P24-Q: every session's headline reading is zombies killed (the death event's `zombiesKilled`, the fights' `targetDead` results, kills per fight and per standing fight); the write-ups and the iteration rulings rank by it; game minutes alive is the second reading; a session with no kill is a failure whatever its length.
16. **Bandaging while walking** (Angus, the same day): "you can bandage anything above the legs while walking". Ruling P24-R: a bleeding part above the legs is bandaged during a flee, a walk or a stance leg, without stopping; the Lua reads the vanilla action's stop-on-walk rule per part (`ISApplyBandage`'s `stopOnWalk`/`stopOnRun` and the body-part groups), lets a `bandage` on such a part run alongside the current moving leg (PZA/1.5.3: the leg keeps driving, the bandage queues; the result says `walking`), and keeps the standing bandage for the legs and feet; the care branch then needs the `CARE_R` gate only for a leg or foot part. Task 9 fix 5 (the Lua, with the read) and Task 12's follow-up (the planner) build it.
