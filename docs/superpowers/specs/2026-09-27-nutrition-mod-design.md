# Nutrition mod — design

Written 2026-09-27 against build `42.20.4` (jar `b0bbce05d5`), a dedicated-server multiplayer target, from the reference library in this repository and the thirteen research reports under `docs/superpowers/research/`. Sections 1 to 4 of the architecture were approved one at a time; sections 5 to 10 were written with the controller's defaults on Angus's instruction, and every default is listed once in § 7 for the re-review that closes the design phase. The spec is tracked while the program runs and deleted at its close; the pages, the registers and the mod are what stay.

## 0. How to read this

- A **ruling** is a decision Angus took; it is not reopened by an implementer.
- A **default** is a decision the controller took under the instruction to assume the sensible choice; § 7 lists them all and each is open at the re-review.
- A **gate** is an experiment or a jar reading a plan must land before the work that depends on it.
- Every number the mod ships cites a row of one of two registers: the claims register `docs/reference/claims.tsv` for what the game does, and the science register this program creates for what the evidence says. A number with no row does not ship.
- Register rows are referred to here by id in prose ("register row 1140"); the bracketed tag grammar is reserved for the pages.

## 1. Goals and non-goals

The mod is a realism nutrition overhaul for Project Zomboid Build 42 on a dedicated multiplayer server, released publicly on the Workshop.

Goals:

- Track about twenty-five nutrients beyond the vanilla four macros, with body pools, real depletion and repletion kinetics, and graded deficiency and excess states pinned to published evidence.
- Replace the vanilla energy and weight model with a two-compartment body of fat mass and lean mass, lean-mass-based resting expenditure, activity expenditure that scales with total mass, evidence-based partitioning of deficit and surplus, and strength capped by lean mass with strength memory.
- Drive hunger, thirst, endurance, fatigue, perception, movement and combat responsiveness, health and healing, mood and thermal tolerance from nutritional state, with a balanced diet at the top of every ladder.
- Rebalance the vanilla food records' macros from public-domain food-composition data and give every record a nutrient vector.
- Show the player symptoms first and numbers only when the character has the knowledge or the server allows it.
- Full unit coverage of the model, live server scenarios that integrate the model against the real server, and paired cross-side reads for everything that crosses the wire.
- No measurable performance degradation on the server tick or the client frame.

Non-goals for the first release: new food items, cooking-skill changes, farming or foraging changes, an animal or NPC nutrition model, single-player claims, and any dependency on a third-party mod.

## 2. Rulings

1. **Release target: public Workshop from day one.** No hard third-party dependency; MoodleFramework is optional and detected at runtime; the mod's own panel is the primary surface; sandbox dials and compatibility rules are in scope from the start.
2. **Hunger and thirst are driven by the model.** The vanilla stats are what the model writes, so other mods that read them keep working.
3. **Visibility: symptoms first, numbers gated** behind the vanilla Nutritionist trait or a server option.
4. **Default pace: real timescales at 1×**, with onset and severity dials.
5. **Architecture C: full takeover.** Vanilla nutrition off at the sandbox and every stat updater replaced through the `CalculateStats` hook on the server. The overlay shape (write after vanilla's tick) is kept as a sandbox-selectable fallback mode. The controller stated the compatibility cost (the hook is global and all-or-nothing) and Angus reaffirmed.
6. **Strength: own the Strength level.** A lean-mass ceiling lowers the level as lean mass falls and restores it as mass returns; vanilla experience gain runs underneath and is preserved through the dip; carry capacity through its own setter. No effective-strength setter exists on this build.
7. **Muscle memory is strength memory.** Lean mass regrows at ordinary hypertrophy rates; the Strength level restores fast as mass recovers; the science register says so.
8. **Performance at the forefront** (Angus, on approving § 4.1): budgets per clock, an instrument before any claim, fast-path rules, a review gate. § 6.

## 4. Architecture

### 4.1 Runtime shape (approved)

Three Lua layers with one direction of dependency. A **pure kernel** in `shared/` holds every formula as side-free functions, tables in and tables out, in the Kahlua-safe subset (no `goto`, no integer format of floats, no length operator on Java lists, no Java objects inside the kernel). **Server adapters** in `server/`, each behind a nil-checked runtime side test, read the engine into plain tables, call the kernel and write the results back. The **client layer** holds the panel, the tooltip wrap, the optional moodle bridge and a read-only mirror of the player's state; it never computes.

Two server clocks, one owner:

- The **fast clock** is the takeover handler on `Hook.CalculateStats`. It fires per player per update tick on the server only (controller note N5: animals return before the hook, the zombie override is empty, the player override calls the base only when the process is not a game client) and reproduces the seven vanilla updaters — endurance, tripping, thirst, stress, the awake and asleep hunger and fatigue updater, morale, fitness — from the game-second delta and a coefficient set the slow clock left for it. It allocates nothing and calls no Java member beyond the hoisted stat handles.
  Registration is itself the takeover, and the handler is unfailable: the hook's trigger returns true whenever any callback is registered and swallows every error, so a handler that throws freezes hunger, thirst, fatigue and stress rather than reverting them to vanilla. The handler body therefore runs inside its own guard, counts failures, and on a failure writes vanilla's own shapes with unit coefficients so the stats keep moving. The hook's registrant list is not readable from Lua, so a second claimant cannot be counted; the mod states that limitation and ships overlay mode as the remedy. Of the seven updaters it replaces, the handler reproduces the six non-stat side effects (the last-endurance stamp, the anger decay, the idle-square timer and in-combat idleness reset, the sleeping time-of-sleep advance, the thirst ghost-mode gate and the fitness stat refresh) and drops the tripping angle, which nothing reads.
  The hook does not stop the real endurance model, which is the player class's own private method and runs from the player's second update step; it stops only the base stub. Controller note N6 reads the order within a server tick as the player's own update step first (vanilla endurance, the vanilla nutrition update and the fitness update), then the base update that hosts the hook, then the rest of the frame and the push. The handler therefore writes endurance from the mod's own stored value every tick and that write is the last before the push, and it maintains the last-endurance field the skipped stub used to. It does read the stat back for one purpose: melee swings, vaults and exercise repetitions, and other mods' event-driven writes, land on the stat between ticks, so the handler takes the difference between the stat and its own last write, discards the part vanilla's per-tick drain or regeneration explains, and folds any remainder above a threshold into its stored value as an external drain or credit before overwriting. That threshold is a game choice.
- The **slow clock** is `EveryOneMinute`, which runs the whole model: stomach and absorption, pools and kinetics, energy balance and body composition, status grades, the derived coefficient set, the weight write into the vanilla slot, the perk ceiling, the health and body-part effects, and the mirror push to that player's client. It runs the players staggered across the minute's ticks so no single frame carries them all.

A player is initialised on first sight in the slow clock's walk of the online list, since the server fires no join event, and dropped when it leaves the list; `OnNewGame(IsoPlayer, nil)`, which fires server-side on creation and on respawn, resets the character's body to a fresh start. Death is detected by the dead flag in the walk.

One sandbox option selects the mode: **takeover** (default) or **overlay**, where the hook is not registered and the fast clock writes its adjustments after vanilla's updaters have run. The kernel computes the same deltas in both modes; the switch changes one adapter. Overlay exists for servers whose other mods claim the hook.

Durable state is one global modData table keyed by username, cached in a server Lua table and never written to player modData. Global modData is loaded and saved per world by the engine as its own file (lifecycle report § C); its save cadence is unread and is the persistence experiment's to measure.

### 4.2 Intake (approved)

Every eat is read off the item, never recomputed. A server-side wrapper of `ISEatFoodAction.complete` snapshots the item before the original runs — full type, raw hunger value, state flags, ingredient list, any per-instance nutrient map on its modData — and reads it again afterwards; the share eaten is the drop in raw hunger over the item's base hunger. A wrapper of the action's `serverStop` catches cancelled eats the same way; a wrapper of `ISDrinkFluidAction:updateEat` reads litres consumed as the container's amount before minus after, the only seat the fluid path has. Each wrapper holds its sentinel in a global of its own, guards on the nil-checked side test, and always calls the original.

The nutrient vector is assembled from four sources: the **per-type baseline** from one Lua data table keyed by full type, generated by the data pipeline and loaded once per side (not script keys, which become a deep per-instance modData copy on every food in the world); **dishes** as the sum of ingredient baselines times counts from the dish's ingredient list, scaled to the dish's own macro total to recover the cooking share the list does not keep; **crafted outputs** that inherit an input's food from a nutrient map written at creation out of the consumed-item map the vanilla handcraft action records; **animal meat** scaled by raw hunger against the type baseline, accepting the ±11 % per-field noise; **fluids** from a per-fluid table keyed by fluid type string, per litre. State multipliers for cooked, burnt, rotten and frozen apply per nutrient class from the retention data; burnt and rotten are labelled mod judgements.

The vector lands in a **stomach buffer**. Gastric emptying moves it into absorption over hours; absorption applies bioavailability, phytate and fat co-ingestion factors; the pools receive the result. Hunger derives from stomach fill and energy state; vanilla's own hunger and thirst writes inside the eat are overwritten on the next tick, so a client sees vanilla's number for at most one push. Cost: one item read per eat, nothing per tick.

### 4.3 Energy and body (approved)

The body is fat mass and lean mass. At first sight a character's vanilla weight is split by a register-cited body-fat relation from weight, build proxies and sex, the creation band traits placing obese, overweight and underweight starts in the right fat range; until the relation is cited the split is a mod judgement.

Expenditure: resting expenditure is the lean-mass form of Mifflin-St Jeor; activity expenditure is a 2024 Compendium metabolic-equivalent table mapped onto the engine's states (sleeping, idle, walking, running, sprinting by extrapolation, the timed-action classes by their vanilla calorie-modifier bands, load carriage from inventory weight through the Pandolf terms flagged as secondary-sourced), per kilogram of total mass; the thermoregulator's cold term multiplies rest and sleep as vanilla does; adaptive thermogenesis is a small bounded reduction under sustained deficit.

Balance is integrated per minute and partitioned per day: a deficit is paid by fat up to Alpert's ceiling of about 290 kJ per kilogram of fat per day and the remainder by lean mass the same day; a surplus is partitioned by the Forbes and Hall relation with the lean share raised by adequate protein and by training, which the engine reports through exercise actions and Strength and Fitness experience events; lean gain is capped at evidenced hypertrophy rates and lean loss under deficit is slowed by protein and training.

**Training signal (wave 2).** The activity input is the engine's own classification: the thermoregulator assigns every player one of twenty-four metabolic-equivalent values each tick and the value is readable server-side, so the slow clock samples it once a minute and banks MET-minutes above the resting value, multiplied by the heavy-load factor vanilla's endurance model uses and by total mass over 80 kg, with a per-event credit for climbs and vaults the sample would miss. Resistance work is counted, not sampled: exercise repetitions and melee hits through the server-side experience and weapon-hit events, heavy timed actions through the current action's calorie modifier, and load carriage above half capacity in weight-minutes. Each event adds set-equivalents to a strength accumulator and a hypertrophy accumulator that decay with a seven-day time constant; the dose is the saturating share of each accumulator. The engine's exercise regularity value is read where a regularity input is wanted rather than rebuilt. No counter of running time or distance exists, so the aerobic dose is a sampled sum and its sampling error is stated.

**Lean mass (wave 2).** Gain requires training and protein and, almost always, a surplus: the daily gain is a base rate of 0.050 kg times a responder constant drawn once per character, the hypertrophy dose, a protein gate that ramps from 0.8 to 1.6 g per kg per day with no leucine or distribution term, an energy factor that is zero in deficit except for a half-credit under high protein and hard training, a headroom factor to a cap of 1.25 times starting lean mass, and an alcohol penalty on days above 0.5 g per kg. Loss under deficit is the overflow past Alpert's fat ceiling divided by the energy density of lean tissue and reduced by a protection factor from training and protein with a floor, so no combination makes a severe deficit free. Loss under immobilisation follows the logarithmic disuse curve fitted to the bed-rest data instead.

**Strength (wave 2).** The level is a clamp and never a grant: the shown level is the smaller of the level vanilla's untouched experience implies and a ceiling, so the mod never adds experience and never engages the anti-cheat. The ceiling is the starting level plus ten times the base-two logarithm of the product of the lean-mass ratio to the starting lean mass and a functional factor, rounded and clamped to the perk range; ten levels per strength doubling is a game choice read off the trait multipliers. The functional factor is the two-term model the evidence supports: a neural term that rises toward a training-dose target with a two-week time constant, holds while a maintenance dose is met, decays over sixty days when untrained but mobile and over ten when immobilised, and never falls below a strength-memory floor set by the highest neural term held for two weeks and decaying over about three hundred days; a cumulative-deficit term of up to minus ten percent that tracks the last ten days of energy debt and recovers within a week of refeeding, independent of lean mass; and a disuse residual. The perk's maximum level is read at runtime rather than assumed to be ten, because a resident skill-extension mod runs Strength and Fitness to fifteen. The level is written through the debug level setter, which fires no event, grants no experience and pushes nothing, so after every clamp write the mod re-runs vanilla's own remap of the Strength level onto the Weak, Feeble, Stout and Strong traits with vanilla's bands. The write is checked every slow tick and made when the shown level differs from what the stat holds, with a six-hour hysteresis on a rise and at most one level per hour on a fall; a clamped level is permanent in vanilla, whose level-up is a threshold-crossing test, and the client's experience sync rebuilds the server's levels from the client's copy, so the check is a standing re-assertion and not a one-off. The level is never written above the experience-implied level, which vanilla's ten-minute decay check would lower again. The server already pushes experience and levels to their owner every second, so no mod packet carries the level. Acute states never touch the level: dehydration in two steps, sleep debt per hour awake beyond eighteen, caffeine, clinical vitamin D deficiency and excess body fat sum into a carry-capacity factor bounded between minus twenty and plus three percent and applied through `setMaxWeightDelta`, with `setMaxWeightBase` left at vanilla to avoid double-counting and because the one resident mod that writes it restores a cached base on expiry. The creation-trait carry factor that vanilla sets only in the player constructor is folded into the same delta so a Strong character keeps the bonus. Carbohydrate availability applies no strength penalty, because the evidence is null.

**Aerobic capacity (wave 2).** A trained aerobic capacity between 0.80 and 1.25, starting at 1.00 for vanilla parity, rises toward a target set by the week's minutes of moderate or hard activity with a fifteen-day time constant gated by iron, protein and the lesser of energy and sleep status, and decays toward the floor with an eighty-four-day time constant unless two hard days in the last seven suspend the decay, which is Hickson's asymmetry. The endurance drain and regeneration the fast clock applies are vanilla's own formulas times two multiplicative coefficients: the drain coefficient from the capacity to the power minus 0.8, glycogen, dehydration in two steps at two and four percent, heat as a monotone ladder replacing vanilla's gated one, excess fat as load, iron grade, hours awake and caffeine, capped at 2.5; the regeneration coefficient from the capacity to the power 1.2, glycogen, protein, iron grade, dehydration, sleep debt, alcohol and the balance dial, floored at 0.25. In the replete untrained state both coefficients are exactly one.

Total mass is written into the vanilla weight slot each minute and re-asserted rather than trusted, since a vanilla admin path lets any client set any player's weight; the three weight-direction flags are set each minute too, because with the vanilla update off nothing else moves them and the character screen's arrow would freeze. The band trait is refreshed only when the band changes, since the vanilla refresh blanket-removes five traits and runs once per two thousand updates, and it is pushed to that client with the player-fields packet's trait bit (controller note N2), so vanilla's fourteen readers, the thermoregulator's fatness term and other mods' viewers keep working. Writing a band trait is a gameplay write, because the band gates vanilla Fitness experience.

**Legacy macro mirror (compatibility wave).** With the vanilla update off, the vanilla calorie and macro stores would only ever fill, and two vanilla consumers still read them: the Strength experience multiplier on the protein store and a dozen third-party status bars. A sandbox option, on by default, has the slow clock write the four vanilla stores from the model each minute, so the protein bonus tracks the model's protein status and the bars keep meaning on the re-based scale. Sweat, respiration and cold-diuresis loss terms feed § 4.4.

### 4.4 Nutrient kinetics (approved)

Every nutrient is one declarative record — pool size, elimination term, absorption defaults and interaction factors, requirement, status ladder (replete, marginal, depleted, clinical), excess ladder with acute thresholds, repletion cap — and the kernel is one generic engine over the records; every number in a record cites a science-register row by id and a checker fails an uncited number. Iron, vitamin A and B12 carry two compartments; water and the electrolytes are fast pools charged by the § 4.3 loss terms and by alcohol and caffeine diuresis, caffeine's fading in a habitual drinker through a tolerance that decays over days. Timelines are calibrated to the register's documented onset from replete at zero intake; B12 is modelled at its real, longer-than-a-game scale and the onset dial is what compresses it. Grades are recomputed each minute; the coefficient set § 4.5 reads is rebuilt only when a grade changes. Interactions ride the records. Thirst is water deficit with sodium concentration as its second input; the dehydration endurance threshold is a ruling on two conflicting sources, mild from 2 % of body mass and steepening toward 4 %, both cited; refeeding after prolonged starvation is a risk state with both incidence figures documented. Four dials: onset speed, severity, whether deficiencies can kill, whether excess effects are on.

Three additions from wave 2. A **glycogen and blood-glucose state** is tracked beside the pools, because carbohydrate availability is the largest endurance lever and true hypoglycaemia is where cognition and reaction begin to fail, while acute fasting and the hunger bar move neither; the store, its depletion rate at a given metabolic rate and the hypoglycaemic threshold are register rows. **Sleep debt** lives in the mod's own state rather than being read from the fatigue stat, because a server that disables sleep resets fatigue every tick ahead of the hook. And every acute impairment source, sleep debt, dehydration, hypoglycaemia, iron depletion, alcohol and caffeine, is expressed on **one impairment unit**, defined as twenty-four hours awake and cross-calibrated to a blood alcohol of 0.05 percent, because those two were measured against each other and the engine's vision cone reads the fatigue and intoxication stats together.

### 4.5 Effects (default, revised by wave 2)

The effects layer is a declarative **effects table**: for each nutrient and grade, a list of (surface, magnitude, register row). The slow clock composes the active rows into one coefficient set per player when a grade changes; adapters apply the set through five channels, chosen by what each surface actually reaches on this build:

| channel | surfaces | mechanism | cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| stat coefficients | hunger, thirst, endurance drain and regen, fatigue accrual and sleep recovery, stress, unhappiness, boredom, panic | read by the takeover handler | per tick, constant between grade changes |
| trait toggles | night vision, short-sightedness | server-side add or remove with an ownership flag so a natively held trait is never removed, then `sendSyncPlayerFields(player, 2)` | on grade change |
| body-damage setters | regeneration tiers, wound and bleeding timers, herb factors, infection progression, temperature stat, pain | public setters the health report lists; `syncBodyPart` where a part field must reach the client | on grade change and per minute |
| health drains | starvation, terminal scurvy, cardiac beriberi, severe hyponatraemia, acute toxicities | `ReduceGeneralHealth` per minute, gated by the lethality dial; nausea through the food-sickness and poison stats | per minute |
| event-driven | aiming delay after each shot, fracture risk on landing, a client-side speed write inside the two-second push window for severe states | wrappers and events the perception and health reports name | on the event |

The governing principle, from the healing report and borne out by every wave-2 survey: **deficiency is a penalty and adequacy is never a bonus.** Every trial that supplemented people who were not deficient found nothing or harm, and every large effect came from a deficient population. Each effect below is therefore a penalty below the replete grade and flat at and above it, with two named exceptions: vitamin C and colds in exerting, cold-exposed people, the one population in the literature that matches a survivor, and the BalanceBonus dial, a labelled game choice.

**Fatigue and sleep.** The fast clock integrates homeostatic sleep pressure with the two-process constants: a saturating rise with an 18.2-hour time constant shortened by sleep debt, an exponential fall with a 4.2-hour time constant scaled by bed quality, the sleep traits and a recovery multiplier; the written fatigue is that pressure plus a circadian term of amplitude 0.12 with its trough at four in the morning, plus a small debt term, minus a caffeine offset. Vanilla's Tired thresholds are kept, because under those constants they fall where the impairment evidence puts them, level two at about eighteen hours awake matching the measured blood-alcohol equivalence. Only three nutritional levers touch accrual: iron depletion without anaemia, mild dehydration from about 1.5 percent body mass, and low energy availability below 30 kcal per kg of lean mass, the last labelled an extrapolation; vitamin D, B12, folate and protein apply nothing. Caffeine follows a five-hour half-life, nine and a half for a slow metaboliser, with a tolerance that builds and fades over days and a withdrawal window from twelve hours to five days; it masks pressure without repaying it. Alcohol's overnight recovery penalty is a bounded judgement rather than a point estimate, and the hangover raises accrual the next day. Exercise improves recovery unless vigorous work ends within an hour of sleep. The caffeine sums, the circadian term and every multiplier are computed on the slow clock and handed to the fast clock as scalars.

**Endurance and fitness.** As § 4.3: vanilla's drain and regeneration shapes times the two coefficients, with iron acting on regeneration and on the adaptation rate more than on capacity, glycogen as the largest lever, dehydration as a two-step penalty, and sleep loss at 0.4 percent per hour awake. Fitness is tied to nutrition through this regeneration and through the aerobic capacity, not through the Fitness perk, which vanilla keeps gating by band.

**Perception and reaction.** The cone and hearing move only because the mod owns fatigue and intoxication; dehydration and the other sources enter through the impairment unit with a hard cap of 0.15 on the fatigue offset so no nutritional state alone crosses a Tired band. Dark vision is the night-vision trait, whose darkness-scaled cone term has exactly the shape of vitamin A's selective loss of dark-adapted vision: it is granted after fourteen days at the replete grade, gated on zinc, slowed by iron or riboflavin depletion, withdrawn with hysteresis and an ownership flag, and the deficiency penalty is silent because most sufferers are unaware; retinol repletion takes about six weeks and carotene does not substitute. The Short Sighted toggle applies only at clinical deficiency behind the severity dial. Two documented surprises from the jar: the night-vision trait also floors the rendered ambient light, so the screen visibly brightens for its holder, and the short-sight penalty is cancelled by worn glasses on the weapon-sight arm and also moves the blur and the zombie reveal rate. Trait writes follow four rules from the compatibility read: set the ownership flag before adding and check it before removing, since vanilla's own remaps blanket-remove whole bands; re-assert owned traits every slow tick, because the client's experience sync rebuilds the server's trait list from the client's copy and runtime trait mods rebuild lists too; push only when a trait changed and only to the owner, because the receiver resets the client's whole list and erases a client-only mod's local traits; and expect other players' copies of a character's traits to stay stale until they relog, since every push is owner-only. **Hearing gets no nutrient term**: the best evidence is under one decibel over years against an engine that swings detection range thirty percent on fatigue. Aiming delay is one plus a quarter of the total impairment units, clamped between 0.90 and 1.60 and re-applied after each shot, with caffeine's credit multiplicative on the sleep-debt penalty; it reads the glucose state at 3.0 to 3.5 mmol per litre and never the hunger bar, because acute fasting has no measurable effect on cognition. The engine cannot represent that caffeine restores detection but not marksmanship, and the register says so. Movement and combat responsiveness come first from the endurance and heavy-load moodles the model already drives, and only for severe states from the direct speed write.

**Mood.** One convention, a standardised mean difference of one equals one moodle band: sleep debt drives panic and unhappiness, omega-3 and vitamin D depletion touch unhappiness only, B-vitamin depletion touches stress only, caffeine charges panic with a knee at 400 mg, alcohol charges stress the morning after and never during, exercise credits both. Boredom is untouched, magnesium and anxiety and B12 and mood are omitted, and the semi-starvation mood effects carry a symptom list and no number.

**Healing, bleeding, infection.** A penalty-only healing multiplier between 0.5 and 1.0 from protein-energy status and vitamin C and zinc deficiency, applied through the four regeneration setters and the per-part wound timers. A bleeding multiplier from vitamin C only, between 1.0 and 1.25 at clinical scurvy, with a spontaneous-bruising event, and no vitamin K term at all; the bleeding-time field appears twice in the damage formula, so the multiplier's effect on damage is about its square and the constant is set accordingly. A wound-infection multiplier between 1.0 and 2.3 from protein-energy status, the best-evidenced effect in the report, applied to infection progress on open wounds through the per-part infection level and its sync, never through the shared growth-rate field. A cold-catching multiplier between 0.5 and 3.0 with sleep loss as the largest penalty, energy deficit next, and vitamin C's credit in the exerting cold-exposed population as the one evidenced bonus; vitamin D's respiratory effect is cut to at most six percent after the 2025 update. Fracture risk gets nothing, fracture healing follows the healing multiplier.

**Thermal.** Vanilla already reads fatness and energy, and the mod does not double them. Two additions: iron at the anaemic grade only, worth about 0.2 degrees of core temperature and written as a per-tick offset to the temperature stat because the engine lerps halfway toward it, and dehydration on the heat side above two percent body mass rising to a cap at four.

**Toxicity and lethality.** A three-rung ladder per nutrient at one to two times the upper level, the published acute threshold and the lethal dose, expressed through the poison and sickness stats together, since the poison drain requires both a poison level above ten and the sick moodle threshold and caps at thirty. Iron and selenium are the two a scavenger can reach; vitamin K has no upper level. Starvation, terminal scurvy, cardiac beriberi, severe hyponatraemia and the lethal rungs drain health per minute behind the lethality dial. No fainting state exists, so hypoglycaemia and severe dehydration express through fatigue, speed and the temperature stat.

**BalanceBonus** remains one dial, on by default at 1.05 on endurance regeneration and fatigue recovery while every tracked nutrient sits in its replete band, labelled a game choice with no evidence claimed.

### 4.6 Item pass and data pipeline (default)

The pipeline is a tool under `tools/` following the repository's scanner conventions: a curated mapping from every food record's id to a USDA SR Legacy entry with a confidence column, the SR Legacy extract subset committed with provenance, the nutrient-id join on FDC's internal ids, iodine filled from the USDA/FDA/ODS iodine database or CoFID, cooked and raw states paired from measured SR entries where they exist and retention factors otherwise, and an Atwater energy cross-check with per-nutrient sanity ranges. It emits `data/food-nutrients.json` and, from it, two shipped artefacts: the per-type nutrient **Lua data table** and the **`module Base` partial blocks** that re-base `Calories`, `Carbohydrates`, `Proteins` and `Lipids` on every mapped food record. `HungerChange` and `ThirstChange` are left at vanilla, because hunger is the model's and the hunger key drives recipe cost, the portion menu, the cancel guard and displayed weight. Drinks are re-based only in the per-fluid Lua table, because `fluid` block merging is unread. Records without a mapping carry an explicit reason and a coverage test fails on a record with neither. The mapping works by composition family, since the 644 per-item food rows hold about 342 distinct macro tuples.

### 4.7 Interface (default)

The **panel** derives from `ISCollapsableWindow`, registers with `ISLayoutManager` for position persistence, and reads the client mirror at the push cadence, redrawing only on change. Its default view is symptoms and coarse bands with text; exact pools, intakes and per-food micronutrients appear when the character holds either Nutritionist trait or the server's **VisibilityMode** option allows them. The **keybind** and options live in `PZAPI.ModOptions`, with the mod calling its load once inside a protected call at boot as CleanUI does. The **tooltip** appends lines by wrapping `ISToolTipInv:render` idempotently and drawing into the space it makes, gated the same way as the panel, and the test plan notes that the harness's debug client shows vanilla's nutrition block without the trait. A **character-info tab** is added through the three wraps AutoCook demonstrates, with the restore-layout gap closed. **Moodles** are optional: when `MF.createMoodle` is a function the client creates one framework moodle per symptom class and sets its value from the mirror; otherwise a small own `ISUIElement` column draws the same icons in a fixed slot, anchored under the vanilla stack if `MoodlesUI` clears the exposure test. Translations ship as B42 `ItemName.json` and interface keys under the mod's prefix, and nothing server-side reads a display name.

### 4.8 Sync and persistence (default)

The **command bus** carries every mod value that crosses the wire, under a module name no resident mod uses. The server sends each player a **mirror message** — a flat table of scalars: grades, band, body composition, the panel's display values — on first sight, on any grade or band change, and otherwise at most once per minute; the client installs it and never writes back. Client requests are limited to "send me my mirror" on panel open. The engine's own pushes carry the vanilla stats the model writes; the trait packet carries band and perception traits after a mod-caused push; the global store is never transmitted, because the global transmit broadcasts a whole table to every connection. Every payload key is a string, number or boolean.

**Missed-intake reconciliation (compatibility wave).** Eleven Workshop mods replace or portion the eat and drink actions, and a replaced action never reaches the wrapped seat. With the vanilla update off, the vanilla calorie and macro stores change only through an eat or a drink, so each slow tick compares them with the mirror's last write before writing again; a rise the wrappers did not report is an intake the model missed, and it is reconciled into the stomach as macros only, with a note that its nutrient vector is unknown.

**Persistence** is the global modData table `NutritionRevamp.players`, keyed by username, holding per player a version field and the model's inputs only (pools, masses, stomach, flags), everything else derived on read so a formula change needs no migration. The server cache writes through on change; the engine saves the table with the world. A respawn resets the character's record on `OnNewGame`; a reconnect reloads it on first sight; a dead character's record is kept until its respawn. Whether the table survives a restart is experiment X28's to grade before any live scenario depends on it.

## 6. Performance constraints

- **Budgets.** The takeover handler costs no more per player per tick than the seven vanilla updaters it replaces, measured. The slow clock's per-player pass is bounded and the players are staggered across the minute. The client redraws only on change and reads the mirror, never the engine, per frame.
- **Instrument before claim.** A harness addition times the handler and the minute pass from Lua and reports per-tick and per-minute costs; every performance claim is a mod run beside a vanilla baseline on the same fixture, committed as artifacts.
- **Fast-path rules.** No table allocation, no string building, no protected call and no Java member lookup per tick beyond hoisted handles; no exponentials, powers or loops over dose lists per tick, so the caffeine sums, the circadian term, every multiplier and the two endurance coefficients are computed on the slow clock and handed down as scalars; per-type data in one table loaded at boot, never per-instance modData copies; coefficient sets rebuilt on grade change only.
- **Review gate.** A reviewer checks each task's hot path against these rules; a measured regression fails the task.
