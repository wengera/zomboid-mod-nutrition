## 2. The work, in order

Every planner rule is a switch in `T14_SWITCHES`, with `pre_t14` for the old replay cases, a killing test and a mutant per rule, the goldens re-recorded only under a named change, the rules' numbers in the register before the mod reads them; a harness change lands in its own commit before the run that uses it.

Cost if wrong: four fields a tick per zombie (the sense radius's list, as the `target` read already is).
2. **Task 2, the jar read of the swing's interruption** (before P30-A's second form): whether a started swing can be cut by a movement input or a stop in the same tick, what the engine does with the swing's damage and animation, and whether the back step during a swing is a walk or a cancel; a read with rows (T95), and a ruling on the second form.
3. **Task 3, iteration 24's reflex and planner rules:**
   - **P30-A, the press that did nothing** (`SWING_CLOSE`, the Lua reflex and `Melee`'s arguments): a chasing target (its `target` the player and its distance falling) is swung at only once it is inside `SWING_CLOSE_R` 1.0 tiles, the stance held until then (an idle target at the reach as today); after a swing whose own next line reads the target neither lowered nor knocked down nor killed, no back step: the swing is pressed again at the cadence while the target is inside `SWING_CLOSE_R`, and the back step comes after a landed hit (the zombie staggered, the next line's `hp` fallen) or when the target is inside `K.STAND_R`; the shove's back step after a miss (P28-B2) stays for shove mode. The second form (the back step as soon as the frames read the target still closing past 0.95 tiles during the swing step) only as Task 2's read allows. Cost if wrong: the race against the zombie's attack when the swing is held to a tile; a landed swing's stagger misread.
   - **P30-B, no standing on a model wait** (`GOAL_CONTINUE`, the goal runner): while a call is pending the runner keeps the last goal's walk running toward its target, or, with no last target, walks toward the view's `edge` square at a walk, and the answer replaces the leg; a `goal` branch tick with a call pending and a zombie within `DANGER_R` hands the tick to the threat branch as today. And the explore hop's length (`EXPLORE_HOP_MIN` 12 tiles): an `ExploreToward` answer whose target is nearer than it along the line to `edge` is extended to `edge` or to the first unknown square beyond it, so one hop does not end in a wait every 1 to 6 tiles. Cost if wrong: a walk continued into a situation the model would have changed (bounded by the threat branch's pre-emption); a longer hop past a building the model wanted looted (the prompt still names the buildings).
   - **P30-C, the door held** (`DOOR_HELD`, the Fight and the route): a held zombie behind a closed door or a locked gate within `DOOR_HELD_R` 4 tiles is a decision on one tick, never a stand: a building door the character can open with one zombie behind it and the character above the flee floor takes the stance at the door's near square (`stand_post`), opens the door (`interact`) and meets the zombie at the edge of reach through the melee; a locked gate, a window, more than one zombie behind, or the character below `FLEE_HEALTH_FLOOR` means leave: the zombie edge-marked, the goal's target past the door marked (`mark_far`) so the route takes another way or the goal is dropped cooled, no walk into the gate.    - **P30-D, the sneak gait** (`SNEAK`, the planner's gait on every goal walk and steer that is not a flee): the character sneaks when any zombie within `SNEAK_R` 8 tiles has a `target` other than the player, upright when none is within it or when one targets the player (the stance then; the flag set false before the melee's first press so the fight runs upright); the sneak walk's speed measured live and read into the grade; the flee's gait stays `run`. Cost if wrong: a slower walk where no zombie would have noticed; the endurance of a sneak run never taken (the flee runs upright).

   - **P30-F, the dressing under a fast bleed** (`DRESS_RIDE`, the care's ride): a dressing on a part bleeding faster than `BLEED_FAST` (the health falling over 2 a game minute) rides through a flee from a zombie beyond the stance's reach (as P27-O rides the `health` flee's leg), superseded only by a zombie within `KEEP_R`.
