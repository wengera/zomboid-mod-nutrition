## 1. The problem

Plan 33 settled the group's entry: the finish's head step is capped, a crawler underfoot is stomped first, and three to four standing zombies closing in the open are met by the fighting retreat, more by the run (F19's rows #4716-#4726; sixteen trials). F19 then killed ten on the `agent` fixture and died at game minute 779 under a column of five to eight standing zombies that followed the character for nine game minutes. Its write-up (`docs/platform/agent-sessions.md`, `testing/artifacts/f_goals_19-20261109-060929/GUIDE.md` §§ 2-6, the threads `docs/reference/research-notes.md#agent-link`) read the chain:

- **The care stopped the run.** Above `RETREAT_MAX_N` the leave's `run` took 29 lines, and between them the care took 21 of 55 lines from game minute 770 to 777: 8 `stop`s, an `unwear` and 5 `rip`s, none done, each rip superseded 0.5 to 1.5 s after acceptance as the column closed back to 1.78-2.47 tiles (#4719). The care's yield reads only `KEEP_R` (`CARE_COMMIT`, `care_yield.CareYield._care_commit`) and runs before the threat plan reads the group (`Threat._threat`, then `_threat_plan`), so a run that had gained 2.5 tiles stopped for a rip five times. A rip cannot ride a leg: the handcraft stops on a walk unless its recipe reads `CanWalk`, and RipClothing names none (`PZAgent_Tend.lua`'s note; the jar read of § 3.7).
- **The door was shut from outside.** On line 1595 the leave's leg turned `door` with the nearest standing zombie at 0.37 tiles; the approach walk ended `done` at 0.99 tiles, the care took two more lines at the door, the open went, the shelter plan's `enter` walk to the inside square ended `done` at 0.93 tiles with no frame moving (the Lua's `K.DONE_R` 1.0), and the `close` shut the door with the character on the outer square and 12431 at 0.60 tiles (#4720). The doorway stand's plan already walks to a post beyond the threshold and reads `stood` only on a room square (P24-AO fix 1, `Flee._stand_at_post`); the plain shelter plan, which the leave takes whenever more than `DOOR_STAND_N` stand (`stand_ok` false), has neither rule, and its close is sent whatever square the character stands on.
- **The retreat dithered at five tiles and stood at one.** Its first episode (lines 274-299) sent 15 back legs alternating with 11 `strung` ends as the count within 6 tiles flicked between three and fewer, the nearest zombie 4.49-5.21 tiles off, the character moving back and forth in a box under a tile for 13 s (#4721): fix 3 forced every stance leg to a backpedal whatever the distance. In the killing group (lines 1536-1537) it sent nothing: the strike-mode melee a281 held the branch at 0.84 tiles while 12454 walked in from behind to 0.49 tiles at 173 degrees off the body and bit (#4718); a strike-mode melee hands back for another standing zombie only in the finish (`PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua`'s rule 4, `handbackR`).
- **The shoves were refused.** Four of the five shoves `GROUP_LEAVE` and `DRAG_GUARD_FIGHT` sent were rejected `busy` (#4718, #4720): the Lua's `K.attackBusy` (`PZAgent_SkillsWorld.lua`) refuses a shove or stomp while `hasHitReaction` or `isAttackStarted` reads true, which is exactly when the drag guard sends one; the rule's premise (#4372: the shove is the one press the engine takes at the reaction's end) needs the press after the reaction, which no skill waits for.
- **One stale tick.** The melee at the first wound was sent off a frame 0.970 s old, the line 1.435 s after the one before (the session's median lag 0.057 s, #4717); the decision line carries no frame age, so the write-up could not say what held the loop.

## 2. Goal and success criteria

With a column at its heels the character runs it off before it tends a wound, enters a door only when it can shut it from inside with no zombie at the threshold, holds the fighting retreat's gap at the stance's band and swings from it, and lands the shove the guard sends; read in trials on the `agent` fixture with the model, `street-1` with the model and the arena pair, then in one evidence session. Success: (1) no care send (a `stop`, `rip`, `unwear`, or a `bandage` without `keepMoving`) on a decision line whose frame reads `GROUP_LEAVE_N` or more standing zombies within `GROUP_ENTRY_R` (the scorecard's new `care@3+` cell 0 in every trial); (2) no door `close` sent with the character off a room square, and no door leg chosen with a standing zombie within `DOOR_RACE_R` of the character; (3) the retreat's lines (`tools/trial_score.py`'s `retreat_gap`) read no line under `GAP_CLOSE` and a median between `FIGHT_REACH` and `KEEP_R` + 1.0, with no `retreat`/`strung` alternation shorter than `STRUNG_LINES`; (4) of the shoves the leave and the drag guard send, at least half end `done` with `attackStarted` true; (5) the two metrics no worse than Plan 33's round four (the `agent` fixture alive at the cap in two of three trials with the hits per kill under 1.0; `street-1` 5 to 8 kills and 0 to 3 hits; the arena pair about 9 and 0); (6) the evidence session F20 alive at its cap or dead by a cause the write-up traces to a rule the trials never reached.

## 3. Design

Seven switched rules, on by default, in `survive.T19_SWITCHES`, each logged on the branch's why under the key its clause names (the grade of § 3.6 reads those keys).

### 3.1 The care under a column (`LEAVE_CARE`, `care_yield.py`, `group.py`)

`ThreatGroup._group_read` keeps the tick's standing count within `GROUP_ENTRY_R` (`group_n`, the leave's own read: `_group_standing`, one read dead or across a blocked edge left out). With `group_n` at or above `GROUP_LEAVE_N`, `_care_commit` returns None (no leg, no yield) and `_care_yields` yields nothing but the ride (P24-U, P24-AR, P26-D and P27-O's riding bandage, `keepMoving` on a part above the legs, which keeps the leg): a rip, an unwear, a stop or a standing bandage never goes with a column within 6 tiles, and the care resumes on the line the count falls under `GROUP_LEAVE_N`. Logged `care_withheld` {why "group", n, key (the care's `commit_key` when one)} on the threat why. The attackers clause of `_rides_many` is unchanged. Cost if wrong: a bleeding part runs longer (F19's part 7 fell 15 health in eight game minutes under the stops); the ride still dresses a bleed above the legs on the run.

### 3.2 The door under a column (`DOOR_INSIDE`, `LEAVE_DOOR_RACE`; `flee.py`, `night.py`, `group.py`)

`DOOR_INSIDE`: every shelter plan, a stand's or a plain one, reads its `enter` as ended only once the character stands on a room square of the plan's building (`policies.on_post`); else the step ends `outside` and the enter is walked again, to the post square beyond the threshold (`stand_post` off `World.door_way(door)`) when the map has one, else to the plan's `inside` square, `STEP_RETRIES` times, then the plan is dropped (`Flee._drop(False)`): never a close from outside. The `close` step and the re-close are sent only with the character on a room square of the building and the door not guarded (the re-close's own read: no known zombie within `DOOR_CLEAR_R` of the door square and none nearer the door square than the character, `Flee._door_guarded`); else the plan is dropped and the branch decides (inside, the Flee's exit plan or the adoption, P23-AJ 3; outside, the steer). The stand's `_stand_at_post` becomes this rule's one reader (`_at_post`), the stand marking `stood` where the plain plan advances to `close`. Logged on the Flee's `shelter` why: {step "enter", ended "outside", retries} and {step "close", refused "outside" | "guarded"}.

`LEAVE_DOOR_RACE`: the leave picks a door leg only when the nearest standing zombie within `DANGER_R` is beyond `DOOR_RACE_R` (3.0 tiles: an approach of up to `LEAVE_DOOR_R`, an open, an enter and a close take about three seconds, which a walker covers from closer) and the door's verdict reads `ok` with the second-exit rule applied whatever the stand's readiness (`Flee.door_verdicts(ts, second_exit=True)`: P26-B's `one_exit`, so the leave never shuts itself into a building with one way out); a running door leg whose plan has not reached `close` is dropped when a standing zombie comes within `DOOR_ABORT_R` (1.0 tile) of the character, and the leg is re-read on the same line (the retreat or the run). Logged `group_leave` {door_refused "race" | "one_exit"} on a withheld pick and {door_dropped "abort", near} on the drop. Cost if wrong: a door passed up at 2.9 tiles that a faster character would have shut; the retreat and the run take its place.

### 3.3 The retreat's gap, its melee and its end (`RETREAT_GAP`, `RETREAT_MELEE`, `STRUNG_HOLD`; `fight.py`, `group.py`)

`RETREAT_GAP`: under the retreat the stance leg's kind is the distance's own (`stance_leg_kind`: the stand beyond `KEEP_R`, the backpedal within it, held to `KEEP_R` + `KEEP_MARGIN`, the melee in reach), with the backpedal's heading the retreat's (away from the group's centroid, `_retreat_heading`) and every approaching kind (`toward`, `seek`, `pull`) read as `stand`: the gap is held at the stance's band, never widened from five tiles, and no step goes toward the column. `_retreat_leg` sends that leg in place of a ladder None or walk, `_retreat_holds` holds a running aim while its kind is the distance's and, for a back leg, its heading within `RETREAT_RESEND_DEG` of the retreat's. Logged `retreat_leg` {kind, in_place_of}.

`RETREAT_MELEE`: under the retreat a running strike-mode melee gives way (`Fight.busy` False, the ladder's turn: the back leg) when a standing zombie other than its target is within `RETREAT_BACK_R` (1.5 tiles) of the character and the melee's last progress step is no press in flight (`melee.STRIKE_STEPS`); the ladder re-sends the melee when the pin is in reach as it does. Logged `retreat_break` {other, dist}. Cost if wrong: a melee given up one press before a kill; the band re-sends it at once.

`STRUNG_HOLD`: the leave ends `strung` only once the count (within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`, `DANGER_R` on the door leg) has read under `GROUP_LEAVE_N` on `STRUNG_LINES` (3) consecutive lines; the lines before log `group_leave` {n, strung_n}. Cost if wrong: a leave held two lines longer.

### 3.4 The shove at the reaction's end (`SHOVE_CLEAR`, the Lua: `PZAgent_Stance.lua`, `PZAgent_SkillsWorld.lua`)

A `shove` or `stomp` whose check reads `K.attackBusy(p)` true is accepted with `st.wait` set and its tick waits, pressing nothing, until `hasHitReaction` and `isAttackStarted` both read false, up to `K.SHOVE_WAIT_TICKS` (45 client ticks) and then fails `busy`; the result gains `waited` (the ticks waited, 0 with none). The `attack` skill's check keeps its refusal (the swing is the melee's). PZA/1.12: `waited` appended to the shove and stomp results in `schema/pza1.json`, the generated files by `tools/pza_schema.py --write`. The lupa tests reproduce F19's refusal (the fake reads `hasHitReaction` true for n ticks: the old check refuses `busy`, the new presses on the first clear tick with `waited` n) and the cap. A smoke boot on the arena before any trial depends on it (ruling P32-R). Cost if wrong: a character held still up to 0.75 s under a reaction where the run would have gone; the engine moves nobody under the reaction either.

### 3.5 The frame's age (`survive.py`)

The decision line carries `frame_age` (the line's `wall` minus the frame's `wallMs` in seconds, `World.frame_wall_ms`) only when it exceeds `FRAME_STALE_S` (0.5 s), so the goldens keep their bytes and the next write-up reads what held the loop. No rule reads it.

### 3.6 The instrument (`grade_x4`, the scorecard)

`grade_x4` is `grade_x3` under `base` plus: `care_withheld` {lines, by_n}; `care_at_group` (the care-branch lines that sent a stop, rip, unwear or a bandage without `keepMoving` on a frame with `GROUP_LEAVE_N` or more standing within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`: criterion 1); `door_leave` {picks, refused {race, one_exit}, aborts, enters_outside, closes {inside, refused}}; `retreat` {lines, by_kind {stand, back, melee}, breaks, strung_holds, gap (the scorer's `retreat_gap`)}; `shoves` {sent, done, started, busy, waited median: criterion 4}; `idle_snap_by_act` (F19's split of x3's frames by the frame's act); `frame_age` {lines, median, max}; and the frozen x3 readers' vocabulary folded in: `group_leave` counting the `shove`, `door`, `retreat` and `run` legs by the why's `leg`, `drag_guard` reading `drag_fight`. `f_grade_16.py`, `e_grade_25.py`; the scorecard gains one cell after `head_ticks max`, `care@3+` (`care_at_group`'s count), a named change of the row.

### 3.7 The jar read

The rip's walk: the craft action's `stopOnWalk` and `stopOnRun` off the recipe (`lua:shared/TimedActions/ISCraftAction.lua`, `recipe:isStopOnWalk()`), the handcraft's walk rule and its `CanWalk` key (`lua:shared/Entity/TimedActions/ISHandcraftAction.lua`), and the RipClothing craft recipe's script keys; the jar side where the recipe's flags are read (`CraftRecipe.isStopOnWalk`). Two to three `C` rows minted with the evidence session's write-up; they settle whether a rip can ever ride a leg (§ 3.1 assumes not).

### 3.8 The trials and the proof

The composition (§§ 3.1-3.4 behind their switches) against Plan 33's promoted head `16f8774b` (its snapshot in `pz-trials`) on three fixtures: the `agent` fixture with the model (`x_trial.py --variant ladder --fixture agent --waves none`), `street-1` with the model, the arena pair; a smoke boot of § 3.4's Lua first; rounds until the readings repeat on both model fixtures; the rows in `testing/trials/trials.md` with the new `care@3+` cell. ## 5. Risks

`LEAVE_CARE` may let a fast bleed run under a column that never strings out (the ride covers a part above the legs; the count falls as the run outpaces walkers, and the trials read the dressed-under-a-minute cell); `DOOR_INSIDE`'s re-walk costs a second at the door with the column behind, which the race rule bounds; `RETREAT_GAP`'s stand lets the column close to `KEEP_R` before any step, which is Angus's band; `RETREAT_MELEE` may break a melee that would have killed; `SHOVE_CLEAR` is a reflex change that can park the `-debug` client, hence the smoke boot; `STRUNG_HOLD` holds the leave through a true stringing-out for two lines.
