## 1. The problem

Three layers run at three speeds. The Lua melee reflex (`PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua`, Plan 31) runs at the client's tick, about 60 Hz, and owns one zombie to the kill: the facing, the press timing, the finish, the hand-backs. The Python planner runs at 2 Hz on frames sampled at 10 Hz and owns everything else, every piece of footwork included: a backpedal is an `Aim(vx, vy, AIM_LEG_S)` leg, a fixed vector held for two seconds, re-decided at 2 Hz. The planner has grown to 16,400 lines, 93 switches across 17 plan tuples (all on) and 201 numeric constants; `fight.py` alone is 1,687 lines over twelve mixins. Every spacing rule since Plan 32 (the retreat gap and its regain, the crawler clear, the side clear, the strung hold, the drag guard, the leave's leg) is a correction to that open-loop leg, written after a death showed one more way it fails.

The one change that moved the metric was the melee reflex: every arena trial of every Plan 31 arm went to zero hits once the press loop left Python for Lua. Plan 31's three alternative deciders and two mixes, all 2 Hz Python, could not be separated from the ladder on kills (ruling P31-H). The decision layer is not where the deaths come from.

The trials under the promoted Plan 34 build (`testing/trials/trials.md`, rounds two to four on the `agent` fixture) are bimodal: 9 of 19 died and 10 reached the cap, the caps at full health with zero hits, the deaths with 3 to 27 hits. F20 (`f_goals_20-20261109-155512`, alive at the cap with one bite) was one draw from that distribution.

An offline probe over the frames and decision logs of F20 and the 27 trials from round two on (the controller's scratch `probe/deadtime.py`, 28 runs, 76,071 frames, 6 deaths, 51 hit onsets; a trial reading, never evidence: P31-T) read what the deaths look like at 10 Hz:

- **The hits land in held contact, not in a gap crossed unseen.** At the 42 death-run hit onsets the nearest standing zombie had been inside 1.0 tile for a median 1.6 s (lower quartile 0.8 s): at -1.0 s it read 0.73 tiles, at -0.5 s 0.59, at the hit 0.53. The planner saw it: `inputs.nearest` at an aim send matched the frame at the send (median 0.0 tiles, 90th percentile 0.37 in the deaths), and the planner ticked every 0.537 s (90th percentile 0.547).
- **The reflex was not in control.** The skill running at the hit: an aim back leg 16 of 42, nothing 10, a flee steer 6, the melee 4, a rip 3, a stomp, a shove and a walk one each. A melee had ended within the three seconds before 23 of the 42 hits: 22 of those ends `failed superseded` (the planner cut the press for a leg) and 15 `done` (a kill or a hand-back). The planner's why at the aim legs was `crawler_clear` with `point_blank_fight` and `holding`; at the nothing lines `holding` under a leg, the strain's withheld shove and the leave's `care_withheld`; the care branch itself twice (`care_commit`, a dressing at contact).
- **A crawler lay within 1.5 tiles at 20 of the 42 hits** (the median crawler distance at a hit 0.9 to 1.1 tiles).
- **The backpedal cannot open a gap from a lunge.** The back leg's pace read a median 0.92 tiles/s (90th percentile 1.41) against the nearest targeting zombie's closing speed within 4 tiles of a median 0.31 tiles/s, 90th percentile 1.53 to 1.72, 99th 2.9. In the deaths 45 % of back legs saw the nearest inside the reach and 34 % inside 1.0 tile (29 hits under back legs); in the runs that lived, 18 % and 7 %.

So the death is the pile: a standing zombie at half a tile for a second or more, a crawler beside it, and the character backpedalling at a walk, steering, ripping or holding a leg with no press in flight, because a planner rule superseded the reflex whose press is the defence (#4688: a player mid-attack facing an attacking zombie is never bitten; #4686: a landed hit staggers it). The reflex's own crowd hand-back (three standing within 0.9 tiles, #4290) returns control to the planner exactly in the pile, and Plan 33's and 34's rules then send legs.

## 2. Goal and success criteria

In contact, one reflex at the client's tick rate owns the press, the facing and the footwork together, and no planner leg supersedes it; the planner names the intent (engage; the heading to give ground along; the health floor at which the reflex hands back) and the reflex keeps pressing whoever steps into reach while it gives ground along the clearest heading between presses, steps off crawlers and never hands back inside a pile. Read in trials on the `agent` fixture with the model and no waves (the evidence fixture; six or more trials an arm), the arena pair and lone waves (the zero-hit sanity) and `street-1`, by the round: the death rate and the median kills per arm beside the two metrics, and a new reading, the contact seconds (the nearest standing zombie inside 1.0 tile with no press in flight and no hit reaction). Criteria: (1) the arena arms keep zero hits; (2) the `agent` arm's death rate under a third over six or more trials against the control's 9 of 19 (P31-T: a trial reading); (3) contact seconds per engagement under half the control's; (4) the median kills not below the control's; (5) every hit traced to the press or step before it in the trial's three lines; then (6) one evidence session, F21, written up with its grade and rows.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The footwork reflex (`FOOTWORK`; the Lua: `PZAgent_StanceWork.lua`, the melee's `work` arguments)

The melee skill (`PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua`) gains optional arguments, the `work` table: `workR` (the standing zombies read for the heading, 3.5 tiles), `crawlerR` (a crawler this near repels the heading hard and is never stepped onto, 1.2), `bandLo` (the gap under which the step back goes, the reach less 0.4), `withdrawX`, `withdrawY` (the planner's bias, a unit vector or absent), `floorHealth` (the hand-back floor, 0 for none) and `pile` (true: the pile is met in the reflex, below). Absent, the melee runs as today (its goldens and tests pin that). A new file `PZAgent_StanceWork.lua` (sorting after `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua`: client files load by name, "StanceM" before "StanceW") registers `K.footwork(p, st, d, px, py, zx, zy, now)` as `K.meleeHitHandback` was registered (Plan 34 Task 4b's block), and the melee's `meet` calls it where it stands or faces today with `work` given. Each tick with no press in flight and no hit reaction:

1. **The gap.** The nearest standing zombie within the reach less `bandLo` is the step back; within the press distance and closing, the press (the meet's own, unchanged: the swing at the predicted collision, the protective shove inside `MinRange`); farther and not closing, the close at a walk (the meet's own). The step and the press alternate as the swing clip and the gap allow; the meet's "no back step after a press" rule stays for the press in flight (the step goes once the clip ends).
2. **The heading.** The repulsion sum over the standing zombies within `workR` weighted by the inverse square of the distance, plus a crawler term (within `crawlerR` x 2, weighted twice), plus the withdraw bias (weight 1.0 at unit length); the candidate headings are that sum turned by 0, ±30, ±60, ±90, ±120, ±150 and 180 degrees, nearest first, and the first clear one is taken: a heading is clear when the squares at 0.6 and 1.0 tiles along it are not solid or solidTrans, the edge from the current square to each is not `isBlockedTo` (the Map's read, `PZAgent_Map.lua`), the edge is not hoppable (`IsoFlagType.HoppableN/W`: the stance cannot climb, #4289), and no crawler lies within `crawlerR` of the point and no fallen zombie within 0.5. The heading is re-chosen every `K.WORK_TICKS` (6 ticks, about 0.1 s) and at once when the nearest standing zombie changes. With no clear heading the character stands and presses (`cornered` step): the press is the defence, and the step goes the tick a heading clears.
3. **The walk.** The step is the stance walk (force-aim held, `setAutoWalkDirection` along the heading in input space through `K.inputVector`, the body snapped on the target through `K.FACING.hold`), never a run (Angus: never turn and run). Its pace is the stance's; the design accepts that a lunge closes faster than the walk opens (§ 1) and relies on the press for the lunge.
4. **The crawler.** A crawler within `crawlerR` with no standing zombie within the press distance is the target: the finish's stomp (the meet's finish machinery at its head), the standing ones faced between stomps (Plan 33's `CRAWLER_FIRST`, in the reflex). A crawler under the heading's first half tile vetoes the heading (item 2).
5. **The pile** (`pile` true). Three or more standing within `K.MELEE_CROWD_R` no longer hand back: the reflex shoves the nearest (the knock-down buys the step; `knock_p` on its leaf), takes the step along the clear heading at once, and presses the next; the `repin` to the nearer stays. The `crowd` hand-back is kept for `pile` false (the goldens).
6. **The hand-backs.** `done handback` why `floor` when the body's health reads at or under `floorHealth` (the planner's flee at the health floor follows); `done handback` why `clear` when no standing zombie is within `releaseR` for `K.WORK_CLEAR_TICKS` (30, half a second) and no crawler within `crawlerR`; `lost` and `kill` as today. No `standing` hand-back at a finish while `work` is given: the standing one within `handbackR` is the next target (the repin).
7. **The lines.** The progress line's result gains `work {heading, turn, clear, gap, n, crawler}` on each heading change and once a second; the steps `work` (stepping), `cornered`, `pile`; the done line's `steps` count them; `contact` (ticks with the nearest standing inside 1.0 tile and no press in flight) on every result line. Every new Java member index-checked at the first tick (not loaded), every read through `PZA.call`; the per-tick budget in contact under 40 Java calls (the melee's today plus the `workR` positions of at most six zombies and the heading's four square reads at a re-choose).

The schema: `pza1.json` gains the `work` arguments, the three steps and the two hand-back whys (PZA/1.13); `tools/pza_schema.py --write`; `PZA.VERSION` moves to 1.13 (the open thread of Plan 34: 1.11 under the 1.12 schema is closed here).

### 3.2 The planner holds the engagement (`ENGAGE_HOLD`; `dispatch.py`, `fight.py`, `group.py`, `crawler.py`, `threat.py`)

With the rule on and the build announcing a melee that takes `work` (the hello's `mod` 1.13 or later, `World.can("melee")` as today), the Fight dispatches the melee with `work` at its dispatch point (`FightDispatch._melee_mode`) and the Python crowd gate is off (the reflex meets the pile). While the melee runs, no Python leg supersedes it: `Fight.busy` holds it (`_melee_holds`) against the retreat's legs (`RETREAT_GAP`, `RETREAT_MELEE`, `RETREAT_REGAIN`), the crawler clear (`CRAWLER_CLEAR`), the side clear, the strung hold, the drag guard's shove and run (`DRAG_GUARD_FIGHT`, `DRAG_DEFER`), the point-blank re-pin's leg and the care's commit (`CARE_COMMIT`, `LEAVE_CARE`'s yields): each of these reads `self.m` running with `work` and answers nothing, logged `engage_hold` {rule} on the why. A re-send goes only when an input changes the reflex's behaviour (`Melee.resend_due`: the mode, the floor swing, the reach, and now the withdraw vector turned more than `WITHDRAW_RESEND_DEG` (30) or the floor), never while a press is in flight; a melee superseded by a melee of the same target keeps its state and the stance and takes the new arguments (the stop hook's `keptOnResend`, extended to the `work` fields). The threat branch's flee under `many` (more than `RETREAT_MAX_N` standing) and `health` read the engagement first: with the melee running under `work` they answer the hold unless the health is at or under the floor, when the ladder's health flee runs as today; `unarmed` and `low` keep their shove-and-stomp Fights, dispatched with `work` too (the stomp and shove modes).

The hand-backs: `floor` ends the Fight's melee and the next choose runs the ladder (the health flee); `clear` ends it and the next choose seeks or stands as today (`seek`, the stance beyond `KEEP_R`); `lost` and `kill` as today.

### 3.3 The withdraw bias (`WITHDRAW`; `group.py`, `fight.py`)

The fighting retreat's heading (`GROUP_LEAVE`'s `retreat`, away from the closing group's centroid, Plan 33 Task 3 fix 3) is passed as the withdraw vector when three or more standing are within `GROUP_ENTRY_R`; the leave's door walk and the `many` run stay behind the floor hand-back (§ 3.2). With fewer than three the vector is absent and the reflex's heading is the repulsion sum alone. The planner recomputes the vector each tick and re-sends only past `WITHDRAW_RESEND_DEG`.

### 3.4 The readings (`tools/trial_score.py`, `grade_x5`)

## 5. Risks

- **The stance walk is slower than a lunge** (§ 1: 0.92 against 1.5 to 2.9 tiles/s). The design relies on the press for the lunge and the step for the walkers; a trial whose hits land under a step with a press due is a timing defect in `meet`, fixed there. Cost if wrong: the hits per kill stay where F20 left them and the deletion (§ 3.7) does not land.
- **The heading's square reads at 60 Hz.** Bounded by the re-choose cadence (`K.WORK_TICKS`) and the four reads; the `cost` group of the frame reads the mod's own cost, read in the smoke round before any trial depends on it (Rule 6's budget is the server's; this is the client's).
- **A diagonal auto-walk under the force-aim** may read a different pace than the back leg's; the smoke round reads `me.pace` under `work` steps.
- **The pile's shove under a hit reaction** is refused `busy` by the engine's `K.attackBusy` (Plan 34's `SHOVE_CLEAR`: the shove waits for the reaction's end): the reflex's hit step already waits; the step along the heading goes during the reaction (the walk is stopped in a reaction today, `reflex`'s `stopWalk`: the design keeps the stop, since the reaction's animation holds the character, #4664).
- **The goldens.** The melee's six decision goldens and the maneuver's tests pin `work` absent; the new behaviour runs only with `work`, behind `FOOTWORK` and `ENGAGE_HOLD` (`T20_SWITCHES`, on by default; off, today's planner byte for byte).
- **The game may update again mid-plan** (Plan 34): CLAUDE.md § 6's clause runs.
