# Plan 34: the column — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** with a column of zombies at its heels the character runs it off before it tends a wound, enters a door only when it can shut it from inside with nobody at the threshold, holds the fighting retreat's gap at the stance's band and swings from it, and lands the shove the guard sends; proven in trials on the `agent` fixture with the model, `street-1` with the model and the arena pair, then one evidence session F20.

**Architecture:** seven switched rules on Plan 33's composition (`16f8774b` and after) in `survive.T19_SWITCHES`: the care's yield barred under a column (`care_yield.py`), the shelter plan's enter read inside and its close sent from inside with the leave's door race (`flee.py`, `night.py`, `group.py`), the retreat's leg by the distance's band with the melee's give-way and the strung hold (`fight.py`, `group.py`), the shove's wait for the reaction's end in the Lua reflex (`PZAgent_Stance.lua`, PZA/1.12); the decision line's `frame_age`; `grade_x4` and the scorecard's `care@3+` cell; the trials; one evidence session.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 (pytest with pytest-xdist, lupa for the Lua tests, the fakes), Kahlua Lua, the register tools, `git worktree`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-plan-34-the-column-design.md` (§ 1 the chain F19 read, § 2 the success criteria, § 3 the design, § 5 the risks). The record behind it: Plan 33's report `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-plan-33-the-group-report.md` §§ 3 and 5, F19's rows #4716-#4726, `testing/artifacts/f_goals_19-20261109-060929/GUIDE.md` §§ 2-6, `docs/reference/research-notes.md#agent-link` (the F19 threads).
