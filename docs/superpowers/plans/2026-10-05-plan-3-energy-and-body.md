# Plan 3 — Energy and Body Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the vanilla energy and weight model with a two-compartment body (fat mass, lean mass) driven by lean-mass resting expenditure plus MET-based activity expenditure, integrated per minute and partitioned per day; write total mass into the vanilla weight slot and the band trait each minute; clamp the Strength level to a lean-mass ceiling with strength memory and route acute states to carry capacity; track a trained aerobic capacity and compute the two endurance coefficients; mirror the four vanilla macro stores; and fill the energy-state term Plan 2 stubbed so hunger reads stomach fill *and* energy balance.

**Architecture:** Five new pure kernel files hold the arithmetic (`NR_Kernel_Body`, `NR_Kernel_Energy`, `NR_Kernel_Partition`, `NR_Kernel_Training`, `NR_Kernel_Strength`, `NR_Kernel_Aerobic` — tables in, tables out, no Java, one statement per line, 100 % line coverage under lupa). Four new server adapters on the slow clock (`NR_Server_Metabolism`, `NR_Server_Training`, `NR_Server_Strength`, `NR_Server_Weight`) read the engine into plain tables, call the kernel and write back: the per-minute expenditure and the per-day partition into a new `record.body` sub-table on the global-modData record; the resistance-training events through server-side hooks; the Strength clamp, trait remap and carry delta; the weight write, direction flags, band push and legacy macro mirror. The fast clock gains two scalars the slow clock stamps (`energyState` into the hunger term; `rmod` into the asleep endurance regeneration it already owns) and nothing else. Because the engine facts the activity and training inputs rest on are unmeasured, the plan runs two live gate sessions before the adapters are written, and each adapter carries a ruled fallback read.

**Tech Stack:** Kahlua (PZ Lua 5.1: no `goto`, no `%d` on floats, no `#` on Java lists); `lupa` 2.8 hosting the kernel under pytest with the line-granular coverage gate; Python 3.13 tools under `tools/`; the harness (`testing/pzt`, `testing/PZTestKit`, profiles, `_template.py`); the claims and science registers and their delta tools.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-nutrition-mod-design.md` — § 4.3 (approved, including the four wave-2 paragraphs), § 4.1 (the two clocks and the handler contract), § 4.2/§ 4.4 (the stomach chain this plan consumes), § 5 row 3 (scope: expenditure, balance, partitioning, masses, weight write, band push, strength ceiling, carry capacity; gate: the perk-level write and anti-cheat rate on a live server; compat gate: BeyondTen's code read before Plan 3), § 6 (performance), § 7 items 2–8, 30, 31, 35, 37, 38, 40, 41 (the defaults this plan ships), § 8 (X36–X41, X48, N1, N6). Platform reading (every mechanism tagged on the pages): `docs/areas/body-effects.md`, `docs/facts/character-stats.md`, `docs/facts/body-and-weight.md`, `docs/facts/nutrition-core.md`, `docs/facts/perks-and-strength.md`, `docs/facts/endurance-fatigue-sleep.md`, `docs/facts/exercise-and-training.md`, `docs/facts/wire-packets.md`, `docs/areas/mp-sync.md`, `docs/platform/server-lifecycle.md`, `docs/platform/lessons.md#rules`, `docs/facts/other-mods/beyondten.md`. Science: `docs/reference/science.tsv` rows named per task (202 settled rows carry the model; 25 open and 4 unverified are named where a number rests on them). The four planning briefings (`plan3-brief-{mod,science,platform,formulas}.md` in the controller's scratchpad, copied to the SDD workspace at Task 0) are the derivation record; the plan is the contract.

## Global Constraints

Everything in Plan 2's Global Constraints holds (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-plan-2-intake.md`): the mod tree and naming; the pure kernel and the `hotpath_lint` rules (fast-path, kernel-shape, kernel-oneline, kernel-java); the vector shape; the wrapper rules; the server side test and event-time reads; the fast-path rules with the one `@rimguard`; persistence on the global-modData record; "a number with no row does not ship"; harness changes first in their own commits; live-session rules; the register read-only for implementers; the gates; pathspec commits; subagent sizing; the environment gotchas. Plan 3 adds:

- **Measure before build.** The adapters that read activity (Tasks 12, 13) are dispatched only after the activity gate (Task 5) has run; the Strength adapter (Task 14) only after the strength gate (Task 3). Each adapter task names its primary read and its ruled fallback; the controller's amendments pick the branch from the gate's verdict. A gate that comes back unmeasured leaves the arm on its fallback, written as such.
- **`record.body` is the one new store.** Every Plan 3 state variable lives in `record.body` (numbers, booleans and tables of numbers only, #1495), versioned by `record.body.bv = 1`; `OnNewGame`'s whole-record reset (Plan 1 `S.reset`) is the respawn reset; the adapter re-creates `record.body` at the next slow tick from the character's then-current weight, sex, traits and Strength level. Derived values (`energyState`, `dmod`, `rmod`, `delta`, `band`) are kept on the record only as change-detection caches.
- **Cadence.** Per game minute (staggered, the Players walk): expenditure, the day accumulators, the aerobic and resistance samples, the accumulator decay, the neural-term integration, the ceiling check and re-assertion, the carry delta, the coefficient stamps, the weight write, the flags, the band check, the legacy mirror, the energy state. Per game day (on `dayIndex` change, catching up missed days one at a time): the partition, the lean law, the cumulative-deficit and memory bookkeeping, the TAC step, the adaptive-thermogenesis step, the ring buffers. Per event: the set-equivalents. Nothing per tick beyond two scalar reads.
- **Two MET tables, never mixed.** Expenditure bills the 2024 Compendium MET of the engine's classified state (settled rows S0018–S0043); the training dose banks the ENGINE's own MET value above its resting floor 1.5 (spec § 7 item 35). The engine's load factor (`1 + 0.35·clamp01(carried/max)²`, #2649) is divided out of the read before either use, so load is charged once (ruling 6).
- **The Strength level is a clamp, never a grant.** The shown level is `min(XP-implied level, ceiling)`; the mod never adds XP, never calls `LevelPerk`/`LoseLevel`, never writes above the XP-implied level (#2701), re-asserts every slow tick (#2740), re-runs vanilla's band remap after every write (#2729), and pushes the trait block only when the trait set changed (#2693). Java's cap is 10 (#2118, #2109); BeyondTen's mastery levels 11–15 live in its own modData and are outside the model (ruling 9).
- **Carry through `setMaxWeightDelta` only** (#2704); the base is never written; the creation-trait factor is read once at first sight before the mod ever writes the delta and stored (`traitCarry`).
- **The weight slot is re-asserted, not trusted** (#2643): total mass written every minute even when unchanged; the three direction flags written every minute (#2644); the band refreshed only when the band changes (#2722, #0534), then pushed (`sendSyncPlayerFields(player, 2)`, #2099), and re-applied as a repair if the trait is missing.
- **Science posture.** Every number carries its `S` id or its `design-phase-v1` label naming the open row it rests on. Thirteen numbers have no settled row (the briefing's GAPS G1–G13): tissue energy densities (S0126), the glycogen store (S0110 — Plan 4), the Forbes/Hall coefficient (a new open row this plan mints), the hypertrophy base rate (S0125), the body-fat split (S1051), adaptive-thermogenesis magnitude (a new open row this plan mints), the strength neural decay and memory constants (S0655), the heat ladder (S0730), the water-loss terms (S1052/S1053 — Plan 4), the Pandolf terms (S0046/S0047/S0132 unverified — NOT shipped; the settled Compendium load METs S0035 ship instead), the sprint extrapolation (S0034), the SMD→percent conversions (named settled rows), the gastric constants (Plan 2's). `python tools/science_check.py --scan mod` → 0 before every mod commit; a superseded id is never cited (the briefing's successor list: e.g. S0095→S0624, S0630→S0718, S0067→S0590, S0614→S0069/S0070/S0087, S0653→S0126, S0656→S0125).

  6. **Load is charged once:** the engine's own load factor is divided out of the metabolic target; expenditure then adds the Compendium load-walking floor (S0035) while walking loaded; the training dose multiplies by vanilla's heavy-load factors {1, 1.5, 1.9, 2.3, 2.8} (#2255) — the Pandolf terms are not shipped (unverified rows).
  7. **Partitioning:** a deficit is paid by fat up to Alpert's ceiling (69 kcal per kg fat per day, S0056) and the overflow by lean at `RHO_LEAN` with the training/protein protection; **the energy the protection spares is paid by fat beyond the ceiling** (mass balance closes; consistent with S0064/S0072 where trained high-protein groups lose more fat). Tissue densities ship as Hall's `RHO_LEAN = 1816`, `RHO_FAT = 9441` kcal/kg naming open S0126 (`design-phase-v1`).
  8. **"Immobilised" is a leg fracture or splint on the server's body damage, never a sedentary day:** the logarithmic disuse curve (S0069/S0612) applies only then; sedentary days use the ordinary deficit law. Cost if wrong: a bedridden-by-choice character loses no lean by disuse.
  9. **The Strength ceiling clamps the Java perk level 0–10** (#2118); `Lmax = 10`; BeyondTen's mastery levels are outside the model and the catalog page says so (Task 1 confirms BeyondTen does not write the perk level through a setter the clamp would fight; if it does, the minute re-assertion wins and the interaction is stated).
  10. **Write policy:** a rise of one level after the ceiling has stood ≥ shown+1 for six consecutive game hours; a fall of at most one level per game hour; re-assertion of the shown level every slow tick; never above the XP-implied level; the band remap after every write; the trait push on change only.
  11. **The carry delta is `traitCarry × (1 + eAcute)`,** `eAcute` clamped [−0.20, +0.03] (spec § 7 item 30); the research report's "remainder factor" is dropped (it grants carry without XP, discrepancy 7). The hours-awake knee is **18 h** for both the carry term and the endurance coefficient (conflict C7; the spec's § 4.3 number). Dawn-to-noon is 06:00–12:00 game time. Dehydration, caffeine, vitamin D and hours awake are Plan 4/5 inputs, stubbed neutral here and named.
  12. **Aerobic capacity and the two coefficients** as § 4.3 and the endurance report's proposed model, with: `protFactor` = the protein gate's two rungs (1.00/0.85, S0715); `excessPct = 100·max(0, FM − FMref)/W` with `FMref` the sex's normal-band fat fraction at 80 kg; Gprot "low" = P < 0.8 g/kg/d (S0509); energy availability `EA = (INday − actKcalDay)/LM` against 30 (S0691); the heat ladder {1.00, 1.10, 1.25, 1.50, 2.00} naming open S0730; iron, glycogen, dehydration, caffeine, alcohol, sleep debt and the balance dial stubbed neutral (Plan 4/5) and named. Excess fat appears in the drain coefficient per the spec (S0723); whether vanilla's own band-trait drain charge double-counts it is Plan 5's call when it reproduces the drain.
  13. **The legacy macro mirror** (sandbox `NR.LegacyMirror`, default on): calories = the trailing-24 h energy balance clamped to the store; proteins = a piecewise map of the trailing-24 h protein intake per kg that keeps vanilla's ×1.5 XP arm reachable from ~1.07 g/kg/d (the legacy behaviour other mods expect; the double-count with the lean-gain protein gate is accepted and labelled); carbohydrates and lipids = the trailing-24 h intake minus a 300 g / 70 g reference. **Missed-intake reconciliation (§ 7 item 38) is deferred to Plan 8,** where the two-client rig exists to test it; Plan 3's mirror is a plain overwrite each minute.
  14. **Energy state:** `E = clamp(1 + 0.5·clamp(−EB24h/1500, −1, 1) + 0.5·fatDep, 0.5, 2.0)` and the hunger target gains an additive floor under deficit, `hunger = clamp((1 − fill)·E + 0.15·max(0, E − 1), 0, 1)`, so a starving character who just ate bulk can still feel hungry. No report defines the form; a game choice for the re-review.
  15. **Adaptive thermogenesis:** `ATmax = 0.10`, full at half the fat store gone, τ 7 d on, 14 d off — a game choice naming the open row this plan mints (G6).
  16. **The body-fat split** is the band-anchored piecewise-linear table of § A with build offsets, naming open S1051 (`design-phase-v1`); the responder constant `R = exp(0.3·z)` clamped [0.5, 1.8] is drawn once by the adapter with `ZombRand` (the kernel never draws).
  17. **Direction flags:** inc when the 7-day trend > +0.02 kg/d, lot when > +0.10, dec when < −0.02 — a game choice; written every minute.
  18. **Caffeine tolerance (C6) and the per-meal protein cap (discrepancy 3) are Plan 4/5 questions;** Plan 3 ships no caffeine term and no per-meal cap (the spec's "no distribution term").
  19. **The two gate sessions mint one new experiment** (the perk-level write, the next unused X id) and settle or re-scope X36, X37, X38, X39, X40, X48; the BeyondTen code read settles its open rows without an X id.
