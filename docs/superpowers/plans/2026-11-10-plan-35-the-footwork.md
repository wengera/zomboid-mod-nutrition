# Plan 35: the footwork — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** in contact one reflex at the client's tick rate owns the press, the facing and the footwork together, no planner leg supersedes it, and the pile is met in the reflex (the shove, the step along the clearest heading, the next press) instead of handed back; proven in trials on the `agent` fixture with the model (the death rate and the median kills by the round, the contact seconds), the arena waves and `street-1`, then on one evidence session F21, then the Python legs it replaced deleted.

**Architecture:** the cerebellum's second cut (memory `cerebellum-layer`): the melee skill gains optional `work` arguments (PZA/1.13) and a new Lua file `PZAgent_StanceWork.lua` registers `K.footwork`, called by the meet between presses: the gap, the heading from the repulsion sum turned to the first clear square, the stance walk along it, the crawler as a target, the pile without a hand-back, the `floor` and `clear` hand-backs. The planner (`survive.T20_SWITCHES`: `FOOTWORK`, `ENGAGE_HOLD`, `WITHDRAW`, on by default) dispatches the melee with `work` at the existing dispatch point, holds it against every leg while it runs, passes the fighting retreat's heading as the withdraw bias and re-sends only on a changed input. The scorer gains the contact seconds and a round summary; `grade_x5` folds `x4` in and reads the work; F21 is the evidence; the harden task deletes the dead legs by name.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 (pytest with pytest-xdist, lupa for the Lua tests, the fakes), Kahlua Lua, the register tools, `git worktree`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-plan-35-the-footwork-design.md` (§ 1 the probe's reading of the deaths, § 2 the criteria, § 3 the design, § 5 the risks). The record behind it: Plan 34's report `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-plan-34-the-column-report.md`, F20's rows #4748-#4758 and `testing/artifacts/f_goals_20-20261109-155512/GUIDE.md`, `testing/trials/trials.md` rounds two to four, the controller's probe `probe/deadtime.py` (copied into the plan's workspace), `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua`'s header, `dispatch.py`'s and `maneuvers/melee.py`'s docstrings.
