# Plan 37 — the disposition: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** one owner of contact at every group size: the Lua reflex executes the disposition it is handed (`hunt`, `hold`, `string`), the planner picks the disposition on events with a dwell and a hysteresis and hands over to the Flee only through the reflex's safe release, and a group of eight to eleven is strung out and killed instead of dragging the character down.

**Architecture:** the three-speed architecture stands (the Lua reflex at the client tick under a Python `Maneuver`, the planner at 2 Hz, the model above) and gains its missing seam: in contact a decision changes the running skill's parameters by a re-send that keeps the footwork's state, never by a restart. The dispositions are tables over a fixed set of reflex primitives; the selector (`planner/disposition.py`) is the one decider in contact; the string primitive is the new reflex. The crowd waves and a pocket fixture reproduce F21's and F22's deaths on demand; the proof is read on the trial rounds (CLAUDE.md § 5 Trials); one evidence session F23 at the end.

**Tech Stack:** Kahlua Lua (PZ Build 42.22.2 client), Python 3 planner, the PZA/1 schema generator (`tools/pza_schema.py`), the trial loop (`x_trial.py`, `driverlib/trial.py`, `tools/trial_score.py`, `tools/trial_snapshot.py`), pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-plan-37-the-disposition-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Exploratory lane (P32-R): implementer tests and lint gates, no review per task; the next trial boot is the review. The evidence lane (F23, its write-up, `grade_x7`, the rows) takes one opus review.
- Every Lua change: `python tools/kahlua_lint.py mod testing/experiments testing/PZTestKit testing/PZAgent` 0, `python tools/luabalance.py <files>` BALANCED, `python tools/pza_schema.py --check` in sync after `--write`; no `%` on a value that can be negative (CLAUDE.md § 5); a smoke boot on the arena pair before any trial depends on it.
- A protocol change is an edit of `testing/agent/base/schema/pza1.json` followed by `--write`; PZA/1.16; `PZA.VERSION` in `PZAgent_Core.lua`; `test_pza1.py`'s committed-line decode guard re-pinned.
- The contract (spec § 3.1): in contact no planner row sends a non-melee command over the engaged melee; a change of disposition is a re-send that keeps the footwork's state; a hand-over to the Flee goes through `released` or `floor`. The `restarts` cell reads 0.
- Deletions follow CLAUDE.md § 3's reset rule: every deleted `def test_` named in the task report with the dead code it tested.
- Constants live in `policies.py` (planner) or the `K.` table of the Lua file that reads them, each with a one-line comment naming its source (the speeds name the frames they were read off).
- Pathspec commits, subjects `Plan 37 Task N: <what>`; no Claude attribution; implementers never push; worktree tasks reach `main` by the controller's cherry-pick.
- The trials' rules (CLAUDE.md § 5 Trials): a trial is one scored line; a hit is traced only where health was lost; the proof is read on the rounds; a narrow fix takes a pair smoke and one trial on the evidence fixture, not a restarted round.
- Angus's standing rules: the fighting retreat at three (never turn and run under what a backpedal holds), the string above it (walk away, turn and kill the leaders, walk again: not running away), the swing rides the back step, the shove is a chosen knock-down, every wound analyzed, kills and hits per kill the metrics.
