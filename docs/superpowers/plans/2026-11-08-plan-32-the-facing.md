# Plan 32: the facing — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the character faces the zombie it fights by the engine's own body angle at every press and through the stance, proven by a probe that reads the engine's facing getters every tick under each candidate mechanism, adopted in the reflex, then the coward fix and the window glass check, then one evidence session.

**Architecture:** a harness probe act (PZA/1.11: four facing getters in the frame's `me`, a `probe` act type run by a Lua reflex skill in `OnTick` that applies one mechanism for N ticks and emits the ring per tick), a zombie-free trial driver on the arena that runs the mechanisms in order and a scorer that ranks them in a facing trials log; the winner replaces the `faceThisObject` writes of the meet reflex and the stance skill; `grade_x2` reads the facing error at every press and stance leg from `bodyAngle`; the planner's coward rules and the window glass gate follow as switches with replay tests; one evidence session closes.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 (pytest, lupa for the Lua tests, the fakes), Kahlua Lua (`kahlua_lint.py`, `luabalance.py`), `tools/pza_schema.py`, RCON through `xlib`'s `xs.rcon`, the register tools (`claims_check.py`, `claims_delta.py`, `page_lint.py`), `git worktree`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-plan-32-the-facing-design.md` (§ 1 the problem and the record, § 2 the success criteria, § 3 the approaches, § 4 the design: 4.1 the ring and the probe, 4.2 the trial, 4.3 adoption, 4.4 the coward fix, 4.5 the window glass, 4.6 harden, § 6 the risks). The research behind it: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-05-plan-31-the-rebuild/facing-jar-research.md` (29 jar claims, 6 experiments), `facing-mods-research.md`, `visual-review-f17.md`, and the scratch read of F17's frames (the controller's `facing_f17.py`, `facing_f17_acts2.py`).
