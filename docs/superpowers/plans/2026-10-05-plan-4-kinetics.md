# Plan 4 — Kinetics Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace Plan 2's provisional pool with a declarative per-nutrient record engine — one record per nutrient (pool, elimination, absorption and interactions, requirement, status ladder, excess ladder, repletion cap), every number resting on a science-register row or a labelled game choice naming its open row — plus the fast pools (water, sodium, potassium) that take THIRST over from vanilla's drain, the acute states (caffeine, alcohol, glycogen and blood glucose, sleep debt, the impairment unit, refeeding), and the dials; and feed every scalar Plan 3 holds neutral.

**Architecture:** Four new pure kernels — `NR_Kernel_Nutrients` (the generic engine over a declarative record table `NR_Data_Records`), `NR_Kernel_Fluids` (water and electrolytes, the thirst view), `NR_Kernel_Acute` (caffeine, alcohol, glycogen, sleep, the impairment unit, refeeding) — and one new server adapter `NR_Server_Nutrients` on the slow clock after Metabolism, consuming the per-minute absorbed vector and stamping grades, `thirstTarget`, the Plan 3 scalars and an `epoch` the effects plan rebuilds on. The nutrient vector grows by twenty-two keys. The fast clock gains one scalar read (`thirstTarget`, written every tick as hunger is) and keeps the ghost gate and the auto-drink call, which the handler now brackets so an auto-drink lands in the water pool. Because the thirst takeover, the sweat term, sleep debt and alcohol rest on engine facts no run has measured — and every live run so far ran with the fixture's `SleepAllowed=false` — the plan front-loads a harness wave and two live gate sessions, and each adapter carries a ruled fallback.

**Tech Stack:** Kahlua; `lupa` 2.8 with the line-granular coverage gate; Python 3.13 tools; the harness (`pzt`, PZTestKit, profiles, `_template.py`); the claims and science registers and their delta tools.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-nutrition-mod-design.md` — § 4.4 (approved, including the three wave-2 additions), § 4.2/§ 4.3 (the stomach chain and the body model this plan feeds), § 4.5 (the effects that READ the grades — Plan 5; this plan exposes, never writes a moodle, POISON, FOOD_SICKNESS, FATIGUE, INTOXICATION or health), § 5 row 4, § 6, § 7 items 9–13, 32, 33, 35, 37, § 8. Platform reading: `docs/areas/new-nutrients.md`, `docs/facts/character-stats.md`, `docs/facts/eating-pipeline.md#fluid-path`, `docs/facts/food-item-model.md`, `docs/facts/body-and-weight.md`, `docs/facts/endurance-fatigue-sleep.md`, `docs/facts/health-surfaces.md`, `docs/areas/body-effects.md`, `docs/platform/sandbox-options.md`, `docs/platform/lessons.md#rules`. ## Global Constraints

Everything in Plan 3's Global Constraints holds (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-plan-3-energy-and-body.md`), and through it Plan 2's. Plan 4 adds:

- **The record is the store.** `record.nutrients`, `record.fluids` and `record.acute` are the three new sub-tables (numbers, booleans, strings and tables of numbers only, #1495), each versioned (`nv`, `fv`, `av`); `OnNewGame`'s whole-record reset is the respawn reset; the adapter creates them at the next slow tick. `record.pool` (Plan 2's accumulator) stays on the record as a diagnostic and is no longer mirrored.

The engine is one generic loop; the tier lives in the record's `kind`.
