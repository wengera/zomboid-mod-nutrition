# Plan 5 — Effects Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the grades, pools and acute states Plans 3–4 compute into consequences the player feels, through a declarative effects table composed into one coefficient set per player and applied through the five channels the spec names — stat coefficients, trait toggles, body-damage setters, health drains and event-driven writes — with deficiency a penalty and adequacy never a bonus.

**Architecture:** One data file `NR_Data_Effects.lua` (surfaces with fold operators and clamps; rows keyed by a selector on a grade, rung, band or flag) and one pure kernel `NR_Kernel_Effects.lua` (the band vector, the composer, the Severity scaling, the protein-energy grade, the night-vision machine, the aiming multiplier, the fatigue offset) build `record.effects` on the slow clock, rebuilt only when the nutrient epoch, the day close, the dials or one of twelve integer bands changes. A new slow adapter `NR_Server_Effects.lua`, called explicitly at the end of the Nutrients minute, applies the trait toggles, the regeneration setters, the per-part wound/bleeding/infection folds, the cold fold, the health drains and the bruise roll. The fast kernel gains the FATIGUE writer (three adds from slow scalars), the endurance delta fold (dmod on drain, rmod on regen, written every tick) and the stress floor; the fast handler gains the panic/unhappiness/sickness floors, the absolute temperature target and the INTOXICATION writer. A client wrapper re-applies the aiming multiplier after each shot from the mirror. Because every surface rests on an engine seat no run has measured, the plan is measure-first: a harness wave, a jar desk task, and two live gate sessions precede the kernels; every adapter carries a ruled fallback.

**Tech Stack:** Kahlua; `lupa` 2.8 with the line-granular coverage gate; Python 3.13 tools; the harness (`pzt`, PZTestKit, TKX probes, profiles); the claims and science registers and their delta tools.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-nutrition-mod-design.md` — § 4.5 (default, revised by wave 2), § 4.1 (the external-delta fold), § 4.3 (the two endurance coefficients), § 4.4 (the grades and epoch), § 4.9 (the Evolving Traits World gate), § 5 row 5, § 6, § 7 items 14–17, 32–33, 41, § 8. The four planning briefings (`plan5-brief-{mod,platform,science,formulas}.md`, copied to the SDD workspace at Task 0) are the derivation record; the formulas briefing's §§ A–J are the model specification every kernel task is coded from and its § I holds the hand values; the platform briefing's §§ 1–6 are the engine seats and § 9 the experiments to mint; the science briefing's tables are the rows.

## Global Constraints

Everything in Plan 4's Global Constraints holds (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-plan-4-kinetics.md`), and through it Plans 2–3's. Plan 5 adds:

- **Deficiency is a penalty; adequacy is never a bonus** (spec § 4.5). Every row is flat at and above the replete grade; the two named exceptions (vitamin C's cold credit in the exerting cold-exposed population; the BalanceBonus dial) carry `bonus = true` and are the only rows the Severity dial does not scale.
- **Measure before build.** The fast-handler and adapter tasks (8–10) dispatch after the two gate sessions (Tasks 3–4); each names its primary read and its ruled fallback; a surface whose seat came back unmeasured ships computed and mirrored but UNAPPLIED, with a limitation string.
- **Every number rests on a row** (Plan 4's rule #3004: a settled science row, an open row the mod labels at the number, or a game choice a plan ruling names and labels at the number). Task 1 mints the open rows for the gaps the science briefing lists.
- **The fast region** gains no exp, pow, loop, allocation or protected call: the FATIGUE writer is three adds and a clamp from slow scalars; the endurance fold is one subtraction, two compares, one multiply; each mood floor is one get, one compare, at most one set; `hotpath_lint` 0; `bench_fast` within 10 % of #3003 (3.24–3.47 µs) — the region grows by the largest step since Plan 1 and the cost is measured, not assumed.
- **No write outside the five channels**: the adapters' writes are listed in Task 8's and 9's briefs and reviewed against the mod briefing's § 10 table (27 writes today); POISON ships 0 at every rung (ruling 12); the mod's own per-minute drains are the only killers and run only behind `NR.DeficienciesCanKill`.
