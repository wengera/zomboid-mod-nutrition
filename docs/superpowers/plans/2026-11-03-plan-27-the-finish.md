# Plan 27: the finish — implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** a survival agent that finishes what it knocks down and lasts through its tired spells: iteration 19 gives the planner a stomp at any fallen zombie under the `strain` and `pair` flees, a `many` episode that ends when the group has strung out, a hold that fights a lone zombie by the shove and the stomp and otherwise stands still to recover, a spent dressing kept on a long bleed and a low fight that swings once after three failed shoves; read live over four sessions, with iteration 20 ruled from the first two.

**Architecture:** the Python planner under `testing/agent/base/planner/` (`survive.py`, `threat.py`, `finish.py`, `hold.py`, `shove.py`, `fight.py`, `care.py`, `policies.py`, `flee.py`) over the unchanged PZA/1.7.1 Lua; new grades `grades/e16.py` and `grades/f8.py` (one module per grade, frozen per artifact, each copying the live names it reads, P26-Q); thin chain and LLM drivers copied under their own keys; the write-ups on `docs/platform/agent.md#agent`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 (pytest, the fakes and the decision-golden replays), the PZA/1.7.1 Lua unchanged, the `claude` CLI for the LLM source, the register tools (`claims_check.py`, `page_lint.py`, `reference_gen.py`, `claims_delta.py`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-plan-27-the-finish-design.md` (§ 1 the readings, § 2 the work, § 3 the carried rulings, § 4 what is out); the guides' notes `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-plan-27-guides-notes.md`.
