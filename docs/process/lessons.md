# Process lessons
Verified against 42.21.0 (4a0e9546ec) · 2026-10-23 · scope: the process pitfalls behind CLAUDE.md's rules, each with the incident that made it a rule and where that incident is recorded.

This page is untagged. [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) states each rule in one line; this page says why, so read it before you do a thing for the first time.
Each entry gives the rule in bold, the incident in a line or two, and its source.
Engine and modding rules are on [platform/lessons](../platform/lessons.md), not here.

How to read a source:
- A plan, spec or research path is read at the tag `program-record-v19` (`git show program-record-v19:<path>`). [`docs/superpowers/HISTORY.md`](../superpowers/HISTORY.md) indexes the plans.
- A ruling id (`B3-2`, `P12-15`) is in that plan's ledger, `.superpowers/sdd/<plan>/progress.md`. The ledgers are local and gitignored, and `L` numbers are lines in them.
- A run id (`x391g`) names a folder under `testing/artifacts/`, whose `GUIDE.md` is its reading guide.
- `#NNNN` names a row of the claims register or its archive, and a short sha names a commit.

## Live runs

- **Boot a one-client session at 10 GB available and a two-client one at 12, and tune a floor on the memory rows, never by waiting.** x411 booted at 13.84 GB free and fell to 0.3 GB at ready (#3818), which set a 12 GB floor; on 2026-11-03 the floor held Plan 27's first boot for over an hour at 11.5 to 11.8 GB while the host's processes used 5.5 GB of 31.4 and its kernel pools 9.6 GB (a driver leak, not the harness's), and Angus asked for less waiting. The reading: `xlib.free_gb` reads `ullAvailPhys`, free plus standby cache, the right metric; E17, E18 and F9 ran steady at 3.3 GB available from 12.0 to 16.6 GB before the boot, the game growing into what was there with both JVMs at `-Xmx3072m` and the client's textures doubled and uncompressed; the in-session stop (`LIVE_MIN_FREE_GB`) guards a paged run. Ruling P27-4: the `java.exe` working sets in every memory row, the one-client floor 10 GB; the heaps are tuned once the rows show the need. Its texture clause (the client's textures single-sized and compressed at every prepare) lasted one boot: E19's first client took 38 s over `LoadTileDefinitions` against E18's 4.7 s and stalled past 63 s in `loadAnimalDefinitions` against 50 ms, the 120 s ready timeout ended the boot (no reading: not a run), and the two keys were withdrawn after that one boot, which changed both at once, so the load cost is the pair's and not one key's; the memory need is read off the Java rows instead. Source: the Plan 27 ledger (11:50Z, 12:35Z), `task-3b-brief.md`, the aborted boot `e_day_19-20261103-071911`'s client debug log.
- **Split a driver longer than about 15 minutes of wall time into separate sessions, each with its own driver.** Claude Code's background-shell reaper killed x242 mid-run for memory, and the 60-ghost sessions took free memory down to 0.55 GB. Source: Plan 10c ruling H2-1.
