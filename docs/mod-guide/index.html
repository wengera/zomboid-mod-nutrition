A survivor's field guide

Nutrition Revamp

Your survivor has a body now. What you eat fills a stomach, feeds a store of every vitamin and mineral, and moves fat and muscle. Hunger, thirst, weight and Strength all follow from it.

This guide sets the mod beside the game you know. Each chart shows vanilla in one colour and the mod in another, and every number on the page comes from a measured run, the mod's own code, the game's own code, or a published study. Tap a source chip under a chart to see where its numbers came from.

Your first day

You spawn, find some tinned tuna, and eat a tin at breakfast, lunch, mid-afternoon and dinner.

Vanilla

Your hunger starts at nothing. Each tin takes its hunger value off at once, and hunger climbs back at a steady rate until the next one.

Skip your meals and the hunger moodle appears within a few hours, then climbs a level at a time. Thirst climbs at its own steady rate while you are awake, whatever you eat.

This mod

The mod writes your hunger every game minute from your body. A tin fills your stomach and your store of recent energy, so hunger dips after each meal and creeps back as the stomach empties. It never drops to nothing.

Skip your meals and the mod's Energy moodle shows Peckish, later than vanilla's first hunger level. Your thirst follows the water your body loses, which at rest runs ahead of vanilla's clock. The panel on the ; key shows each of these as words, bands or numbers, as the server chooses.

Read next Hunger Eating and the stomach Thirst and salt What you see on screen

The science

The vanilla lines are the game's own rates run forward (VM1, VM3 to VM5): thirst rises linearly and hunger at a rate that eases as it climbs (#0476, #0474), and a food subtracts its HungerChange (#0006). The moodles cross at the times of #0492 to #0495, at the thresholds of #0508. The mod's lines are its code replaying a fresh no-trait body from 07:00; the hunger line's proof run is x362 (#3703). The Managed cap on thirst is #3123, held live in #3103.

The measured lines are the vanilla sessions x441, x442 and x443 on 42.21 (#3882, #3883, #3884) and M1, the mod's fresh body measured live (x446, #3890).

Skipping meals and starving

You miss a whole day of meals and then eat again. Later the food runs out altogether, and you have only water.

Vanilla

Hunger climbs at its steady rate whatever your body holds, and any food takes its hunger value off. Hunger reaches the top level about hours after your last meal, and there it drains your health until you die.

In a live test a vanilla survivor whose hunger was already past halfway starved to death about a day and a half later, when the game's code predicts.

This mod

Your hunger follows your body. It rises through a missed day, and the next day you eat more than usual. It also grows as you lose fat. In Managed mode it stops below the top Hungry level, so hunger itself never kills.

A long fast still can. With water and no food, your body runs short of thiamine, and beriberi, a thiamine deficiency that fails the heart, kills long before your fat runs out. After a long spell of starving, the mod marks you at refeeding risk.

Read next Hunger The body Deficiency and excess Deficiencies can kill

The science

Vanilla's starvation is VM10: hunger from 0 reaches the top level at 34.84 h, then health drains at the DayLength 4 rate (#0518) with no regeneration (#2353, #2354). The live session x442 started a vanilla survivor at HUNGER 0.60 with thirst held at 0, and its death and every graded quantity matched the model run from its own start (#3882).

The Managed cap is #3123, held live on an empty stomach in #3074. The mod's hunger after a missed day follows Johnstone 2002 (S1613) and Clayton 2016 (S1614); x27 read a rise of about a fifth live (#3670) on an earlier build. Hunger rising with fat lost follows Polidori 2016 (S1254); a thin start's hunger is #3874.

The long fast is the mod's code replaying its no-trait body with water every two hours and no food (proof x434, #3869). Its first lethal state is cardiac beriberi, from the thiamine store; the dial that lets a deficiency kill is #3105. The refeeding risk state is #3875 and #2987; the refeeding event itself has no reading and is not promised. The vanilla weight dots are a 42.20.4 control run whose hunger was pinned (#0500).

Living on one food

You find a case of tinned tuna and eat nothing else, a tin at each of your four meals, for weeks.

Vanilla

Tuna fills you like any other food. The game tracks no vitamins and does not remember what you ate, so weeks of tuna do you no harm.

This mod

Tuna carries plenty of protein and no vitamin C. Your vitamin C store runs down through Marginal and Depleted to Clinical, which is scurvy. Thiamine runs short as well, and on tuna alone beriberi kills you first.

Eating the same food day after day also lowers your mood, up to the first Unhappy and Bored level and no further. An orange a day keeps you alive and refills your vitamin C.

Read next Deficiency and excess Monotony Nutrients and foods

The science

The vitamin C line is the mod's code replaying four TunaTinOpen a day with water; its grade crossings fall where the code puts them live (x151r, #2977, #2978). The mod calibrates vitamin C's onset on Hodges' controlled series as Padayatty 2016 reports it (S0225), so the code agrees with that mark by construction; S0224 is the self-experimenter. Vitamin C is never sped up by the onset option. On this diet fibre turns Marginal first, then choline, and cardiac beriberi (thiamine) is the first lethal state.

The mood readings are #3683, from x341 and x342 on the test food TKX.Mono450; the guide reads the stat against the first-level thresholds of #2369. The orange's thiamine (0.120 mg against a tuna tin's 0.071) keeps the thiamine grade off Clinical. The week of improvement is Ververs 2019 (S0229).

A year at the gym

You train hard all year, a few hard sets every day, then stop for a year and start again.

Vanilla

Each set grants Strength experience, and protein in your stores multiplies it. At normal protein a year of training ends at level . Only the game's protein bonus reaches level , on day .

Read from the game's code, with no live check: stop training and rust takes experience back so slowly that the level holds through a year off. A fresh character sits exactly on its level's line, so its first rust step, around day , costs it a level.

This mod

Your level reads your muscle. Training builds it fastest with plenty of protein and enough food, and ever more slowly toward a natural cap. With too little protein it builds less, and on too little food it slips back.

Stop and your muscle fades back toward your body's set point. Train again and it returns faster than it first came.

Read next Strength The body Exercise and energy

The science

The vanilla lines are VM11 and VM12: 360 XP a set (#3844), the level thresholds (#2102), the cap (#2109), the protein factor pinned at 1, 1.5 or 0.7 (#2112, code only), and rust at one XP a step once its timer passes (#2151, #2155). The first rust step of a fresh character at day 48.6 is a reading of the code with no live run. The mod's lines are its daily Strength step at 20 hard sets a week; x434 and x435 showed the daily closes equal to the code's replay (#3869, #3870, #3871).

The natural cap is Kouri 1995's fat-free mass index limit (S1915) as a level (#3853). Detraining and regain follow Blocquiaux 2020 (S0618), whose subjects regained their trained strength within eight weeks; the mod regains more slowly than that. Psilander 2019 (S0078) found strength stayed above baseline after twenty weeks off, with no muscle-memory advantage on retraining. The detraining and regain arms were never measured live.

Choosing your body

At character creation you pick Strong, Weak, Overfat or Gaunt. Here is the body each pick starts with, and where it goes when you leave it alone.

Vanilla

A muscle trait sets your starting Strength level: Weak starts at level . A weight trait sets your starting weight. From then on Strength follows experience and the weight traits follow the scale.

This mod

Each pick builds a body: lean mass, fat and an innate strength. Weak starts at level , the mod's own floor. Left idle, a muscle start fades partway toward its set point, so a Strong survivor settles a level lower.

A thin start begins with too little fat. You are hungrier than usual and regain weight until your fat recovers.

Read next Body traits and character creation Strength The body Hunger

The science

x436 read the creation bodies and levels live (#3872); a thin start's hunger is #3874, and the trait bands #3856 and #3873. The permanent half of a muscle trait's lean difference is #3855. Vanilla's creation weights are #0533, and its start level is 5 plus the trait's boost, clamped at 0, so its Weak starts at 0 (a script read); the mod's Weak 1 is its own floor.

The drift lines are the mod's code over 90 days, eating when hunger asks; vanilla's rust step is a reading of the code with no live run.

Coffee, beer and no sleep

You live on coffee, drink a few beers one evening, and cut a night short.

Vanilla

A cup of coffee takes a fixed amount off your fatigue, the same on the first day as the hundredth, and it lasts only when the server has sleep on. A beer raises drunkenness by an amount fixed by the drink, a little more when you are hungry. At the default day length it clears within minutes, as a live session measured.

Sleep has no debt and no effect on hunger.

This mod

Your body absorbs caffeine and clears it over hours, and a daily coffee drinker builds tolerance, so the same cups do less. Beer raises your blood alcohol, which sets how drunk you are, and heavy drinking leaves a hangover once the alcohol has gone.

When the server turns sleep on, a short night builds sleep debt, and the debt makes you hungrier.

Read next Caffeine, alcohol and sleep Hunger The sleep options

The science

The caffeine lines are the mod's code with a 5 h half-life (x151r, #2982) and a tolerance with a 7-day time constant; the offset follows caffeine and its tolerance live (#3077). Both are game choices on open science rows, so the chart draws no study. Vanilla's cup is −10 fatigue a litre (#2949), and with sleep off vanilla resets fatigue every update (#3009).

Vanilla's drunkenness is VM16: 400 × alcohol × the hunger factor a drink (#2923, #2926), decaying 45.36 a game hour at DayLength 4 (#2922). The mod's blood alcohol and the drunkenness it writes are #3086. The hangover of the live session x447 started where the code's replay of its own drinks puts it, in the regrade x447-regrade-20261015 (#3891); that session's night is not used here.

x301 and x302 read the sleep debt and its hunger factor live (#3677, #3638), after Brondel 2010's one short night (S1566).

Reference

The systems

Each system below starts with what the game does, then what the mod does instead. The charts follow, then what can now happen to you and what no longer can. The science behind each one sits in the closed box at its end.

Nutrients and foods

Vanilla

Every food carries calories, carbohydrate, fat and protein. The game counts nothing else, and its developers set many values by hand.

This mod

The mod re-bases each vanilla food on published food-composition data and gives it a full nutrient table: the vitamins, the minerals, fibre, essential fats, sodium, potassium, caffeine and alcohol.

Your body keeps a store of each nutrient. It fills from food, empties at its own pace, and shows on the panel as a grade: Replete, Marginal, Depleted or Clinical. Cooked dishes and crafted food count their ingredients. Drinks count by what is in them. A food from another mod that the table does not know gets values estimated from its type and its calories.

What can happen

  • Living on one food brings on scurvy and the other deficiencies. See Deficiency and excess.

What no longer happens

Nothing for this system.

The science

The food values come from the mod's food table, built from USDA FoodData Central records and written into the game as an item pass (data/food-nutrients.json, generated by tools/food_nutrients.py). The live run x172b ate a whole apple and a slice of bread and landed the table's vector exactly (#3182, #3183).

The nutrient stores are the mod's kernel pools. Each has a daily requirement, a half-life or a calibrated onset rate, and the four grades the panel shows.

Eating and the stomach

Vanilla

An eat applies the food's values the moment it finishes. Hunger drops by the food's hunger value at once.

This mod

The server records every eat and drink made through the game's own actions. Food lands in a stomach that empties over the following hours before your body absorbs it. Drinks leave quickly and a solid meal leaves slowly.

Some nutrients absorb better or worse with what else is in the meal. Fat-soluble vitamins absorb better with fat. Eat past a comfortable amount and the Overfull moodle rises. It warns you and never stops you eating.

What can happen

  • Overeating raises the Overfull moodle, and it never blocks an eat. The game itself still refuses an eat at the top level of its own fullness moodle.

What no longer happens

  • A food's hunger value no longer sets your hunger. Only its bulk in your stomach and the energy it carries count.
The science

The stomach is a two-phase emptying model, liquid and solid, read live in x363 (#3702, #3668). The mod computes hunger from the stomach and the energy pool and ignores the item's HungerChange (#3614); vanilla couples the two (#0503).

The Overfull moodle reads the stomach's mass (#3623, #3778). Vanilla's own refusal at its fullness level stands (#0521, #2889).

Hunger

Vanilla

Hunger rises at a steady rate, and a big meal pauses it for a while. Each food subtracts its hunger value. Left long enough, hunger reaches the top Hungry moodle and drains your health until you die.

This mod

In the default Managed mode the mod writes your hunger every game minute from your body. Three things drive it: how full your stomach is, with protein filling more and drinks less; a pool of recently eaten energy that fades over a few hours; and a deficit drive from the last day's energy balance and the fat you have lost.

The time of day, recent hard exercise, sleep debt and nicotine withdrawal nudge it too.

What can happen

  • You get hungrier as you lose fat.
  • A missed day makes you eat more the next day.
  • A short night raises the next day's hunger. See Caffeine, alcohol and sleep.

What no longer happens

  • In Managed mode hunger never reaches the top Hungry moodle, so hunger never kills.
  • Hunger no longer drops to nothing after a meal.
The science

Managed mode caps the written HUNGER below the top moodle's threshold (#3123); live, HUNGER stayed at or under that cap on an empty stomach (#3074). Vanilla's top Hungry level is where its starvation drain runs (#0508, #0516).

The meal-size curve is fitted to Callahan 2004's liquid preloads (S1247) and checked against Cummings 2004's intermeal intervals (S1248); x361 measured the live interval (#3700). Six against three meals is Ohkawara 2013 (S1608), which the mod does not reproduce.

The fat-loss drive follows Polidori 2016's rise in intake per kilogram lost (S1254); a thin start's hunger is #3874. The day after a fast follows Johnstone 2002 (S1613) and Clayton 2016 (S1614); x27 read a rise of about a fifth live (#3670); the current code's replay of a fast reads percent. After a meal HUNGER does not return to zero: the replay of a can of bolognese bottoms out at , and M1's live ring matched its replay (#3890).

Thirst and salt

Vanilla

Thirst rises at a steady rate, slower asleep than awake, and a drink lowers it. At the top Thirsty moodle it drains your health fast.

This mod

Your body keeps a water pool with sodium and potassium. Water leaves through basic turnover and through sweat, and you sweat more with work and heat. Salt changes how much water you hold.

Thirst follows the water your body lacks, and water still in your stomach already eases it. In Managed mode thirst stays below vanilla's top level.

What can happen

  • On a salt-free diet your body loses sodium slowly and holds its blood sodium.

What no longer happens

  • In Managed mode thirst never reaches the top Thirsty moodle, so the game's thirst drain never runs.
The science

The thirst cap is the same Managed-mode cap as hunger's (#3123); live, THIRST stopped under the top level (#3103, #3011).

x281 to x284 read the sodium balance on a salt-free diet live over five days: a loss of about mmol a day, plasma sodium held, no grade change (#3688). The plasma reading is not drawn: the mod's replay misses it by more than the run's tolerance.

Monotony

Vanilla

Mood from food comes from each item's own boredom and unhappiness values, applied when you eat it, and made worse when the food is stale, frozen, burnt or rotten.

This mod

The mod remembers what you have been eating. The same food day after day lowers your mood, up to the first level of the Unhappy and Bored moodles. A staple such as bread, rice or pasta counts for less. The effect fades over the days after you vary your diet.

What can happen

  • Eating one food lowers your mood to the first Unhappy and Bored level, and varying it lifts your mood again.

What no longer happens

Nothing for this system.

The science

The live mood reading is #3683: the stat against the first-level thresholds of #2369, with boredom read as the mod's remembered target. The line is the mod's code replaying x343's own meals; x343's live ring misses that replay by more than its tolerance, so its points are not drawn.

The body

Vanilla

Your character has one weight number. A hidden calorie store fills when you eat and drains as you live. When it runs high you gain weight, and when it runs low you lose it.

This mod

Your body is fat, lean mass, and a small reserve of water and stored carbohydrate that a big day fills and a lean day spends first. Your resting burn, your activity, what you eat and how you train move them, and the lean side moves once a game day.

The mod writes the total into the game's own weight.

What can happen

  • A total fast kills when the server lets deficiencies kill, which is the default. On a fast with water the first killer is beriberi, a thiamine deficiency that fails the heart, long before your fat runs out.
  • Long starvation puts you at refeeding risk.

What no longer happens

  • An idle character no longer gains weight on a small daily diet. Compare the two lines on the body mass chart.
The science

Vanilla gains on the headline chart's diet because the idle day burns less than it eats and the calorie store passes the gain threshold (#0149, #0455). The mod's lines start from its no-trait creation body (#3872).

The lean partition under deficit and surplus is the Strength day's; x434 and x435 showed the deficit closes equal to the code's replay of their inputs (#3869, #3870). The weight bands read the fat axis scaled by lean (#3856, #3873); vanilla bands by weight (#0155).

On a total fast with water the mod's first lethal state is cardiac beriberi, and health is gone by day (the dial is #3105). The refeeding risk state is #3875 and #2987; the refeeding event itself has no reading and is not promised.

Exercise and energy

Vanilla

Calories burn at a rate set by what you are doing: asleep, idle, walking, running or sprinting, scaled by your weight. A timed action multiplies the idle rate, so every Fitness exercise costs the same.

This mod

Energy use follows the game's activity level, priced from the published tables of what activities cost. The mod bills each Fitness exercise at its own cost, so burpees cost far more than squats. Hard exercise leaves a short after-burn.

Muscle glycogen falls with hard work and a low-carbohydrate diet, and blood sugar falls with exhausting work or alcohol on an empty stomach.

What can happen

Nothing for this system.

What no longer happens

Nothing for this system.

The science

The mod's bill is MET × body mass per class, from the Compendium of Physical Activities; x371 read every Fitness bill live and each equals the code's table (#3730). Vanilla's rates are its per-second burn by activity (#0453 to #0461) and the Fitness and reading calorie modifiers (#2187).

The post-exercise dip in hunger is the satiety signal's exercise term; its proof run, x291, misses the replay at HEAD, so the line has no passing live proof.

Strength

Vanilla

Strength is experience. Exercise and heavy work grant experience, each level needs more of it, and protein in your stores multiplies the gain. When you stop training, rust takes experience away very slowly.

This mod

Your Strength level reads your body: your lean mass, how trained your nerves are, and an innate strength set at creation. Experience no longer moves the level. It counts as your training, in hard sets a week.

Muscle grows only when training, protein and enough food act together, and ever more slowly toward a natural cap. Stop training and it fades after a grace period toward your body's set point. Muscle you lost comes back fast. The mod writes the perk you see once a game day: it moves up at each whole level and down only once your body falls clearly below it.

What can happen

  • A trained, well-fed body with plenty of protein grows toward a natural cap that no one passes.

What no longer happens

  • Strength experience no longer levels you up. In vanilla a year or so of grinding takes you to level ; see the Strength chart.
  • Strength no longer stays with you when you stop training. Vanilla's rust is so slow it barely shows.
The science

XP is the training signal and the level follows the body (#3851, #3871). The natural cap is Kouri 1995's limit on the fat-free mass index (S1915), the lean mass over the height squared, read as a level (#3853).

Detraining and regain follow Blocquiaux 2020 (S0618), where under eight weeks of retraining restored the trained strength. Psilander 2019 (S0078) found no retraining advantage in muscle size, so the fast regain is the mod's reading of S0618. Their arms were never closed live, so those lines carry the code's badge only. Vanilla's rust is one XP a step once its timer passes (#2151, #2155); its protein factor is #2112, and one push-up set to exhaustion is #3844.

Body traits and character creation

Vanilla

Weak, Feeble, Stout and Strong set your starting Strength. Underweight, Very Underweight, Overweight and Obese set your starting weight, and they follow the scale from then on.

This mod

The muscle traits keep their vanilla costs and, but for Weak, their start levels: vanilla starts Weak at and the mod at . Each is built now from lean mass and an innate strength. Part of a muscle trait's head start is permanent, so an idle Strong survivor settles into the Stout range.

The weight traits become Overfat, Obese, Overly Lean and Gaunt, and they follow your body fat, scaled by your muscle. You reach Emaciated only in play. A fat trait starts you with more fat on the same lean. A thin trait starts you with less fat, hungry and with a slowed metabolism until you regain it.

The scenario Choosing your body charts each pick and how it drifts.

What can happen

  • A muscle start fades toward its set point when idle. An idle Strong survivor settles in the Stout band.

What no longer happens

  • Weight traits no longer follow the scale. A muscular, heavy survivor is no longer Overweight.
  • A thin or fat trait no longer changes only the scale. A thin start is hungry until it regains its fat.
The science

The creation bodies are #3872; a thin start's hunger is #3874. The half share of a muscle trait's lean difference that is permanent is #3855. The trait bands read the fat axis scaled by lean (#3856), read live in x436 (#3873).

Deficiency and excess

Vanilla

The game tracks no vitamins or minerals, so there is nothing to run short of beyond calories.

This mod

Each nutrient store steps down through Replete, Marginal, Depleted and Clinical, and rises to an excess level above its upper limit. Each step brings its own effects: fatigue, low mood, sickness, slower healing, heavier bleeding, faster wound infection, colds, body temperature and bruising.

A clinical vitamin A deficiency makes you Short Sighted. A long, well-stocked vitamin A and zinc intake grants Night Vision. The most severe states drain health until treated, when the server lets deficiencies kill, which is the default.

What can happen

  • Scurvy and the other deficiencies on a one-food diet.
  • After a long spell at clinical scurvy, untreated scurvy kills in about game days. The pace is a game choice.
  • Night Vision from a long, well-stocked vitamin A and zinc intake.
  • Short Sighted at a clinical vitamin A deficiency.
  • An iron overdose makes you sick.

What no longer happens

Nothing for this system.

The science

The grade crossings fall where the code puts them (#2978). The mod calibrates vitamin C's onset on Padayatty 2016's account of Hodges' controlled series, about days to scurvy (S0225), so the code agrees with it by construction; a self-experimenter took days (S0224). The other stores rest on half-lives or game choices.

The scurvy drain was read live in x161b (#3106). The time from clinical scurvy to death has no settled study value (S1168 is open), so the pace is a game choice.

Night Vision is #3093 and #3095, Short Sighted #3098, and the iron excess rung that drives food sickness #3084. A health drain from low blood sodium or an iron overdose is not promised.

Caffeine, alcohol and sleep

Vanilla

Coffee lowers fatigue by a set amount per drink. Beer and other alcohol raise drunkenness, which fades at a fixed rate: a beer's worth is gone within minutes at the default day length. Sleeping lowers fatigue, and there is no sleep debt. With the server's sleep options off, the game clears fatigue on its own.

This mod

Your body absorbs and clears caffeine over hours, and a daily coffee drinker builds tolerance. Alcohol raises blood alcohol, which drives the game's drunkenness, and heavy drinking leaves a hangover once the alcohol has cleared.

When the server turns sleep on, short nights build a sleep debt that raises your hunger the next day, and long nights pay it back.

What can happen

  • Coffee works less well when you drink it every day.
  • Heavy drinking leaves a hangover once the alcohol has cleared.
  • A short night raises the next day's hunger.

What no longer happens

Nothing for this system.

The science

The caffeine offset follows caffeine and its tolerance live (#3077). x301 and x302 read the sleep debt and its hunger factor live (#3677, #3638), after Brondel 2010's one short night (S1566). The hangover's start was read live, where the code puts it, in the regrade of x447 (#3891). Caffeine withdrawal has no live reading yet, so the guide does not promise it.

What you see on screen

Vanilla

The Hungry and Thirsty moodles, and your weight in the character screen.

This mod

A panel on the ; key, which you can rebind. What it shows depends on the server's visibility setting: Symptoms, Bands or Numbers. A survivor with the Nutritionist trait always sees Numbers. A Nutrition tab in the character window shows the same rows, and a band under each food's tooltip lists its values.

The mod adds its own moodles: Energy (Peckish to Starving), Hydration (Slightly dry to Severely dehydrated), Deficiency (Marginal to Clinical), Excess (Elevated to Excessive), Stimulant (Alert to Overdosed), Sleep (Short on sleep to Collapsing) and Overfull (Pleasantly overfull to Painfully stuffed). Vanilla's hunger and thirst moodles still show.

What can happen

  • Overeating raises the Overfull moodle, and it never blocks an eat.

What no longer happens

  • In Managed mode hunger and thirst never reach the top Hungry and Thirsty moodles.
The science

The Overfull levels map the stomach's discomfort scale to four steps (#3623); x401 read the client's levels live (#3778). The Managed cap is #3123.

Server settings

The options you will meet

Your server's operator sets these on the sandbox page Nutrition Revamp. The charts on this page use the defaults and a male character. The operator's guide lists every option, the Strength tuning included: the operator README on GitHub.

Mode

NR.Mode

Managed, the default, has the mod write your hunger, thirst and fatigue every game minute from its model. Overlay leaves vanilla's hunger, thirst and fatigue alone and adds only the mod's effects.

Deficiencies can kill

NR.DeficienciesCanKill

On by default. The most severe states drain health until treated. Off, they never drain health.

What the panel shows

NR.VisibilityMode

Symptoms shows a word for each system. Bands, the default, adds your body-fat band, your weight, your Strength and a grade for each nutrient. Numbers shows every store.

Onset speed

NR.OnsetSpeed

How fast the slow deficiencies develop and recover. The default is real time. Vitamin C and the other fast vitamins, water, caffeine, alcohol, glycogen and sleep are never sped up.

Severity and excess

NR.Severity, NR.ExcessEffectsOn

Severity scales every penalty a deficiency or an excess brings. The excess option turns the effects of eating too much of a nutrient on or off.

Sex-specific values

NR.SexSpecific

Off by default, so every character uses the male values. On, a female character uses her own requirements and body-fat bands.

Sleep

SleepAllowed, SleepNeeded

The mod's sleep debt needs both of the server's own sleep options on. With either off, the game resets fatigue itself and you never build sleep debt.

Other mods

Compatibility

  • Nutrition Makes Sense cannot run beside this mod. Both write hunger and body weight. The server warns at boot when you load both.
  • MoodleFramework is optional. When you have it, the mod's moodles use it. Without it the mod draws its own icon column.
  • BeyondTen works with it. Above the vanilla top level, the mod writes BeyondTen's Strength mastery from your body.
  • Mods that tweak hunger or thirst rates have no effect in Managed mode, because the mod writes those stats itself.
  • Food mods work. Their foods get values estimated from their type and calories, and their authors can declare a food's nutrients.
  • Mods that replace the hunger, energy or weight model cannot run beside it.

The full table is COMPATIBILITY.md on GitHub.

Know before you play

Known limitations

  • Hunger never reaches zero after a meal.
  • The game does not tell the server when you run or sprint, so the mod bills running as walking.
  • Time offline is not simulated. No sleep debt builds, a repeated food does not fade, and your body and Strength stay as you left them.
  • The game itself still refuses an eat at the top level of its own fullness moodle.
  • A trait the mod gives or takes reaches you at once, and other players see it after they reconnect.
  • Vitamin D has no sunlight term, so its effects ship turned off.
  • The mod computes movement speed and aiming effects, but the game resets them, so they do nothing.
  • Your panel and moodles update with the next game minute.
  • The mod writes the Strength perk once a game day, so it can read a little above your body's true level. The panel shows the true level.
