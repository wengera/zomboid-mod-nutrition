A survivor's field guide

Nutrition Revamp

Your survivor has a body now. What you eat fills a stomach, feeds a store of every vitamin and mineral, and moves fat and muscle. Hunger, thirst, weight and Strength all follow from it.

This guide is about what the mod changes. Each topic opens with what changes from vanilla, the biggest change first, and a topic that plays about as vanilla does says so in a line. Each chart shows vanilla in one colour and the mod in another, and every number on the page comes from a measured run, the mod's own code, the game's own code, or a published study. Tap a source chip under a chart to see where its numbers came from.

At a glance

SystemVanillaNutrition RevampWhat it means for you
Hunger Each food carries a hunger value. Eating subtracts it, and hunger climbs at a steady rate between meals. Hunger comes from your body: how full your stomach is, the energy you ate recently, and how much fat you have lost. The game's own Hungry moodles show it, paced close to vanilla's, with Peckish back a little sooner. A satisfying meal empties the hunger bar and Peckish comes back a few hours later. A food's hunger value no longer counts: its weight, a little of its bulk and its calories do.
Thirst Thirst rises at a steady rate and a drink takes it down. Your body keeps a pool of water and salt. You lose water through sweat and turnover, a little more for a more muscular body, and faster when you work or it is hot. Hard work in the heat makes you thirsty sooner, water still in your stomach already helps, and the thirst traits change how fast you dry out.
Weight and the body One weight number, moved by a hidden calorie store. A body of fat and lean mass. Eating, resting and training move each one. A diet costs you mostly fat. Starving costs you muscle too.
Strength Experience raises the level, and slow rust takes it back. The level reads your muscle. Training, protein and enough food grow it, and it fades when you stop. Push-ups build Strength fastest when you eat enough protein and food.
Weight traits Follow the scale. Follow your body fat, scaled by your muscle. A heavily muscled survivor is no longer marked Overweight.
Food mood Each food lifts or sinks your mood by its own values, the same whatever else you eat. Plain fat and sugar wear you down when they are most of what you eat, your Cooking level shows in your dishes, hunger eases a plain meal, and variety counts by taste as well as by food. Butter and sugar are cheap calories, not a diet. Cook, and mix your meals.
Nutrients Calories, carbohydrate, fat and protein. Vitamins, minerals, fibre, essential fats, salt and potassium, most with a store and a daily need, and caffeine and alcohol beside them. Living on one food catches up with you as a deficiency.
Foods Values set by hand, some of them far off. Every vanilla food re-based on published food-composition data, with a full nutrient table. Tinned tuna is a protein food, and a burger's bun has carbohydrate in it.
Death by hunger, thirst or deficiency Hunger or thirst at their top moodle drain your health until you die. Starving, going without water and the severe deficiencies damage your health. Each takes it down in a straight line from the day it starts, and health reaches zero only when whatever the science says comes first would kill a real body. Two server options set how fast that comes and how several dangers add up. Along the way you feel them on the game's own Exertion, Pain, Stress and Unhappy moodles. A day without food costs you health, not your life. Weeks without food, days without water or months of untreated scurvy still kill. A real meal stops starvation's damage, and drinking enough water stops dehydration's, but the health they took comes back only as your body recovers: after a long fast about as slowly as it was lost, and after thirst as you drink. Living on past vanilla's death point, you are worn out and aching, and in time stressed and miserable.

Skipping meals and starving

You miss a whole day of meals and then eat again. Later the food runs out altogether, and you have only water.

What changes from vanilla

  1. Starving lasts weeks, not a day

    VanillaHunger reaches its top level about hours after your last meal, and there it drains your health until you die, about a day after Starving shows.

    This modStarving shows once your stomach has been empty for more than a day, about when vanilla's would. From then on your health falls in a straight line, keeping step with your body's wasting, and runs out only when your body has wasted to a real body's limit. You live on far longer, weak and wasting. A heavier body has further to waste, so its line runs longer and falls no differently.

  2. On water alone your vitamins give out first

    VanillaThe game tracks no vitamins, so a fast on water ends only in hunger's death.

    This modWith plenty of water (four litres a day) and no food your body's vitamins run out too, their damage compounds with starving's, and the beriberi that follows kills an average survivor after about eleven weeks, before the body has wasted to starvation's limit. A heavy survivor lasts no longer, dying of the same beriberi on the same day, while a very thin one starves to death first, in about six weeks.

  3. You feel the danger

    VanillaStarving shows on the Hungry moodle alone.

    This modExertion and an aching stomach from the start, then Stress and Unhappy, on the game's own moodles and deeper the nearer you are to death (more below).

  4. Health comes back slowly, and a snack does not end it

    VanillaEating takes hunger back under its top level, the drain stops, and your health heals as usual.

    This modA real meal stops the damage, but the health it took comes back only as your body recovers: at the default pace, as you regain the weight. After days on almost nothing a snack stops it only for a few hours, and a body starved thinner than a BMI of thirteen goes on starving until it puts weight back on. After a long spell of starving, the mod marks you at refeeding risk.

  5. Your server sets the pace

    VanillaOne fixed drain.

    This modYour server can make starving and thirst kill faster, or as fast as vanilla, and can choose how several dangers at once add up (see the options).

  6. Your appetite remembers

    VanillaHunger climbs at its steady rate whatever your body holds, so a missed day changes nothing after it.

    This modThe day after a missed day you eat about percent more than usual, and your hunger also grows as you lose fat.

Starving shows on the game's own Starving moodle. A real body's appetite fades after the first days of a fast, but the moodle stays on screen so the danger stays in sight. Without water you dry out far sooner, in under a week: see Thirst and salt.

You feel a danger as well as lose health to it, on the game's own moodles. Exertion shows from the moment a danger starts, so you tire sooner, and it deepens a level at , and per cent of the way to the danger's death, until near the end you can barely run or fight. A low ache shows on the Pain moodle while the danger still worsens: in your stomach while you starve, in your head while you dry out, and in your legs with scurvy or beriberi. Two dangers that hurt in different places add up. Unhappy shows from per cent of the way and Stress from , and both deepen from per cent. When a danger eases, the ache fades within a couple of real minutes, while the exhaustion, the stress and the unhappiness ease only as your body recovers: as the danger's mood floors fall, the unhappiness drops with them and the stress fades over a few game hours. With sleep on, deep exhaustion also makes you tire faster, as it does in vanilla.

Living on one food

You find a case of tinned tuna and eat nothing else, a tin at each of your four meals, for weeks.

What changes from vanilla

  1. Scurvy can kill you

    VanillaThe game tracks no vitamins and does not remember what you ate, so weeks of tuna do you no harm.

    This modTuna carries plenty of protein and no vitamin C. Your vitamin C store runs down through Marginal and Depleted to Clinical, which is scurvy. From then on scurvy wears your health down in a straight line over the following months, and left untreated it kills you.

  2. An orange a day is the cure

    VanillaThere is nothing to cure.

    This modAn orange a day lifts the store out of Clinical within days and ends the scurvy damage. The health scurvy took comes back over about as long as it was Clinical.

  3. Scurvy hurts

    VanillaNothing to feel.

    This modScurvy also makes your legs ache, wears you out and, as it goes on, makes you stressed and unhappy, on the game's own moodles.

  4. One food wears on your mood

    VanillaEach food lifts or sinks your mood by its own values alone.

    This modEating the same food day after day also lowers your mood, as far as the first Unhappy and Bored level, and further if that food is plain fat or sugar (see Food mood).

A year at the gym

You train hard all year, a few hard sets every day, then stop for a year and start again.

What changes from vanilla

  1. Your level reads your muscle

    VanillaEach set grants Strength experience, and protein in your stores multiplies it. At normal protein a year of training ends at level . Only the game's protein bonus reaches level , on day .

    This modYour level reads your muscle. Training builds it fastest with plenty of protein and enough food, and ever more slowly toward a natural cap.

  2. Gains fade when you stop

    VanillaRead from the game's code: stop training and rust takes experience back so slowly that the level holds through a year off.

    This modStop and your muscle fades back toward your body's set point. Train again and it returns faster than it first came.

  3. Food decides growth

    VanillaProtein in your stores multiplies the experience each set grants.

    This modWith too little protein muscle builds less, and on too little food it slips back. Keep training on too little food long enough and, once the fat is gone, your body spends its muscle until it starves.

Coffee, beer and no sleep

You live on coffee, drink a few beers one evening, and cut a night short.

What changes from vanilla

  1. Drunkenness lasts hours

    VanillaA beer raises drunkenness by an amount fixed by the drink, a little more when you are hungry, and at the default day length it clears within minutes, as a live session measured.

    This modBeer raises your blood alcohol, which sets how drunk you are and clears over hours. By default each beer adds about vanilla's drunkenness.

  2. A hangover

    VanillaNone.

    This modHeavy drinking leaves a hangover once the alcohol has gone. By default it is mild.

  3. Coffee wears off, and works less every day

    VanillaA cup of coffee takes a fixed amount off your fatigue, the same on the first day as the hundredth, and it lasts only when the server has sleep on.

    This modYour body absorbs caffeine and clears it over hours, and a daily coffee drinker builds tolerance, so the same cups do less. By default a single cup's relief is about two and a half times vanilla's.

  4. A short night makes you hungry

    VanillaSleep has no debt, so a short night leaves the next day's hunger alone.

    This modWhen the server turns sleep on, a short night builds sleep debt, and the debt makes you hungrier.

  5. Your server picks the strength

    VanillaThe strengths are the game's own.

    This modThe timing follows the studies. The strength stays near vanilla's by default, and the server's Realistic setting restores the full strengths the studies give.

Nutrients and foods

What changes from vanilla

  1. Every food carries a full nutrient table

    VanillaEvery food carries calories, carbohydrate, fat and protein, and the game counts nothing else.

    This modEach food also carries the vitamins, the minerals, fibre, essential fats, sodium, potassium, caffeine and alcohol. Your body keeps a store of each nutrient. It fills from food, empties at its own pace, and shows on the panel as a grade: Replete, Marginal, Depleted or Clinical. Logging off and your record

About the same as vanilla Nothing about your survivor changes while you are logged off, as in vanilla: the mod's stomach, stores, sleep debt and body wait exactly as you left them. The server also keeps a backup copy on your character, so a lost file or a crash rollback brings your record back without counting a meal twice.

The science

Vanilla's disconnect takes the player out of the server's maps (#2403), and the server writes a connected player's character, its modData with it, to the player store on its own cadence and again at the disconnect (#3970).

At the first touch of a returning player the mod moves every world-age stamp its record keeps past the offline gap, so no step integrates the absence (#3955, #3961), and the record day closes after 24 online game hours (#3958). A slow server or a fast-forwarded night while you stay online is play, integrated in steps of at most an hour (#3956). Live, x463a logged a survivor off for 73.85 game hours with the world running and found every compared value equal and every stamp moved by the gap (#3983), the written hunger and thirst unchanged (#3984).

The backup is the record's own text on the character, checked when it is read (#3967). The store restores it when its files are missing or unreadable, or when its record is ahead of the world after a hard kill (#3968); live, x463b killed the server after an eat and the restored record held no eat, the food back in the inventory (#3985), the character's saved blob holding the copy (#3986). No record is ever pruned (#3965).

Server settings

The options you will meet

Your server's operator sets these on the sandbox page Nutrition Revamp. The charts on this page use the defaults and a male character. The operator's guide lists every option, the Strength tuning included: the operator README on GitHub.

Mode

NR.Mode

Managed, the default, has the mod write your hunger, thirst and fatigue every game minute from its model. Overlay leaves vanilla's hunger, thirst and fatigue alone and adds only the mod's effects. In Overlay starving and thirst bring none of the mod's exhaustion, ache, stress or unhappiness either, while the deficiencies' still do.

Stimulant strength

NR.StimulantRealism

Game, the default, sizes coffee's relief, a beer's drunkenness and the hangover near vanilla's. Realistic gives the full strengths the studies report. The timing is the same in both.

What the panel shows

NR.VisibilityMode

Symptoms shows a word for each system. Bands, the default, adds your body-fat band, your weight, your Strength and a grade for each nutrient. Numbers shows every store.

Onset speed

NR.OnsetSpeed

How fast the slow deficiencies develop and recover. The default is real time. Vitamin C and the other fast vitamins, water, caffeine, alcohol, glycogen and sleep are never sped up.

Severity and excess

NR.Severity, NR.ExcessEffectsOn

Severity scales every penalty a deficiency or an excess brings, and how hard a deadly danger's exhaustion, ache, stress and unhappiness hit, though not how fast your health falls. At half Severity one danger's ache stays under the Pain moodle, its exhaustion never passes the first Exertion level, and stress and unhappiness show only from about three quarters of the way to death, at their first level. The excess option turns the effects of eating too much of a nutrient on or off.

How fast starving and thirst kill

NR.DeathPace

Every pace takes health down in a straight line. Realistic, the default, runs the line to the real death points: starving lasts until your body has wasted to a real body's limit, and thirst until you have lost as much water as kills a real body. Shortened ends starving after two weeks and thirst after two days. Vanilla runs at vanilla's own steady rate, so unbroken starving kills about a day after Starving shows. Change the pace in the middle of a fast and health above the new line falls at least at vanilla's rate until it reaches it; health below a slower new line stays where it is while Starving or Parched shows. At every pace a danger that eases keeps its cap on your health, which rises back only as your body recovers: about as long as the decline took, with the weight a starving body regains, or as a dehydrated body drinks. Unlike vanilla, a danger that stops and starts again picks its line up where it left off. The vitamin deficiencies keep their own timing at every pace.

How several dangers add up

NR.HealthCombine

Multiply, the default, makes several dangers at once wear you down faster, but you die only when one of them reaches its own end. Worst only counts just the worst danger. Add adds up the damage of each, as vanilla does with hunger and thirst, so several at once can kill you before any one of them would.

Together the two options span vanilla to the science. Vanilla with Add plays unbroken starving and thirst like the unmodded game, though a danger that pauses picks up where it left off and lost health comes back only as the body recovers, where vanilla heals freely; its onsets are the mod's, Starving within half an hour of vanilla's and Parched about four hours later; the deficiencies still run on their own timing. Shortened with Multiply is a quicker game of the same shape. Realistic with Worst only follows the science most strictly. Realistic with Multiply is the default. A change takes effect within a game minute.

Sex-specific values

NR.SexSpecific

Off by default, so every character uses the male values. On, a female character uses her own requirements and body-fat bands.

Sleep

SleepAllowed, SleepNeeded

The mod's sleep debt needs both of the server's own sleep options on. With either off, the game resets fatigue itself and you never build sleep debt.

With both on, each survivor also keeps a body clock (see Caffeine, alcohol and sleep); with either off there is no daily rhythm. A change takes effect within a game minute.

Other mods

Compatibility

  • Nutrition Makes Sense cannot run beside this mod. Both write hunger and body weight. The server warns at boot when you load both.
  • MoodleFramework is optional. When you have it, the mod's moodles use it. Without it the mod draws its own icon column.
  • BeyondTen works with it. Above the vanilla top level, the mod writes BeyondTen's Strength mastery from your body.
  • Mods that tweak hunger or thirst rates have no effect in Managed mode, because the mod writes those stats itself.
  • Food mods work. Their foods get values estimated from their type and calories, and their authors can declare a food's nutrients.
  • Mods that replace the hunger, energy or weight model cannot run beside it.

Know before you play

Known limitations

  • The game does not tell the server when you run or sprint, so the mod bills running as walking.
  • Another mod that syncs your character's data from your game wipes the backup copy until the mod's next save, at most a real minute later while you are online, so a restore in that window cannot use it.
  • If your record's files are ahead of the world after a crash and your character holds no valid copy, the files are kept and moved back onto the world's clock, so food you ate after the world's last save stays counted.
  • A server stall while you stay online is caught up in hour-long steps, a few at a time. If you log off before the catch-up ends, the rest is frozen with your absence.
  • The food you carry keeps spoiling while you are logged off, and vanilla's exercise regularity counts the world's days: both are the game's, not the mod's.
  • A drink arrives sip by sip, so a big drink right after a full meal can still reach the top Overfull level for a few minutes.
  • At the top Overfull level the server refuses an eat only in the default Managed mode. In Overlay mode the game's own fullness decides, as in vanilla.
  • A drink with food value, such as milk or juice from a bottle or carton, is refused at the top Overfull level only by the menu. One queued past the menu still goes down.
  • The message that you cannot eat more arrives in the server's language, not your own.
  • With QualityCooking loaded after this mod, a refused dish can still give its cooking buff, though nothing is eaten.
  • Another mod that makes you eat by calling the game directly, not through the eat action, is not refused at the top Overfull level.
  • Records are kept by the server's name: a world wiped and restarted under the same name keeps every survivor's old record until an admin deletes the mod's folder.
  • Well Fed lasts a fixed number of game hours, matched to vanilla's at the default day length. Vanilla's changes with the server's day length, lasting more game hours on shorter days.
  • A trait the mod gives or takes reaches you at once, and other players see it after they reconnect.
  • Vitamin D has no sunlight term, so its effects ship turned off.
  • The mod computes movement speed and aiming effects, but the game resets them, so they do nothing.
  • Your panel and moodles update with the next game minute.
  • The mod writes the Strength perk once a game day, so it can read a little above your body's true level. The panel shows the true level.
  • Below a tenth of your health vanilla wakes a sleeper on every tick, so you cannot sleep there, and every line spends its last tenth there. On the default fast with water, health is under a tenth from day to the death on day . Starving alone at Realistic pace is there from day to day , and at Shortened pace from day to day . Without water it is there from hour to the death at hour . A deficiency alone spends its last tenth there: beriberi's last three and a half days and pellagra's last eighteen.
  • Health a danger took comes back only as your body recovers, so climbing back to full after a long fast takes about as long as the decline did.
  • After a snack in a long fast the starvation cap holds until the day's weight update books your true weight, so it can sit above your true line for up to a day and your health then falls to it at vanilla's rate.
  • While a danger's cap recovers, the panel says Recovering from it, and Well Fed can show though the cap still holds your health and food does not heal it.
  • No live game has shown a cap recovering with the body yet: one test lifted a starving cap it had set by hand with a snack's pool written to the record and watched health heal back up to it, and the rest is checked against the mod's own code only.
  • A danger's ache is flat and low while the danger worsens, a stand-in until research gives it a shape, such as hunger pangs that fade after the first days and deeper aches later. Where it aches is the mod's choice.
  • A painkiller should hold a danger's ache down while it works, as the game's code holds down any pain, but no test has shown it on a server yet.
  • Several deadly dangers at once can ache at the third Pain level: a headache from thirst or low sodium, a stomach ache and both legs' aches from scurvy and wet beriberi together. A server's Injury Severity option scales every ache, the mod's included.
  • A danger's ache takes about a minute of real time to show, and when pain jumps the server's Pain moodle can trail it by up to about twelve seconds.
  • On the longest day lengths the ache and the exhaustion, its deepest level first, can flicker a level between the mod's once-a-minute settings.
  • With Add, health can run out before any one danger would kill you on its own. That is the option's point, but it can surprise.
  • Realistic and Shortened run in game time, so they last the same game days at any day length. Only Vanilla pace follows your server's day length, as vanilla's own drain does.
  • A sudden jump in a danger's progress lands in one game minute, so a very thin body's first day's close of starving or the fifth day's close of a fast without water drops up to most of a point at once, and an admin's write of a deficit takes its whole drop in that one minute. The chart's notes give each drop.
