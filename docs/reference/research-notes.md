### Satiety and hunger

- **Protein leverage on a low-protein diet.** At most about +12 % of daily energy, ramping over 1-2 days, through between-meal hunger, asymmetric (about 4.5 kJ per kJ of protein shortfall against 1 per kJ of excess). It would read a rolling 1-4 day protein share and act below about 15 %. Low certainty with two nulls at 5 % (S1414 open), so it could ship only as a labelled game choice. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/22-protein.md@program-record-v19` L136-140.
- **Liquid sugar is under-compensated.** Over days drinks are compensated at about −17 % to 45 %, solids about 100 %. The option discounts carbohydrate-only drinks (soda, juice) in the meal feed at about 0.3-0.5, leaving Callahan's mixed liquid meal, a fitted oracle, untouched. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/23-carbohydrate-and-fat.md@program-record-v19` L183-195.
- **Diet-specific damping of the deficit drive.** Ketosis (under about 50 g carbohydrate a day for 2-3 days) damps it, size open (S1455). The high-protein form (Leidy 2007, direction only) is withdrawn: two year-scale head-to-head trials are null (S1638, Leidy 2015; Das 2007, CALERIE), and `NR_Kernel_Deficit.lua` states no coupling. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/23-carbohydrate-and-fat.md@program-record-v19` L196-203; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/28-dieting-consensus.md@program-record-v19` L143; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-satiety-revisit-memo.md@program-record-v19` L307.
- <a id="aerated"></a>**Aerated food and foams.** A doubled volume at equal mass gives about −12 % at the next meal (S1483); the shipped fill counts only 0.05 of a solid's plate volume beyond its weight (`satiety.BETA`, Plan 15). For whipped or aerated liquids a separate β of about 0.7 (0.5-1.0; Rolls 2000 over Rolls 1998, S1483 over S1233) applies; for chewed solids any β above 0.15 is absurd (22 g of popcorn would read as 120 g). The mod maps no foam, and carbonation's gas is outside the evidence. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/24-water-volume-and-form.md@program-record-v19` L158-162; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-16-game-feel/volume-report.md@program-record-v19` § 3.
- **Illness cuts appetite; injury runs the deficit drive the wrong way.** Intake falls during fever or diarrhoea (high certainty, S1540; inpatients under-eat, S1541); the size is open (S1544, S1548; expenditure per °C of fever S1545). FOOD_SICKNESS or SICKNESS would lower hunger while raised and leave nothing after; the memo's option (b) was a labelled cut on SICKNESS. Ruling 11e-0 kept illness neutral, and the writer's limitation line does not name illness. If the mod ever bills injury or burn expenditure (130-140 % of REE), the deficit drive will raise hunger where clinical appetite falls. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/26-player-states.md@program-record-v19` L199-217; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-satiety-revisit-memo.md@program-record-v19` L189-203.
- **Acute cold and heat.** Cold raises intake (pooled g +0.44, about +10-13 % on a cold day), heat lowers it (g −0.39), both only while exposed; intake moves, VAS ratings mostly do not. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/26-player-states.md@program-record-v19` L209-213; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/25-exercise.md@program-record-v19` L262-266.
- **Smaller exercise and alcohol terms.** Fitness could scale the exercise lag's gain down (S1523, S1527); the lag could be sub-proportional to dose or carry a ceiling near the 90-125 kcal/d rise measured by doubly labelled water (the mod ships gain 0.7, τ 16 d); alcohol's aperitif effect is about +343 kJ at the next meal. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/25-exercise.md@program-record-v19` L267, L331; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/26-player-states.md@program-record-v19` L221-223.
- **Body size and the meal capacity.** Fat-free mass predicts meal size (R² 0.46, S1629), but the capacity is one constant. At 10 % below usual weight post-meal fullness falls 17.8 mm of 150 (Kissileff, S2177), so depletion could blunt the satiety read (`x·(1 − b·fatDep)`) as well as raise the drive. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/27-food-and-behaviour.md@program-record-v19` L159-160; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-14-strength-redesign/hunger-fat-report.md@program-record-v19` L98.
- **The fat reference re-bases only upward.** `NR_Server_Metabolism.lua` ratchets `fmRef` up and never down. Candidates: a slow downward re-base on a time constant of a year or more (S2197 open), and a fat-free-mass depletion term so hyperphagia lasts until lean is restored (Dulloo); the lean drive adds to the fat drive (S1616: r −0.6 fat, −0.5 lean). `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-14-strength-redesign/hunger-fat-report.md@program-record-v19` L99-102; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/27-food-and-behaviour.md@program-record-v19` L157-158.
- **Learned aversion after food poisoning.** Unhappiness rising when the food type that last caused FOOD_SICKNESS is eaten again; direction supported, size not (S1606 open). `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-09-satiety-research-2/27-food-and-behaviour.md@program-record-v19` L168.
- **Gut-satiety leftovers.** Carbohydrate's late ghrelin rebound (+37 % at 3-6 h, S1813), which a first-order stage cannot make; distal spill (ileal nutrient 2.4-4.2 times as potent per kcal, so large meals would sate more per kcal); an F × G interaction in place of the probabilistic OR. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-12-gut-satiety/11-hormone-time-courses.md@program-record-v19` L236-239; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-12-gut-satiety/12-transit-absorption.md@program-record-v19` L202-209.
- **The liquid-lane hedge.** A plate-kcal slowing, `SOLID_PER_KCAL` 0.02 min/kcal (a 26 min half-time after 667 kcal), would stand for water mixing into a large meal (Moore 1981, S1272). No row sizes it, the evidence favours 0, and the split is the open row S2277. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-16-game-feel/liquid-emptying-report.md@program-record-v19` §§ 4, 7.

### Fluids and sodium

- <a id="salt"></a>**Salt carries no sodium (C-7).** `Base.Salt` is `spice_only` with null nutrients in `data/food-nutrients.json`, and `Food.spices` (#4119, #2652) is never read, so salting a dish adds nothing. The candidate reads `Food.spices` at the eat at a per-use dose (#0381, open) and adds a hypernatraemia rung for eating salt straight; 1 g NaCl is 17.1 mmol Na (S1691, derived). Ruling 5b-1 deferred it. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/05-sodium-drain.md@program-record-v19` L357-358; `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/06-renal-sodium.md@program-record-v19` L204-210.
- <a id="sweat-sodium"></a>**Sweat-sodium follow-ons (C-15).** Heat acclimatisation without a deficit needs a stored exposure EMA over days to weeks, worth −15 mmol/L or 17-30 %; with no stored state the deficit-driven response is detectable 6.3 h into a deficit against about 12 h (S1773) and a 1-4 day gland lag (S1748). A within-person sweat-rate term would raise concentration with rate; sweat-side recovery is open (S1771). `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/08-sweat-sodium.md@program-record-v19` L294-296, L362-372.
- **Bill the engine's target, not its lagged rate (option A).** Non-Fitness activities still under-bill their first τ_rise of about 0.56 game h at DayLength 1 (about 33 game minutes). Grade it offline on rings that carry the raw rate before shipping. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/09-post-exercise-tail.md@program-record-v19` L152-160, L197-201.
- **A volume-defence signal ladder** (a game choice; no effect ships): under 8 % ECF loss silent; 8-15 % AVP, mild exertional tachycardia, weak thirst; over 15 % orthostatic events and falling plasma sodium, symptoms below 130 mmol/L. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/07-thirst-volume.md@program-record-v19` L459-474.

### Effects and the symptom plan

- **The endurance drain coefficient `D_mod` is unbuilt.** It would scale the drain by empty glycogen (up to ×1.35, the bonk), dehydration (a kink at 2 % and a steeper slope from 4 %), a monotone heat ladder 1.00/1.10/1.25/1.50/2.00 in place of vanilla's level-4-only `getHyperthermiaMod`, iron, hours awake and caffeine. Only the recovery factor `R_mod` ships, and only asleep (`NR_Kernel_Hybrid.lua`). Its seat question is X35 plus Rule 6. `docs/superpowers/research/wave2-science-endurance-fitness.md@program-record-v19` § 7.2-7.4, L354-430.
- **Meal glycaemic index has no effect on sleep.** A high-GI meal 2-6 game hours before bed shortens sleep-onset latency by 8.5 min (Afaghi 2007); a low-GI evening meal raises deep sleep, recovery `r` 1.10 (Gaylor 2025, halved). S0786-S0790 are settled and cited nowhere; the seat exists (the `eff.SOL_*` dials). `docs/superpowers/research/wave2-science-fatigue-sleep.md@program-record-v19` L231-232, L368.
- **EPA and DHA are untracked, so S0919 ships nothing.** Omega-3 on depression by EPA share (Liao 2019, EPA-pure SMD −0.50, about +11 unhappiness at clinical depletion); FDC carries EPA (629) and DHA (621) per 100 g. The decision is stated only in mod comments (`NR_Server_Effects.lua`, `NR_Data_Records.lua`). `docs/superpowers/research/wave2-science-cognition-mood-perception.md@program-record-v19` L269; `docs/superpowers/research/food-data-pipeline.md@program-record-v19` L163-164.
- **Zinc shortening a cold.** Scale `setColdStrength` down and `setTimeToSneezeOrCough` up at replete zinc, about 2 days shorter (Hunter 2021, very low certainty). Low priority. `docs/superpowers/research/wave2-science-healing-immunity-thermal.md@program-record-v19` L221.
- **Partial fasting ends in Wernicke-Korsakoff.** Fasting with some intake plus oral thiamine stretches survival to 130-324 days and ends in Wernicke-Korsakoff rather than early death (S2244); the mod models no Wernicke state. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-16-game-feel/starvation-report.md@program-record-v19` § 1.

## Research results the pages do not carry

- **Why the deficit arm has no fat ceiling.** Alpert's 69 kcal/kg/d is the Minnesota men's own fat flux at a moderate deficit, not a maximum: Keys 1950 Table 152 gives 72 over weeks 0-12 and 63 over weeks 12-24. The Ranger course drew about 111 kcal per kg of initial fat a day (S1910), and measured fasts put protein at 13-18 % of energy, which Forbes reproduces and the ceiling (about 50 %) cannot. Re-basing the overflow at dry protein's density still leaves about 50 % of energy from protein; a sparing term over time has support only in the obese. [body-effects](../areas/body-effects.md) states the rule without this reason. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-18-fasting/partition-report.md@program-record-v19` §§ 1, 4, 8.
- **Forbes runs high at the lean end.** It overshoots the very lean protein share by about 1.3-1.8 times early in a Gaunt fast, and Elia's figure has too few lean points to fit a correction. Its share also rises as fat falls (the default body 14 → 22 → 27 % over days 1-60), against Elia's and Henry's "little change over a month" in lean subjects; the rise is mild before day 30. Pinned in `testing/tests/kernel/test_fasting_partition.py`. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-18-fasting/partition-report.md@program-record-v19` § 9.
- **The deficit drive reads loss from the person's own reference, not the fat level.** Constitutionally thin people show normal or low appetite, so a stable 10 % fat character should feel nothing; this is the reasoning behind the built `fatDep`. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-14-strength-redesign/hunger-fat-report.md@program-record-v19` L46-66, L104.
- **Coupled to metabolic water.** The fluids model omits metabolic water, so the 2000 g basal is the net loss. If metabolic water from oxidation is ever added, the basal goes back to the gross figure of about 2200 g, or the water is counted twice; the per-gram yields are the open row S2228. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-16-game-feel/water-report.md@program-record-v19` § 4.
- **Measured sodium kinetics behind the dials.** The renal τ is 33.8 h at ages 30-59 (25.4 h under 30, 44.6 h over 60). Free-water clearance is 672 g/h, about twice the old 320. Isotonic retention fails at high intakes, where sodium is stored without water. The dials carry the values but not the age bands or the failure regime. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/06-renal-sodium.md@program-record-v19` L105-117, L161-175, L271-327.
- **Karl 2021, from the primary paper.** TDEE is by doubly labelled water (n 9); PAEE is undefined in the paper, and arithmetic suggests it covers all activity above rest and food thermogenesis. The balance (%) is intake/TDEE − 1. DEF ate the same as REST (10.5 against 10.6 MJ/d). The ratings are 8 VAS points a day, not every minute. Johnstone 2002 reports neither body mass nor maintenance intake. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-10-live-study-replays/03-karl-2021-facts.md@program-record-v19` L29-210.
- **Karl's surplus arm in detail.** A vector read of Suppl. Fig. 1B gives BAL −57 % overall and −46 / −61 / −65 % by day; BAL's post-meal troughs sit about 0.05 on a 0-1 scale, and its pre-breakfast hunger on days 2-3 is −44 to −48 %. The regression intercept says about half of BAL's −55 % comes from large meals at high turnover, not the surplus (Hägele 2019 agrees, −27 %). Overfeeding suppression clears within about a day of the surplus ending. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-12-gut-satiety/13-surplus-repeated-meals.md@program-record-v19` L63-125, L311-331.
- **The hedonics alliesthesia rule (R3) rests on the weaker side.** Repletion lowering pleasantness is settled (Cabanac and Duclaux 1970); hunger raising it is disputed by Drewnowski 1983's fasting null; no row gives a size, and the only anchor is Rolls 1981's −0.27 of a 4-point scale for the non-specific drop. Ruling T4-2 put this in the dial's `src` only. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-21-hedonics/hedonics-report.md@program-record-v19` § 3.
- **The science's bounds on monotony and plain food.** Repetition brings a food to neutral, not to aversive (Rolls 1981), so any monotony sadness past neutral is vanilla's pacing with no science behind it. Food neophobia gives no support for a novelty bonus (Pliner 1992). Palatability belongs to a pairing of components (Fazzino 2019); that plain fat is unpleasant is vanilla's per-item value, which no study shows. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-21-hedonics/hedonics-report.md@program-record-v19` §§ 1, 2, 6.
- **Script and Lua levers on vanilla trait definitions (42.21).** A same-name `character_trait_definition` block replaces the whole definition (omitted keys default, the last body wins), unlike `item`'s per-key merge. Cost, `IsProfessionTrait` and the name have no Lua setter, and a grant cannot be removed from Lua. `DisabledInMultiplayer` is inert. The server applies its own definitions for XP boosts and weight; cost and exclusions are client-UI only. `applyTraits` climbs levels through `LevelPerk`, so the band remap decides the final trait (Weight Gain ends with OUT_OF_SHAPE). Eight live checks are listed and unrun (§ 7); only the worked example #3860 is on a page. `docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-14-strength-redesign/trait-levers.md@program-record-v19` L69-106, L127-136, L186-195.
