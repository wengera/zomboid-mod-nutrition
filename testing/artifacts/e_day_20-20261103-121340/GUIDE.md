## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` the zombies the threat branch holds, `inputs.nearest` the nearest listed, each read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is `agent.base.replay_decisions.align`'s, the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour`: one frame on every line, the same frame (seq 6605, the log's last) on each of lines 754–845. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 193 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a194 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a195 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (13 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e17.kills.per_fight` uses). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true, "idle" `hasTarget` false. `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** is the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player (PZA/1.4); a **bite reaction** is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` `Bite`. A body part is named by its index `i` in the frame's `body.parts`. A square's `building`, `room`, `door` and edge fields are the kept map's square record (the last map line naming it before the reading cited). A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id.
