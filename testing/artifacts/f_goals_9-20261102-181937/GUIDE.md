## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is the one `replay_decisions.align` pairs with it (the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`). The threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`, the goal's at `inputs.branches.goal`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 283 lines with `sent` true are its 283 rows, a2 to a284 (the equip a1 and the stop a285 are the driver's), each type checked. Every result below is read off the `act` lines of `state.log`. Spans in seconds are `wallMs` and `wall` differences on the one host's clock, used as spans only (the wildcard sentence below). A zombie reads fallen when its `onFloor` reads true; it reads null on a standing zombie.

**Calls and goals.** A call is `goals.log` line *i* (call *i*, 1 to 35, its `cycle` *i*), `loop.llm.calls[i-1]` its row and `loop.goals[i-1]` the goal it yielded; every row reads `ok` true, `reask` false, `skipped` `[]` and `source` `llm`. A call's view is its row's `prompt` (`llm.py` sends it on stdin; the system prompt goes by `--system-prompt`). Each call started on the decision line where the goal field turns to `{"pending": "llm"}` (35 such runs, from line 1 at game minute 562 to line 1302 at game minute 735), and its goal's span is read off the goal field of the lines after it (its first line with a `type`, and the line its `state` leaves `running`).
