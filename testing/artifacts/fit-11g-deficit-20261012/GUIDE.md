How to read it. The objective maximises the smallest scored margin (a margin is the distance inside a band over its half-width; `loss` = −`smallest` + 10 × `shortfall`): the scored oracles are the 36 h fast's next-day intakes `fast_r2` and `fast_r3` (S1613, S1614), Karl 2021's DEF arm `karl_def` at the worst of twin K's five billings (S1325) and King 2011 at steady state `king` (S1312); `karl_bal` (S1325 inside S1892's −64 to −46 %), `steady`, `six` (S1608), `s1255` and the Whybrow and E-MECHANIC entries are constraints (the Whybrow and E-MECHANIC entries "fixed": no fitted constant moves them, the Task 7 report). A grid of 1568 points (`spec.params`: BALANCE_SPAN_KCAL 2600 to 7500 in 14 values, DEFICIT_FLOOR 0 to 0.2 in 8, EX_BYPASS_LO 0.282, 0.33, EX_BYPASS_HI 0.372 to 0.75 in 7) and 4 refinement rounds at ×1.03, 1585 candidates.
