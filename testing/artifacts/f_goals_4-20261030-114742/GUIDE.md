## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs`, or of a `decision.log` `wall` or a call's `wall` against one, on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 223 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a224 in turn, each type checked (the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a225 the loop's). An act's frames are those between its `accepted` line and its end line. `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (36 rows, threat episodes, counted from 0). A call is `goals.log` line *i* + 1, cited as call *i* (0–45), the same row as `loop.llm.calls[i]`; a call's start line is the first decision line at or after its `wall`, and its collection the first goal-branch line after its worker ended whose why reads a goal `proposed` or `refused`. A goal is `loop.goals[r]`, cited as row *r* (0–31). A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read at the mark; the grab follows it.
