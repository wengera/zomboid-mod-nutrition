## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin` the line's own); its frame is `agent.base.replay_decisions.align`'s, the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour` (one frame on every one of the 616 lines). `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` the held list and `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names; its why's `dist` is the one its sending line logged. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 136 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a137 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a138 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (17 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e20.hits.by_fight` uses); a decision line lies in the fight whose `startWall` to `end.endS` holds its `wall` (fight 2 lines 198–241, 8 lines 367–399, 12 lines 557–577, 13 lines 578–600). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true, "standing" `onFloor` null, "chasing" `hasTarget` true. `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. "Read attacking" is a frame reading `me.attackers` 1 or more. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding`; a part is `body.parts[*]` by its index `i` (the dressings' worn items name parts 0, 1, 3 and 9: `Base.Bandage_LeftHand`, `Bandage_RightHand`, `Bandage_RightLowerArm` and `Bandage_Neck`, `state.log` seq 2243–6326; part 9 is the neck). A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event. The frame's `act` group is {aid, state, step}: `act.step` is the reflex's current step (PZA/1.8).

**A melee's steps** (the reflex's header in `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua` at `967c7076`, code not readings): `stand`, `face`, `swing` (the press; the swing's progress line is written at it), `back` (after a strike that left the target standing, along the planner's heading `backVx`, `backVy`, facing the target) and `hit`: **the reflex reads the player's hit reaction through `hasHitReaction` on the client's tick, raises its `hits` count once a reaction and takes the step `hit`, pressing nothing** (its header's rule 2, L29, and L329–L334); the frame's `me.hitReaction` is `getHitReaction()` read at 10 Hz (`PZAgent_Sense.lua`). A **swing's distance** is the progress line's `d` at its `swing` step's first line, the **press**; what a swing **did** is the next progress or done line's `hp` against the swing line's (lowered: below it; killed: `end` `dead`, `kill` true); a **knock-down** is the next line's `knockdowns` above the step's. Times after a press are differences of `wallMs` from the swing line's.

**Survival iteration 23** (code at `6350d9ab`, not readings; the spec's § 2), as the F14 guide reads it (`testing/artifacts/f_goals_15-20261105-092752/GUIDE.md`, How to read it): P29-A `ROUTE_CROSS`, P29-B `GOAL_STALL`, P29-E `HEALTH_NEAR` (under `FLEE_HEALTH` 40 with no zombie within `DANGER_R` the line logs `health_near` {near: 0} and the hysteresis fires no `health` flee; with a held zombie in view and the low fight barred the `health` flee still fires, `threat._flee_reason`, P29-E fix 1; the low fight is barred under `FLEE_HEALTH_FLOOR` 20), P29-D `DRESS_FINISH` (only with nobody within `DANGER_R`), P29-G `MANY_NEAR` (`many_near` {n}: the standing count within `DANGER_R` on a line where the old count would have fled `many`), P29-F `FINISH_LONE`, P29-C `CLOSE_IDLE` (P27-J's walk to close at an idle zombie only) and P29-H' `FLEE_HELD_STANCE` (`flee_held_stance` {target, d, bearing, heading}: a flee walk heading at a held zombie dropped).
