## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs`, or of a `decision.log` `wall` or a call's `wall` against one, on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 139 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a1–a139 in turn, each type checked (the `stop` a140 is the loop's; the kit sent nothing). An act's frames are those between its `accepted` line and its end line. `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (27 rows, threat episodes, counted from 0). A call is `goals.log` line *i* + 1, cited as call *i* (0–30), the same row as `loop.llm.calls[i]`; a goal is `loop.goals[r]`, row *r* (0–30): every collected call yielded one goal, so goal *r* is call *r*'s answer or, for a refused answer or the timeout, the scripted stand-in. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read at the mark; the grab follows it. Game minutes 559–764 are 09:19–12:44 of the first day. A part is a frame's `body.parts` entry by its index `i`; `body.bitten` and `body.scratched` count the parts so wounded and not bandaged. Building 2's door square is (10721.5, 10638.5), the centre of the door square (10721, 10638) the views list for it; a distance "from building 2" below is the frame's or the view's `pos` against that point, never the view's own one-decimal column. A kill is read three ways that agree here: the act line's `result.targetDead`, the `zombie_dead` event naming the id, and the death event's `zombiesKilled`.
