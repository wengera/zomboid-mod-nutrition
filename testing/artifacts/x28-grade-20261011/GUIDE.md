# `x28-grade-20261011` reading guide

**`x28-grade-20261011/summary.json`**, **`rows.json`**, **`x281.json`**, **`x282.json`**, **`x283.json`** and **`x284.json`** — a derived artifact: an analysis run, not a live run. Produced by `testing/experiments/x28_grade.py`, committed at `1bbf6d3` before the chain's first boot and amended once at `548f71c` after the chain ran and before any staged output (the projection arm keeps its events inside its own synthetic clock: the first grade raised a ValueError on 9 drinks after the arm's end at 137.25, and that output was deleted unpublished), over the four committed runs `x281-20261011-033356`, `x282-20261011-034142`, `x283-20261011-035042` and `x284-20261011-040034` with `tools/replay_live.py` at `6575cc5` (`sleep_allowed` false, the live server's option; the auto-drink detector counted, never injected) and `x28_steady_lib.py` at `1bbf6d3`; nothing is booted. Its inputs are each run's committed `steady-N.json`, `events.json`, `start.json`, `start-stats.json`, `player.json`, ring pages and slot files. `python testing/experiments/x28_grade.py --out <dir>` reproduces all six files byte for byte (re-run on 2026-10-11 into a scratch folder, `cmp` silent; the files carry no wall time): `rows.json` 64327 bytes, sha256 `a25d517b…58a62054`; `summary.json` 3003, `45e2648f…6c236908`; `x281.json` 5080, `a78cee4f…a9325c3f`; `x282.json` 5787, `6c980924…ffa31aec`; `x283.json` 6266, `8f6c046a…1b761061`; `x284.json` 5403, `98655582…4d3685e2`.

How to read it. The chain by server reuse is four sessions on one world (session 1's guide; the first attempt `x281-20261010-190415` was reaped before any reading, B3-1, and is not an input). The drink events follow ruling T5R-1: every ring row's `fluids.autoDrop` is an exogenous drink event of 2 × autoDrop L, landed by the tool one minute ahead of the server (session 1's guide); the events held in each start record are left out so that none lands twice. F-T5R-2's noise sips (autoDrop 3.66e-15 to 3.72e-9, fixed after these runs at `5244b4a`) are among the events and the counts; F-T5-2 is fixed in the lib. Each `x28N.json` holds one session: `diff` (the session's replay of live re-run from its own start record, under HUNGER 0.002, es 0.005 and L 0.5 %), `autodrop_events.water_g`, `detector_alone`, `meals` (each meal stop: `es_read`, `es_ring` the first live ring row after it, `es_replay` the session replay's row, `es_diff`, with `target`, `portions`, `eaten_kcal` and the carry), `refills` and `driver_verdicts` (copied). `rows.json` holds `row7`, `drinking` and `sodium`; `summary.json` one line a session and each verdict. Chain day d runs from age 24d − 7 to 24d + 17; mod day k from age 24k to 24k + 24 (07:00 to 07:00).
- **Row 7, es on day 5** (`row7.day5_hourly`, `row7.es_min`, `row7.es_max`, `row7.replay_max_diff`, `row7.band_ok`; `summary.json` `verdicts.R7REPLAY`, `verdicts.R7BAND`): the ring row at or before each hour from 01:00 to 24:00 of day 5 (ages 114 to 137, all x284), live against x284's session replay at the same s. Live es reads 1.04486 to 1.04668; live minus replay at most 0.0011186, so the 0.005 agreement holds at every hour (`replay_ok` true). - **Row 7, the intake** (`row7.intake.<d>.live_kcal`, `.over_maint`, `.within_3pct`; `summary.json` `verdicts.R7INTAKE`): landed eats at their ring age per chain day over the maintenance 2089.32 (`row7.maint_kcal`). Days 2 to 5 each read 2033.33 kcal, 0.97320 of it, inside ±3 %. **The intake is quantised**: the meals are whole portions (`TKX.Mixed650` 650, `TKX.Mixed217` 216.67, `TKX.Unit50` 50 kcal) with the miss carried on, and every request day eats the same 583.33 + 700 + 750; the requests themselves sum to 2029.94, 2031.93, 2034.47 and 2036.06 kcal on days 2 to 5 (0.9716 to 0.9745 of the maintenance; this guide's arithmetic over `row7.meal_es[i].target`). The identical 0.97320 is the portion grid, not four equal readings, and the ±3 % clause holds on the requests as on the eats. Day 1, the maintenance day, reads 2100.0 (`row7.intake.1.live_kcal`); day 0 1400.0 from two meals.

- **The drinking** (`drinking.by_day.<d>`, `drinking.intervals`): the mod side is every ring row's autoDrop × 2 L (`mod_l`); the canteen side each refill's fill less the next refill's left-over, booked on the later refill's day (`canteen_l`; no interval ran dry, `intervals[i].empty_at_end` false). **Only days 2 and 5 are graded** (`in_band` true): their ring covers the whole day (`ring_hours` 23.98 and 23.99), and the mod side reads 2.809 and 2.607 L, inside the drinking-volume oracle's 2.0 to 3.0 L/d as a reading on the live body; the canteen side reads 2.608 and 2.807 L, a cross-check whose ±0.2 L differs by the booking day. Days 1, 3 and 4 miss 1.1 to 1.2 h of ring to the session-attach gaps (`ring_hours` 22.81, 22.86 and 22.87), so a drink there is missing from the mod side and the grade leaves them ungraded (`in_band` null; their `mod_l` is do-not-cite, their `canteen_l` 2.609, 2.809 and 2.664 a cross-check only). No total over the days is graded. Food water adds 938.46 g on each request day (`drinking.by_day.2.food_water_g`). Every real drink is 2 × the 0.1 THIRST trigger, 200.02 to 202.03 g across the four rings (this guide's count over the ring pages; #2770's `min(container, 2 × thirst)`), so the intake is quantised in 0.2 L steps; daily volume is unaffected.
