## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py` at `42e7f49b`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo` (#4220, #4222). A line reading is this write-up's replay of `policies.line_to` at `42e7f49b` on the World as the planner held it at a tick (every line whose `wallMs` is at or before the tick's `wall`); over fight 2 (lines 144–229) the replay's hold condition (a room square, and every zombie within the hold radius reading `False`) holds on exactly the 23 ticks the log reads `held_inside`. A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall` less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (`DISENGAGE_R` 10 while a threat policy runs), `inputs.nearest` the nearest held zombie's distance. The why's `flee` field names the rule the branch answered (`held_inside`, `many`, `held`, `pair`, `health`, `unreachable`), and `shelter`, `step`, `turn`, `stuck` and `ahead` are the flee's own fields; all are read as values here. `loop.actions` is the planner's 32 sends a2–a33 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's seven rows; `loop.fights` the two runs of the threat branch.

The threat rules at the boot (`planner/survive.py` and `planner/policies.py` docstrings at `42e7f49b`; code, not readings): flee `many` when more than `MAX_FIGHT` 2 zombies are counted (over the hold radius while a fight runs, else within 6); `health` under `FLEE_HEALTH` 40; `pair` with two or more within 6 and health under 60 or any wound; `unreachable` when no held zombie is a reachable target; a running flee is kept as `held` while more than one zombie is held or health is under 60. **The inside hold** (fix wave P, M-6): when the character stands on a room square and every held zombie's `line_to` reads `False`, the branch sends nothing (`held_inside`); the hold ends when a line clears or reads unknown. The `Fight` walks to the centre of the target's square beyond `FIGHT_REACH` 2.0; within reach a blocked line sends one walk to the centre of a `reachable_adjacent` square within `REPOSITION_R` 2.5, and a second blocked read marks the target unreachable for `UNREACHABLE_S` 20 s. A `Flee` first picks a shelter (standing on a room square it adopts that building, kept to its exterior door; else the exterior door the map reads neither locked nor barricaded whose approach square is nearest, within `SHELTER_FLEE_R` 25) and walks its steps at a run; with no plan, it steers 3 s legs along the away vector weighted by 1/distance, turned by `STEER_TURNS` (0, ±30, ±60, ±90 degrees) to the first heading whose map line `STEER_PROBE` 3 tiles ahead reads clear with no held zombie ahead, else the first clear one, else the away vector kept; a leg that moved under `STUCK_MOVE` 0.5 tiles is stuck, and the next leg skips the 0 turn; a sprint with the nearest within `SPRINT_R` 2.0, else a run.
