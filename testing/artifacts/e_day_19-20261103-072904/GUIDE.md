## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` the zombies the threat branch holds, `inputs.nearest` the nearest listed, each read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour`: one frame on every line but line 1289, where two consecutive frames (seq 8227 and 8228) read the same hour and `worldAge` and the first is taken (`agent.base.replay_decisions.align` refuses the record for that line, so the alignment here is its rule with the first frame taken; seq 7114/7115 and 9534/9535 also share an hour, on no line's). A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 157 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a158 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a159 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (12 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e16.kills.per_fight` uses). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player (PZA/1.4). A square's `building`, `room`, `door`, `window` and edge fields are the kept map's square record (the last map line naming it before the reading cited); a zombie's square is its `x` and `y` floored. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id. "The frame after an act's end against the frame before its accept" is how a strike's effect on its target is read below; a hit landing late in one act's span is read in the next.
