**Reading notes (Plan 17 Task 1 review).** The shipped dehydration constant is 79.83361067025903, the solve for the 1 to 0.1 curve form under ruling T1-1 from `dehyd_dpdt_per_h`; the file's `a_dehyd` 69.64685478441292 is the full-span (1 to 0) solve and is not the shipped constant (read `dehyd_dpdt_per_h`).
