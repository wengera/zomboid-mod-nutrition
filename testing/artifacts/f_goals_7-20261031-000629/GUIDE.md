## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 308 lines with `sent` true are its 308 rows, a2 to a309 (the equip a1 and the stop a310 are the driver's), each type checked. Every result below is read off the `act` lines of `state.log`.

**Survival iteration 15** (code, not readings): E14's (`testing/artifacts/e_day_14-20261030-235607/GUIDE.md`, "How to read it"). Two rules carry this session. **The endurance hold** (`testing/agent/base/planner/hold.py` L73–L107 at `b5082f14`): it sets at `ENDURANCE_T` or under, and with P25-C's timed release clears once `HOLD_SPAN_MIN` game minutes have passed and no zombie has been within `DANGER_R` for `HOLD_QUIET_S`, logging P25-M's `release` {kind, why, endurance, held_min} on that tick's threat why; released so, it sets again only under the endurance it released at. Under it the threat branch's flee reason reads `strain`, its why carrying `strain` {kind `endurance`, endurance, fatigue, armPain, strain}. **The care's bleeding step** (`testing/agent/base/planner/care.py` L355–L388 at `b5082f14`): the worst bleeding part is bandaged with a clean bandage, else a clean rag, else a dirty rag only when the part's `bleedingTime` exceeds `DIRTY_RAG_MIN` 10.0 (L105, L370); with a dressing of any kind held it sends nothing (L378), and only with none held is a cotton garment ripped (`no_rag`, L381–L382); a dirty rag held is washed at a known water source within `WASH_R` 15.0 (step 3); an item whose action ended in a failure other than a supersede is held out of the choice for `CARE_RETRY_MIN` (`observe`, `_items`, L252–L322).
