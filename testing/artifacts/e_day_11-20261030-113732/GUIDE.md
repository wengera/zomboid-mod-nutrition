## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or a `decision.log` `wall` against a `wallMs`) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held, all read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names; on such a line the why's other fields are the running action's, written at its send. An act's frames are those between its `accepted` line and its end line. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 120 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a121 in turn (each type checked; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a122 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (10 rows, threat episodes); a **fight-line run** is a run of consecutive threat lines whose why reads `fight` true (`grade_e8.pull.fight_runs` 17 counts these). A part is a frame's `body.parts` entry by its index `i`. `body.armPain` is the summed `getPain()` of the six arm parts and `body.strain` each arm's largest `getStiffness()` (the schema's notes, `PZAgent_Schema.lua` at `eff3bdce`; code). A zombie's `health` and `onFloor` are the frame's listing of it; a kill is read three ways that agree here: the act line's `result.targetDead`, the `zombie_dead` event naming the id, and the death event's `zombiesKilled`.
