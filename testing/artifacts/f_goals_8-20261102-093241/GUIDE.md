## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`, the goal's at `inputs.branches.goal`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 288 lines with `sent` true are its 288 rows, a2 to a289 (the equip a1 and the stop a290 are the driver's), each type checked. Every result below is read off the `act` lines of `state.log`.

**Calls and goals.** A call is `goals.log` line *i* (call *i*, 1 to 40), `loop.llm.calls[i-1]` its row and `loop.goals[i-1]` the goal it yielded: the model's when the row reads `ok` true (`source` `llm`), else the scripted source's stand-in (`source` `scripted`). A call's view is its row's `prompt`, the view the model answered (`llm.py` sends it on stdin; the system prompt goes by `--system-prompt`). Each call started on the decision line where the goal field turns to `{"pending": "llm"}` (40 such lines, from line 1 at game minute 563 to line 1433 at game minute 753).

**The goal source** (`testing/agent/base/planner/llm.py` at `f20ca7e9`; code): the parse takes the first balanced `{...}` of the answer's text that decodes as a JSON object (`first_object`, L137–L153) and checks it against the view that collects it, the range measured from the calling view's `pos` (`place_error`, L210–L234): a `LootBuilding` of a shown building whose `looted` reads true is `invalid: looted already` (L230–L231); an `ExploreToward` farther than 60 tiles from `pos` is `invalid: out of range` and one farther than the view's `woods_r` from every listed building `invalid: in the woods`, unless (P25-R) its square lies within `WOODS_R` 40 of the view's `toward` square or door, which is accepted whatever its distance (L213–L215, `_at_toward` L237–L247). A refused answer is laid over the next call's `last_goal` once, `end` `refused` with its `why` (L386–L389, `_start` L341–L343), and the scripted fallback's goal stands in. The scripted source answers a view with no unlooted building within 60 tiles by an `ExploreToward` at the view's `edge` (`testing/agent/base/planner/goals.py` L100–L106 at `b5082f14`; code). **The system prompt** (`goal_prompt.system`, rendered from `schema/goals.json`; 5861 bytes) says, in its refusal rule, "a LootBuilding only a building listed without the L flag", in its first strategy line "loot a building until it reads L before the next", and in its loop sentence "you loot the nearest building listed without L within 60 tiles (LootBuilding), then the next nearest … an ExploreToward finds the next house at the edge of the known buildings"; the view's building line flags `L` as "looted (nothing left to take)".

**The pre-boot probe** (`testing/agent/drivers/f_goals_8.py` L120–L161 and L164–L174; code): `claude_answers` makes one live `-p` call through the goal source's own argv with a one-line system prompt, ok only on exit 0 with a non-empty `result` text, and the boot is refused unless it is ok (L174), after `claude --version` (`claude_available`) answers.
