## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs`, or of a `decision.log` `wall` or a call's `wall` against one, on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 302 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a303 in turn, each type checked (the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a304 the loop's). An act's frames are those between its `accepted` line and its end line. `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (28 rows, threat episodes, counted from 0). A call is `goals.log` line *i* + 1, cited as call *i* (0–75), the same row as `loop.llm.calls[i]`; a goal is `loop.goals[r]`, row *r* (0–75): every call yielded one goal, so goal *r* is call *r*'s answer or, for call 38, the scripted stand-in. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read at the mark; the grab follows it. The game clock crosses midnight: game minutes 1278–1439 are 21:18–23:59 of the first day, 0–66 00:00–01:06 of the second. A part is a frame's `body.parts` entry by its index `i`; `body.bitten` and `body.scratched` count the parts so wounded and not bandaged. A kill is read two ways that agree here: the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id; the death event's `zombiesKilled`, the third, never came.
