## How to read it

As run 1's guide: a frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before; a planner tick is one `decision.log` line (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`); `loop.actions` is the planner's sends with their ends; `loop.needs` rows are `[gameMin, hunger, thirst, fatigue, health, serverHunger]`.
