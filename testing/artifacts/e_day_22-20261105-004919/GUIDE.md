## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of the state logs in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is `agent.base.replay_decisions.align`'s, the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour` (one frame on every one of the 1675 lines). `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6 and `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names; **its why's `dist` is the one its sending line logged, carried, while `inputs.nearest` reads its own frame** (lines 383, 395 and 405 read `dist` 1.8461373600468207, 1.6398415284234893 and 1.605264989540826, their sending lines 380, 392 and 404's, with `inputs.nearest` 0.7203331801617183, 0.7352711505668892 and 0.7549895572571734). A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 350 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a351 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a352 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (43 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e19.hits.by_fight` uses); a send lies in the fight whose `startS` to `end.endS` holds its `sentS`. A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true, "standing" `onFloor` null, "chasing" `hasTarget` true. `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player; "read attacking" is a frame reading it 1 or more. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding`. A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event; each here names the target of a melee whose done line read `end` `dead` and `kill` true. The frame's `act` group is {aid, state, step}: `act.step` is the reflex's current step (PZA/1.8).

**A melee's steps** (the reflex's header in `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua` at `ea4ba827`, code not readings): `stand` (in the stance facing the target), `face` (the swing's face, a tick before its press), `swing`, `shove`, `stomp` and `floor` (the presses: a shove's face is already step `shove`, so a shove's progress line is written at its face, up to `K.FACE_TICKS` ticks before its press, and the press itself is read at its `swing` event), `back` (after a strike that left the target standing, along the planner's heading `backVx`, `backVy`, facing the target), `hit` (a hit reaction on the player: no press), `knock_wait` (the target knocked down, not yet on the floor), `stomp_walk` (the walk to a fallen target). A **strike's distance** is the progress line's `d` at its step's first line (the live `DistToProper` read, #4621), unless named as a frame's. A **knock-down** is the next line's `knockdowns` count above the step's; a strike that **lowered** its target is the next line's `hp` below the step's.
