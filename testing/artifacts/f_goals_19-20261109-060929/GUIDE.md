## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, **cited as line *n*, counted from 1** (`replay_decisions`'s convention and the shot index's `decision_line`); the grade's `crawler_first.rows[*].i` and `group_leave.rows[*].i0`/`i1` are indices counted from 0, so its row `i` is line `i` + 1. **The frame at a line** is the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`, the latest such at or before the line's `wall`: `replay_decisions.align` pairs every one of the 1614 lines with one frame. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 324 lines with `sent` true are a2–a325 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1); a1 is the kit's `equip`, a326 the stop after the death. A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health`, `onFloor`, `known`, `state`, `behind`, `headX`, `headY`}; **standing** is `onFloor` not true; a **crawler** is `crawler` true; a **fallen** zombie `onFloor` true.

**The facing.** `me.bodyAngle` (`getAnimAngleRadians()`), `me.lookAngle`, `me.fwdAngle` and `me.aimAngle` are radians on the world axes; a zombie's **offset** below is the absolute angle between `bodyAngle` and `atan2(dy, dx)` of the line from the character to the zombie on the same frame (`grade_x2`'s convention). The press leaf (PZA/1.11) carries the Lua's own reading at the press: `look_off` and `body_off` in degrees, `facing` the snap mode (`unaimed_snap` on all 53 here) and, from Plan 33 Task 1, `head_ticks`.

**A press** is a meet press: a `press` leaf on a melee act line, one per press, 53 in all; its kind (`swing`, `shove`, `stomp`, `floor`) the step it came from and its outcome `grade_x1`'s off the leaf. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; the **union** joins those runs with the reflex's own `hit` step runs; a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding` on a frame. A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event. Part *i* is `body.parts[*].i`.

**The composition** (code at `16f8774b` and `749eef49`, not readings). `GROUP_LEAVE` (`group.py` `_group_leave`, `_leave_leg`) opens when `GROUP_LEAVE_N` 3 standing zombies within `GROUP_ENTRY_R` 6.0 each read nearer than on the line before, the character in the open with no wall at its back; its leg on each line is the first that applies: `shove` once a leave at the nearest standing zombie within `STAND_MIN_R` 0.61, `door` while a known door within `LEAVE_DOOR_R` 6.0 is held or reads `ok` (the Flee's shelter plan: approach, open, enter, close), `run` (the threat plan's `many` flee) with more than `RETREAT_MAX_N` 4 standing within 6.0, else `retreat` (the Fight's stance on the nearest standing zombie, its back leg heading away from the group's centroid); it holds while three stand within 6.0 (within `DANGER_R` on the door's walk) and ends `strung` when fewer do. `DRAG_GUARD_FIGHT` (`_drag_fight`) sends the drag-down guard's one shove of a stuck episode under a hit reaction or a pinned run, logged `drag_fight`, then the run. `SIDE_CLEAR` (`_side_clear`) turns a run steer off a standing zombie within `SIDE_R` 1.0 over its first `SIDE_LOOK` 3.0 tiles ahead, and with no clear turn sends the least-bad heading. `CRAWLER_FIRST` (`crawler.py`) stomps a crawler or fallen zombie within `CRAWLER_R` 1.0 while a standing one is within `DANGER_R`. `CARE_COMMIT` (Plan 32, `care_yield.py` `_care_commit`) yields the threat branch to a wanted care action whenever no standing zombie is within `KEEP_R` 2.5. The walk skill ends `done` within `K.DONE_R` 1.0 tiles of its target (`PZAgent_Skills.lua`). The idle snap (`FACING.idle`, `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua`) turns the body onto the nearest standing zombie within 2.5 tiles only while no skill runs (`K.cur` nil).
