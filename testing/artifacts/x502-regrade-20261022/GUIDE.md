# `x502-regrade-20261022` reading guide

**`x502-regrade-20261022/regrade.json`** — produced by `testing/experiments/x502_regrade.py` at `6b130b45` (committed before its one run, unedited since), a derived artifact: an analysis run and not a live run. It reads x502's committed `floor.json`, ring, `events.json`, `start.json`, `start-stats.json`, `player.json` and `replay.json` (their sha256 in `source_sha256`) and runs `tools/replay_live.py` (unchanged since the run's `replay_tool_commit` `212e89f2`) four times: `frozen` (the committed events), `early` (each eat at its landing row's previous row's age plus 1e-6 h), `late` (each eat at its landing row's age, as an `age` event) and `mid` (half-way). 13511 bytes, sha256 `99f9b76afa7028e42b0d70cadb9558eb9f6636d94f4eaabc40dfc57af8cac727`.

How to read it. `frozen_reproduces_committed` true: the frozen arm's diff is the run's `replay.json` diff to the last bit. `arms.<name>.fields` holds each arm's diff (`late` equal to `frozen`: max 0.0010714037308545699, 13 over; `early` max 0.0010978405788257817, 13 over; `mid` max 0.0002311821150793314, none over). The grade (stated in the script's docstring before its run): every row the frozen diff graded reads a live `moodLast.u` inside [min(early, late) - 1e-3, max(early, late) + 1e-3] and `hedonics.f` within 1e-4 of both; **as predicted** on all 35 rows (`observed.n` 35, `observed.off` empty), every live value inside [early, late] itself (`observed.rows[*]`); the band is 0.0021692443096803515 wide after the fourth eat (`observed.max_width`). A replay of x502 grades `moodLast.u` to the band's width, not to 1e-3, unless the eat's landing age is logged.

**Do not cite from this file:** `tol`, `eps_h` (the script's settings); `arms.early` and `arms.mid` as replays of live (each is a placement the live run may not have had: only the band they span is the reading).
