## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py` at `259a58bc`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo` (#4220, #4222). A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall` less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (`DISENGAGE_R` 10 while a threat policy runs, else 6), `inputs.nearest` the nearest held zombie's distance. The why's `flee` field names the rule the branch answered (`unreachable`, `held`, `many`, `unarmed`), `fight` true a fight, `cornered` the zombie a corner runs on, and `shelter`, `turn`, `probe`, `stuck`, `ahead` and `ended` are the flee's own fields; all are read as values here. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`; the counts "within 1.5 tiles" below are read off that frame's zombie list. `loop.actions` is the planner's 244 sends a2–a245 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's 24 rows; `loop.fights` the three runs of the threat branch. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read when it marked the shot and `decision_line` the decision log's line count then; the grab itself comes later, on the worker (§ 6).

The threat rules at the boot (`planner/survive.py` and `planner/policies.py` docstrings at `259a58bc`; code, not readings), as they bear on this session: `many` counts the hold radius's list while a fight runs and the `DANGER_R` count otherwise, and while a flee episode begun as `many` runs it reads the hold radius's count over `MAX_FIGHT` 2 or the nearest held zombie within `DANGER_R` (P23-AM 1); a running flee is kept as `held` while more than one zombie is held, and fights again only with one held at health 60 or over; a `many` flee beyond `SPRINT_R` 2.0 steers at a sprint while the frame's endurance reads above `SPRINT_END` 0.5 (P23-AM 3). A flee with no shelter plan steers 3 s legs along the away vector, turned by `STEER_TURNS` (0, ±30, ±60, ±90 degrees) to the first heading whose map line reads clear `AHEAD_R` 9 tiles (`probe` 9.0) or else `STEER_PROBE` 3 tiles (`probe` 3.0) ahead with no held zombie in its corridor (`ahead` the ids that refused a heading); a leg is **stuck** when it ended having moved under `STUCK_MOVE` 0.5 tiles, or (fix round 1, R1) while it runs having left the player within 0.5 tiles for `STEP_STALL_S` 1.0 s with a held zombie within `CORNERED_R` 1.5. **The corner** (`_cornered`): armed, with the nearest held zombie within 1.5 tiles, it fights the nearest zombie in reach, a `stuck` corner after a stuck leg (it ends with its zombie dead or gone, or the nearest beyond `CORNER_FREE_R` 5.0) and a `walk` corner on any other threat tick whose line to the zombie is not read blocked (it ends with the nearest beyond 1.5); with three or more held zombies within 1.5 tiles (`GROUP_R_N` 3) and no stuck leg there is no corner and the flee steers (P23-AM 4), and a running corner not taken `stuck` is dropped for such a group (fix round 1, G1). The cornered fight swings at its target within `POINT_BLANK_R` 1.0 whatever the line reads. The flee's shelter pick (`Flee._pick`) takes a known building's exterior door, read by its own edge (amendment 5), unlocked and unbarricaded, whose approach square lies within `SHELTER_FLEE_R` 25 tiles with no known zombie in its corridor and none on the building's squares. `unarmed` (the frame's weapon group null) flees before every other reason.
