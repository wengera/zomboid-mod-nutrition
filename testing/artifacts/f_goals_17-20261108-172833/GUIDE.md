## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of the state logs in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; **the frame at a line** is the one `replay_decisions.align` pairs with it (the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`; it accepts this log). `inputs.threats` is the planner's own count of the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6; **standing within 6 tiles** below is this writer's count off the frame at the line: a listed zombie with `onFloor` not true at `dist` 6.0 or less. The threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat` (its `flee` the flee's reason, its `strain` the hold that named it). A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 365 lines with `sent` true are a2–a366 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; a1 is the kit's `equip`). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health`, `onFloor`, `known`, `state`, `behind`, `headX`, `headY`}.

**The facing.** `me.dir` is the frame's written facing as one of eight compass points (`getDir()`, #4591): the grade's `facing` and `facing_all` read it, so their resolution is 45 degrees. **PZA/1.10 adds `me.lookAngle` (`getLookAngleRadians()`, the animation's angle with its twist and the head look) and `me.bodyAngle` (`getAnimAngleRadians()`, the body alone), in radians on the world axes** (`pza1.json`'s descriptions; code). Their convention, which the schema asks to be calibrated on live frames before a reading relies on it, is read here: on the 1058 pairs of consecutive frames whose later frame reads `running` with the position moving 0.25 tiles or more, `lookAngle` sat a median of 0.2549184999252751 degrees off `atan2(dy, dx)` of the movement, within 30 degrees on 1050, so a zombie's **look offset** below is the absolute angle between `lookAngle` (or `bodyAngle`) and `atan2` of the line from the character to the zombie on the same frame. The press leaf's `look_off` (PZA/1.10) is the Lua's own reading of the look's angle off the target at the press, in degrees.

**A press** is a meet press: a `press` leaf on a melee act line (`d_press`, `d_collide_pred`, `d_min`, `look_off`, `hp0`, `hp1`, `knock`, `cut_by`, `knock_p` on a shove), one per press, 82 in all; its kind (`swing`, `floor`, `shove`, `stomp`) is the step it came from and its outcome `grade_x1`'s off the leaf (`killed` hp1 0, `knocked` `knock` true, `lowered` hp1 below hp0, `cut` a `cut_by`, else `nothing`). The ladder's own `attack`, `shove` and `stomp` actions carry no leaf; their swing events are read off the `swing` events of the state log. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; the **union** joins those runs with the reflex's own `hit` steps; a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding` on a frame. A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event. Part *i* is `body.parts[*].i`; the names of parts 0, 1, 4 and 9 (`Hand_L`, `Hand_R`, `UpperArm_L`, `Neck` in the engine's body-part order) are this writer's inference.
