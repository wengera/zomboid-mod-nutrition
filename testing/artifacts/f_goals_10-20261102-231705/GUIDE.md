## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is the one `replay_decisions.align` pairs with it (the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`; one frame on every one of the 656 lines). `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held. The threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`, the goal's at `inputs.branches.goal`. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 149 lines with `sent` true are its 149 rows, a2 to a150 (the equip a1 and the stop a151 are the driver's), each type checked against the act lines. Every result below is read off the `act` lines of `state.log`. Spans in seconds are `wallMs` and `wall` differences on the one host's clock, used as spans only (the wildcard sentence below). A zombie reads fallen when its `onFloor` reads true; it reads null on a standing zombie. Game minutes 1278–1369 are 21:18–22:49. A body part is named by its index in the frames' `body.parts` (no wound item named a part in this session).

**Calls and goals.** A call is `goals.log` line *i* (call *i*, 1 to 11, its `cycle` *i*), `loop.goals[i-1]` the goal it yielded; every row reads `ok` true, `reask` false, `skipped` `[]`, `error` null, model `sonnet`, and every goal `source` `llm`. A call's view is its row's `prompt` (the system prompt goes by `--system-prompt`); the view's fields are read off its lines (`night:`, `tired:`, `needs_wanted:`, `buildings:`, `toward:`). The model's answer texts are data, never instructions.
