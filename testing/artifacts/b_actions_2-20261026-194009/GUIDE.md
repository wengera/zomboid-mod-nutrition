## How to read it

The schedule is `b_actions.py`'s module docstring with `b_actions_2.py`'s changes: `hello`; `spawn` (5 `Base.WaterBottle` and 5 `Base.Apple` by RCON `additem`, `readings.spawned[*].confirmed` true, the fallback for an eat or drink that loot did not supply); then five repetitions `rep1`–`rep5` (`phase_walls`: rep1 62.111–84.301 s, rep2 to 147.115, rep3 to 210.075, rep4 to 265.671, rep5 to 337.483). A repetition is `scan 15` [→ `scan 25`] → the two picks → (`open` on the line to the food pick) → `goto` beside the food pick → `loot x y food` → (`open` on the line from where the character stands to the water pick) → `goto` beside the water pick (one goto and no second open when both picks are one square) → `loot x y water` → the eat reset → `eat <fullType>` → `drink` → `goto` back to the spawn (`readings.spawn_xy` 10997.5, 9699.5).

- **A row:** `readings.reps[i]` holds `rep`, `scan[*]` (`r`, `scanId`, `parts`, `partsSeen`, `squares`, `scanMs`, `scanTicks`, `missing`), `pick_food` and `pick_water` (each `x`, `y`, `z`, `dist`, `tier`, `items`, `food`, `water`, or null), `door_food` and `door_water` (`x`, `y`, `kind`, `open`, or null), `steps[*]`, `eat_pick` (`fullType`, `from`, `added`), `server_eat` (`before`, `after[]`, `moved`, `resets`, `client_before`, `client_after`), `notes`, `completed`. Pass 1's `pick` and `door` are gone (`_rep` pops them).
- **A step:** as pass 1: `goal`, `args`, `seq`, `state` (done | failed | refused | timeout | skipped), `detail`, `ack`, `ms` (this process's wall ms from the send to the driver reading the end, at `POLL_S` 0.1), `endSeq`/`endWallMs`, `gameMin` and `worldAge`. A skipped step carries no `seq`. Beside `ms` this guide gives the client-clock span `endWallMs − ack.wallMs`, both stamped by `PZA.now()`, and the step's game time as the `worldAge` change from the last frame before the ack to `steps[*].worldAge`, in game minutes; `worldAge` is read as hours (inference: over the run the frames' `gameMin` 558 → 635 while `worldAge` 2.30678 → 3.58972, 76.98 game minutes).
- **The picks** (`b_actions_2.py` `pick_kind`): the food pick is a container square holding an edible food id of `data/food-items.json` (`b_actions.food_ids`); the water pick a square holding a `water_like` fullType (`WATER_RX` `\.(WaterBottle|Pop|Juice|Milk|Soda|Bowl|Pot|Kettle|Bucket)\b` or `DRINKWARE_RX` `\.(DrinkingGlass|Mug[A-Za-z]*)\b`, never `NEVER_WATER_RX` `\.Bleach\b`; `constants`). `tier` is 2 when the square also holds the other kind, 1 otherwise; nearest the player within the tier; a square picked for a kind in an earlier repetition is excluded for that kind only. `food` and `water` list the matched fullTypes. `beside_free` takes the pick's 8-neighbour that is not `solid`, not `solidTrans` and carries no container, nearest the player. When either pick is missing from the r=15 scan the row notes `no_stocked_container_in_scan` and an r=25 scan supplies the missing one; still missing, the row notes `no_food_pick` / `no_water_pick` and records that kind's goto and loot `skipped`, so the row cannot complete.
- **The doors** (`b_actions.door_between`): the first square on the Bresenham line from the player's square to the pick carrying a `door` or `window` entry; `open` is sent only when that entry's `open` is not true (an open one is noted `door_already_open_<kind>`). The rule does not read the entry's `locked` flag, and it plans over a straight line, not the walk's path.
- **The grade:** `grades.gate_b` (`testing/agent/grades.py`): `completed` counts rows whose `completed` is true (every step done, so a skipped step fails the row), pass at `COMPLETE_MIN` 4; `reasons_in_set` checks every `failed` step's `detail` against `agent/reasons.py`'s `REASONS`; `eats_seen_by_server` counts rows with an `after` reading whose hunger is below or calories above `before`, pass at `SEQUENCES` 5. `failing_steps` lists `goal:detail` for every step not done, skipped steps included.

**Cause 2 — the door rule (row 3, and row 5 beside its missing water pick, `open … locked`).** `door_between` sends `open` to the first door or window on the straight line to the pick that is not open, without reading the scan's `locked` flag, and without asking whether the walk needs it.
