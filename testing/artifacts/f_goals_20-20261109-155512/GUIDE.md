## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, **cited as line *n*, counted from 1** (`replay_decisions`'s convention and the shot index's `decision_line`); the grade's `crawler_first.rows[*].i` and `group_leave.rows[*].i0`/`i1` are indices counted from 0, so its row `i` is line `i` + 1; `standing.rows[*].first` and `last` count from 1. **The frame at a line** is the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`, the latest such at or before the line's `wall`: `replay_decisions.align` pairs every one of the 1678 lines with one frame. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 351 lines with `sent` true are a2–a352 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1); a1 is the kit's `equip`, a353 the stop after the cap. A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health`, `onFloor`, `known`, `state`, `behind`, `headX`, `headY`}; **standing** is `onFloor` not true; a **fallen** zombie `onFloor` true. **The planner's nearest** is a line's `inputs.nearest`, the nearest zombie the planner listed, a fallen one included; **the nearest standing** is this writer's reading of the frame at the line.

**The facing.** `me.bodyAngle` is in radians on the world axes; a zombie's **offset** below is the absolute angle between `bodyAngle` and the bearing to it on the same frame (`grade_x2`'s convention). The press leaf (PZA/1.11) carries the Lua's own `look_off` and `body_off` at the press.

**A press** is a meet press: a `press` leaf on a melee act line. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; the **union** joins those runs with the reflex's own `hit` step runs; a **wound rise** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding` on a frame. A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event. Part *i* is `body.parts[*].i`.

**The restricted leaves** (the run's do-not-cite rows): the grade's own union (`hits_union.union`, `landed_hits.union`) is a frame-window join; here the union is read off the runs and the steps (§ 1). `wounds.real` counts frames, not wounds (§ 2). `care_at_group.lines` and `door_leave.closes.inside` are the readers' own alignments; §§ 3 and 5 read the care lines, the interact lines, the frames' positions and the map lines instead. `group_leave.to_door`, `group_leave.runs` and `drag_guard.*` are the frozen x3 readers' (§ 10).
