## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is `agent.base.replay_decisions.align`'s, the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour`: one frame on every one of the 1680 lines. `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6 and `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 386 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a387 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a388 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (19 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_f9.kills.per_fight` uses; each is one contiguous run of `threat` lines here, fight 18 lines 1202–1680 with no end at the stop). A call is `goals.log` line *i*. A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true, "standing" `onFloor` null, "chasing" `hasTarget` true and "idle" `hasTarget` false. `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. A body part is named by its index in the frames' `body.parts`; the dressing's own item names it where one went on (part 4 took `Base.Bandage_LeftUpperArm` at seq 10344, part 6 `Base.Bandage_Chest`, part 1 `Base.Bandage_RightHand`, part 7 `Base.Bandage_Abdomen`, part 2 `Base.Bandage_LeftLowerArm`, the `item_added` events). A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty, and a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding`. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id. **A strike's effect** is the target's health on the first frame after its swing event, within 3 s, that reads it changed, against the frame before the swing event (on every strike here that lowered its target the change came 0.204 to 0.402 s after the swing event); "lowered nothing" means no such frame came before the next strike's own change. A strike's **distance** is the target's `dist` on its decision line's frame unless named as the frame after the swing event. The swing event carries no aid; it is paired with the strike whose aid the first frame after it names (`act.aid`, `grade_f9.facing.paired.frame` 62 of 62).
