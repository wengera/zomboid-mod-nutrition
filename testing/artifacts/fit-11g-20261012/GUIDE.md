# `fit-11g-20261012` reading guide

**The Plan 11g fit artifacts: how to read a compact fit.** Six folders, `fit-11g-20261012`, `fit-11g-20261012b`, `fit-11g-20261012c`, `fit-11g-20261012d`, `fit-11g-deficit-20261012` and `fit-11g-deficit-20261012b`, each hold **`fit-compact.json`** and **`grid.json`** and nothing else (ruling T6c-4: a fit artifact is committed in a compact form, because fix 1's full file was 54 MB; the full `fit.json` stays in the gitignored `testing/runs/<the same name>/`, byte-identical to the fit's output, and the compact file names it by `source_sha256` and `source_bytes`). `grid.json` is the search the tool was given (each parameter's grid values and the refinement's `rounds` and `factor`); `fit-compact.json` holds `spec` (the same search, as the tool read it), `head` (the git HEAD of the tree the fit ran on), `candidates` (how many points were scored), `chosen` (the point the tool chose and its scores) and `profile` (per parameter, the best loss the fit found among its candidates at each value it tried), written by `tools/fit_gut_satiety.py --compact` (`2e52af1d`; the first three folders' compacts were projected from their full files after the fact, the others written by the fit run itself). **What is citable**: every leaf of `chosen` (the chosen constants in `chosen.params`, the loss in `chosen.loss`, and the scores at the chosen point: for the satiety fits each oracle's model reading `chosen.oracles.<name>.value` and its `miss`, null for a reading-kind entry; for the deficit fits each scored oracle's `values.<name>` and `margins.<name>`, `smallest`, `shortfall` and `feasible`); `profile` as a reading of the loss surface the candidates sampled (it is the minimum over the candidates that happened to carry that value, not a conditional optimum, so its shape depends on the grid and the refinement path); `spec`, `candidates`, `head` and the source fields as provenance. **What a fit is not**: every oracle value here is the mod's own model replayed on the lupa host (Karl 2021's twin on the server path) against a study's band in the registry, not a study reading and not a live reading; a `miss` is relative to that registry entry's band at the commit the fit scored, not to the study. **Reproducing a fit**: the full file is reproducible only by re-running the tool on the tree that fit ran on (below, per folder); a later HEAD scores a different kernel or registry. The four satiety fits ran on an uncommitted working tree over their `head`, so the kernel and the registry they scored are those of the pathspec commit that landed that work, named per folder below.

**`fit-11g-20261012/fit-compact.json`** and **`grid.json`** — `fit-compact.json` 12002 bytes, sha256 `d5fac2b9…26036dbd`; `grid.json` 310 bytes, `2d2b4102…e79a7e41`; the full `fit.json` 11532973 bytes, sha256 `69bae8fe0d2452e6…` (`source_sha256`), in `testing/runs/fit-11g-20261012/`. **Plan 11g Task 6, the gut stage G fed at emptying; superseded.** `tools/fit_gut_satiety.py` on the working tree over `8c21890a` (`head`; Plan 11g Task 6 refactor: `NR.sandbox()` and `K.satiety.drain`), whose fitted constants, labels and registry additions (`BY_NAME`, `NAMED`, `check`) landed in `5c95f28a`. The kernel it fitted is `8c21890a`'s (the fit patches constants only); the registry it scored is `testing/tests/kernel/gut_oracles.py` as at `8c21890a`.

How to read it. A grid of 2592 points (`spec.params`: G_HALF_LIFE_H 0.35, 0.6, 1.0, 1.6; G_REQ 40, 120, 360; G_STEEP 0.02, 0.04; FULL_WEIGHT 0.45, 0.55, 0.65; W_PROTEIN 1.0, 1.5, 2.5; W_FAT 0.4, 0.7, 1.0, 1.4; PROTEIN_FILL 4, 8, 12; LIQUID_WEIGHT 0.2) and 8 refinement rounds at ×1.25 (`spec.refine`), 2704 candidates (`candidates`); the chosen point (`chosen.params`) is FULL_WEIGHT 0.542383, G_HALF_LIFE_H 1.25, G_REQ 158.606, G_STEEP 0.019723, LIQUID_WEIGHT 0.181397, PROTEIN_FILL 11.8338, W_FAT 0.405618, W_PROTEIN 1.5, at loss 1.8465319636570465 (`chosen.loss`; the provisional constants read 5.1403, the Task 6 report). **Status: superseded, and degenerate.** The Task 6 review (Opus; the plan's ledger, `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-12-plan-11g-gut-satiety/progress.md`, the line opening "Task 6 review") found that G_STEEP 0.019723 flattens G to a near-constant offset: the fasting rise about 4× slower than the P model it replaced, an evening request flipping about 10 h on a 2 % hunger factor, and Karl's troughs not deepening; the cause was the model form (the stomach's energy-limited emptying already scales with meal size, and G fed at emptying counted it again), not the loss weighting. Angus ruled "fix the model form" (2026-10-12, the same ledger) and Task 6c replaced G with the meal pool M fed at the eat (`bc92b13f`). Its constants shipped only in `5c95f28a` and were replaced in `bc92b13f`; cite it only as the record of that fit (its `chosen.oracles` hold the 13 named non-reproductions the Task 6 report lists, `callahan_825` 360 and `meal650` 349 among them).
