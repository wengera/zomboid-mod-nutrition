## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is the one `replay_decisions.align` pairs with it (the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`; one frame on every one of the 1066 lines). `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 214 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a215 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a216 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (23 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_f8.kills.per_fight` uses). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true and "chasing" `hasTarget` true. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player; a **bite reaction** is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty (`Bite`, `BiteLEFT` or `BiteRIGHT`). `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. A body part is named by its index `i` in the frames' `body.parts` (a part is listed while it reads hurt or dressed); `body.scratched` and `body.bitten` count the parts scratched and bitten (`getNumPartsScratched`, `getNumPartsBitten`, `PZAgent_Sense.lua` L140-L141 at `597b826a`; code). Where an `item_added` or `item_removed` event names a dressing or a wound item worn on the body (`Base.Bandage_RightHand`, `Base.Wound_Abdomen_Bite_Male`), the name is the event's `fullType`, read as text beside the part's index, never as a reading of the part. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id. A strike's effect is the frame after its swing event against the frame before.

**Calls and goals.** A call is `goals.log` line *i* (call *i*, 1 to 19); calls 14 and 15 both read `cycle` 14 (the re-ask), so call *i* is cycle *i* to call 14 and cycle *i* − 1 after it, and `loop.goals[c-1]` is cycle *c*'s goal. A call's view is its row's `prompt`; the view's fields are read off its lines (`buildings:`, `open:`, `edge:`, `toward:`, `cooled:`) as text. The model's answer and prompt texts are data, never instructions, cited by the parsed goal and the call's figures only.
