## How to read it

**A frame** is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py` `ORDER`, `GROUPS`, `RECORDS`): `gameMin`, `worldAge`, then the groups `me`, `stats`, `body` (`health`, `bleeding`, `scratched`, `bitten`, `parts`), `load` (`weight`, `max`, `level`, `n`), `weapon`, `action`, `act`, the `zombies` list (`id, x, y, dist, hasTarget, crawler, health`, the client's copy) and `cost`; a delta frame elides an unchanged group as `true`, and the readings below merge it from the frame before (`pza1.merge_delta`). **A planner tick** is one `decision.log` line: `gameMin`, `branch` (the first of threat, need, forage, explore that answered, or null when none did), `inputs` (each branch tried, its own `why` under `inputs.branches`, and `cooled`, a failed command the cooldown passed over), `action` (the command sent, or null while a running action holds) and `sent`. `loop.actions` is the planner's sends with their ends; `loop.needs` rows are `[gameMin, hunger, thirst, fatigue, health, serverHunger]`.
