## How to read it

**A frame's zombie** (the schema's `zombie` record `id, x, y, dist, hasTarget, crawler, health`) is every live zombie of the client's cell within `S.ZOMBIE_RADIUS` 12 tiles, nearest first, its `id` `getOnlineID`; `me.zombiesSeen` is the cell list's size and `me.zombiesListed` the frame's zombie records (`PZAgent_Sense.lua` L67, L234–L250 at `e3e68793`).

**The pin and `unpinned`** (`x5_combat_2.py` L772, L828–L834; `policies.py` L214–L372 at `dcd74415`). The encounter's `Fight` is built once a frame lists the spawn, `target=` its id, with no `unreachable` map passed in, so each encounter's `Fight` starts with none. The `Fight` keeps the pin until the target is dead or gone from the World, unreachable or beyond `DISENGAGE_R` 10; dropped, the same `choose` pins the nearest zombie not marked and returns that one's command. The driver's end check reads `fight.target != zid` on every loop pass and ends the encounter `unpinned`, then stops a running action (`stopped_aid`).

**The line check and the reposition** (ruling P23-K; `policies.py` L195–L203, L291–L305, L350–L372 at `dcd74415`; `world.py` L346–L419). Within `FIGHT_REACH` 2.0 the `Fight` reads `world.line_clear` from the player to the target over the kept map's `blockN`/`blockW` edges (a square stores its own north and west edges); beyond reach the line is not read and the `Fight` walks to the target's square floored (`WalkTo(int(x), int(y))`, the approach walk). On a blocked line it walks once at a run to the first square of `world.reachable_adjacent(target)` within `REPOSITION_R` 2.5 tiles of the player, by its centre, whose centre has a clear line to the target (`repositioned`); a second blocked read, or no such square, marks the target unreachable (`UNREACHABLE_S` 20) and drops the pin. The frames carry no `why`: which branch each command came from is read here by replaying `state.log` through a `World` (`World.apply`, line by line) and the boot's `line_to`, `reachable_adjacent` and reposition rule, against each act's own target in its `args`.

**The `walkTo` skill's end** (`PZAgent_Skills.lua` L259, L269, L287–L291 at `e3e68793`): `done` once the player is within `DONE_R` 1.0 tile of the point (x, y) the command names, read on the skill's first tick, with `result.d` that distance. A `WalkTo(int(x), int(y))` names a square's north-west corner, not its centre.

**The poll and the pair** (ruling P23-S; `x5_combat_2.py` docstring L7–L23, L505, L862–L900). From the spawn's confirmation the worker reads the encounter's zombie with `zombie.get` every `ZOMBIE_POLL_S` 0.5 s, one bus step at a time; a read that takes longer is followed by the next at once. A swing's `server_before` is the newest read at its send (no read is taken for the send, so `pre_read_ms` is 0); its `server_after` is the first read **sent** after its end frame (the first frame after its end act), a read in flight across that frame refused, and the driver waits for it, pumping frames and **not ticking the `Fight`**, until the end frame's `wallMs` plus `PAIR_AFTER_MAX_S` 2.0 s; a read not done by then voids the swing. `poll_pair` records `frameSeq`, `frameWallMs`, `sentMs`, `readWallMs`, `dtMs` (the read's `epoch_ms_after` less `frameWallMs`), `void`, `why`. `pair_after` sets `server_after` against the frame after the end act nearest its midpoint, as X5's did, and `measure_x5`'s agreement reads `pair_after`.

**The listing record** (`readings.encounters.rows[*].listing`): `firstListedSeq`, `waitMs` (the driver's wait after the `zombie.near` reply until it read a frame listing the new id), `ackMs` (that reply's `epoch_ms_after`) and `frames` (each frame's `seq`, `wallMs`, `zombiesSeen`, `zombiesListed` from the spawn step's start to the first listing). A reply's `epoch_ms_*` is the driver's clock and a frame's `wallMs` the client's, both epoch milliseconds on this one host.

**The kept map's squares** below are a `World` fed every `state.log` line (`agent.base.world.World.apply`), the last record of a square winning; each square named below was sent once, by the watch's first sight, and never again.
