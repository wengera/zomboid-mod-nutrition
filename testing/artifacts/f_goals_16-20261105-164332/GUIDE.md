## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; **the frame at a line** is the last frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall` × 1000 (§ 0). `inputs.threats` is the planner's own count of the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, fallen ones included; **standing within 6 tiles** below is this writer's count off the frame at the line: a listed zombie with a position, `onFloor` not true, at 6.0 tiles or less by its x and y from the character's. The threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat` (its `flee` the flee's reason), the goal branch's at `inputs.branches.goal`, the care branch's at `inputs.branches.care`; `inputs.sneak` {on, near} is P30-D's want. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 268 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a269 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a270 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (27 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_f12.kills.per_fight` and `hits.by_fight` use; fight 26 has no end, the death coming inside it). A call is `goals.log` line *i*. A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`, `known`, `state`, `behind`}; "chasing" is `hasTarget` true and "idle" false; `known` is `isTargetLocationKnown()` and `state` `getRealState()`, the owner's state name the zombie packet carries (`PZAgent_Sense.lua`'s PZA/1.9 block, code). `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south); a zombie's **bearing offset** is the angle from that compass point to the line from the character to it on the same frame, so its resolution is 45° (`getDir()`, #4591). `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; the **union** (Plan 30) joins those runs with the reflex's own `hit` steps; a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding` on a frame. `body.armPain` is the arms' summed pain (#4370's sum); the melee's **pain** is the `melee` {mode, pain} log on its send line. A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event. Part *i* is `body.parts[*].i`; part 2 is the left forearm by the two `Base.Bandage_LeftLowerArm` items its dressing added (seq 2027–2028); the names of parts 0, 1, 5 and 8 (`Hand_L`, `Hand_R`, `UpperArm_R`, `Head` in the engine's body-part order) are this writer's inference.

**A melee's steps** (the reflex's header in `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua` at `e43e1330`, code not readings): `stand`, `face` (a tick before the press), `swing`, `back` (the back leg after a miss, rule 7), `shove`, `knock_wait`, `floor` (the floor swing at a fallen target), `stomp_walk`, and `hit`, the step the reflex takes on any tick its own read of the player's hit reaction is non-empty (rule 2), read at the client's frame rate. A **press** is a progress line entering the `swing` step (its `swings` count rising); its **distance** is that line's `d`; what it **did** is the melee's next line of another step against the press line's `hp` (lowered: below it; a knock-down: `knockdowns` above it; nothing: equal). The **collision span** is the frames 0.15625 to 0.3125 s after the press line's `wallMs`: a quarter of the bat's clip at the two bounds of its `CombatSpeed` (#4668), the span in which the swing's damage is applied if it lands (#4665); the frames step 0.1 s, so a press has one or two frames in it.
