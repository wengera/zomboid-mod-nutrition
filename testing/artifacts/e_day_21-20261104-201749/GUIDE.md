## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is `agent.base.replay_decisions.align`'s, the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour`: one frame on every one of the 847 lines, the same frame (seq 7066, the log's last) on each of lines 752–847. `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6 and `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 203 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a204 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a205 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (12 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e18.kills.per_fight` uses; each is one contiguous run of `threat` lines here). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true, "standing" `onFloor` null, "chasing" `hasTarget` true and "idle" `hasTarget` false. `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player; a **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty, and a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding`. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id. **A strike's effect** is the target's health on the frame after its act's end against the frame before its swing event: the client's copy of a zombie's health changed three or more lines after the swing event, never on the frame right after it, so the frame after the swing event reads the old health on every strike here. A strike's **distance** is the target's `dist` on its decision line's frame unless named as the frame after the swing event.
