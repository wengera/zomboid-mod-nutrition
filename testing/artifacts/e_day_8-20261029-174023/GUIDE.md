## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is the map lines up to the seq named; a **building square** is a square record whose `building` is not null (169 in the session, of buildings 2, 3 and 5, the last new building square at seq 1656, building 5 first read at seq 1342), and a distance to a building is to the nearest such square's centre. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or a `decision.log` `wall` against a `wallMs`) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. An act's frames are those between its `accepted` line and its end line. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 429 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a430 in turn (each type checked). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k*; `loop.goals` holds the goal runner's 299 rows. A part is a frame's `body.parts` entry by its index `i`.
