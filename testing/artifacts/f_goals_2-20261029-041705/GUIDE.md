## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py` at `407f739c`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo`, and PZA/1.4's `hopN` and `hopW` its own north and west edges' `HoppableN` and `HoppableW` flags (#4288). A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall`, or a call's `wall`, less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (`DISENGAGE_R` 10 while a threat policy runs, else 6), `inputs.nearest` the nearest held zombie's distance. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. `loop.actions` is the planner's 364 sends a2–a365 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's 22 rows; `loop.fights` the 41 runs of the threat branch, cited as fight *k* for `loop.fights[k-1]`; `loop.llm.calls` the LLM source's 22 collected calls, cited as call *i* by its index (0–21), the same call being `goals.log`'s line *i* + 1. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read when it marked the shot and `decision_line` the decision log's line count then; the grab itself comes later, on the worker (§ 8).
