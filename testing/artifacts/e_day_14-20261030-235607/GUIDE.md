## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest listed, both read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 105 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a106 in turn (each type and its args checked; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a107 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (9 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e12.kills.per_fight` uses). `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player (PZA/1.4; the drag-down's own count, #4290), `me.hitReaction` the player's hit reaction and `me.dragDown` the player's `isDeathDragDown()` (the schema's notes, `PZAgent_Schema.lua` at `eff3bdce`). A square's `building` and `room` are the kept map's square record (the last line naming it); a zombie's square is its `x` and `y` floored. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id; the death event's `zombiesKilled` reads the same count.
