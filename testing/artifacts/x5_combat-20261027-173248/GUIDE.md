## How to read it

**A frame's zombie** (`state.log` frame field 14, the schema's `zombie` record `id, x, y, dist, hasTarget, crawler, health`) is every live zombie of the client's cell within `S.ZOMBIE_RADIUS` 12 tiles, nearest first, its `id` `getOnlineID`, its `health` `getHealth` on the client's copy (`PZAgent_Sense.lua` L69–L92 at `c8493a0e`). A delta frame elides an unchanged group as `true`; the readings below merge it from the frame before.

**The attack skill** (`PZAgent_Skills.lua` L517–L636 at `c8493a0e`): within the held weapon's reach it calls `faceThisObject` and `DoAttack(0)` once (beyond it one approach walk at a run first, `APPROACH_MS` 4000), then reads the player's `isAttackStarted` and `getMeleeDelay` every tick; it ends `done` when `isAttackStarted` has read true and then false, when the target reads dead, or `SWING_MS` 1500 after the swing. Its result: `attackStarted` (true ever read), `meleeDelay` (the largest `getMeleeDelay` read, 0 when none), `swings`, `targetDead`. The Sense `swing` event (`PZAgent_Sense.lua` L380–L389) fires on `isAttackStarted`'s rising edge and carries the `getMeleeDelay` read on that tick.

**A swing's pairs** (`readings.encounters.rows[*].swings[*]`): `server_before` and `server_after` are the `zombie.get` replies taken right before the send and right after the end act is read; `pair_after` sets `server_after` against the frame after the end act whose wall time is nearest the reply's midpoint (`dt_ms` apart), void beyond `PAIR_SLACK_S` 1.0 s, when no frame lists the target, when the server read no health, or when a fence act is missing. `measure_x5`'s agreement reads `pair_after` alone. A reply's `wallMs` is the driver's clock (the step's midpoint) and a frame's `wallMs` the client's, both epoch milliseconds on this one host.
