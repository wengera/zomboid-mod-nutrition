How to read it. Inside `diff.fields` the field names are literal keys with dots in them (`satiety.nicH`, `body.energyState`), and so are `drinking_sodium.by_day.<d>.fluids.na_end` and the like: a pointer names them as written. `rows.json` holds `row12` and `drinking_sodium`; `summary.json` the verdicts. Chain day d runs from age 24d − 7 to 24d + 17. The design departures are x321's guide's; the oracle's study agreement is not re-tested live (ruling 8-R: live against the replay shows the mechanics hold in the live game, and a live match to a study is by construction wherever the constant was fitted to that study).
