How to read it. **The seams.** Every ghost run, under `mod` and `budget15` alike, runs the writer dry (`W.dry`) and skips the store step for ghost names (`S.skip = H0.isGhost`), both set inside the harness's `H0.work`, which every scheduler calls (Plan 11a ruling 15); `summaries.<arm>.seam` reads the store's writes from `q_start` to `q_end` against the two real players' own saves (at most one a real minute each plus the index): 2 to 4 in every arm here, so no arm is void. **The batching differs:** `budget15` times its ghost runs in one batch a tick; `mod` runs them one at a time from the mod's own `P.runOne` and keeps one batch open across a drain's contiguous ghost runs (`H0.modRun`, `H0.modWrap`), paying a second when a real player's run or a task falls between ghosts, a possible small bias against `mod` (at most one extra swap and two clock reads per real player a tick); `summaries.<arm>.batching` sizes it (about 4.1 frames with ghost runs a minute event in both arms).
