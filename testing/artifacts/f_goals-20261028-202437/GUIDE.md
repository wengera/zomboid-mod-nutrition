## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py` at `4a86c05c`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo` (#4220, #4222). A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall`, or a call's `wall`, less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (`DISENGAGE_R` 10 while a threat policy runs, else 6), `inputs.nearest` the nearest held zombie's distance. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. `loop.actions` is the planner's 89 sends a2–a90 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's 14 rows; `loop.fights` the eight runs of the threat branch; `loop.llm.calls` the LLM source's 14 collected calls, cited as call *i* by its index (0–13), the same call being `goals.log`'s line *i* + 1. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read when it marked the shot and `decision_line` the decision log's line count then; the grab itself comes later, on the worker (§ 8).

**The threat rules** at the boot are the fifth chain's, at the same commits (`planner/survive.py` at `c77f69e3` and `planner/policies.py` at `4a86c05c`): `testing/artifacts/e_day_5-20261028-201255/GUIDE.md`'s "How to read it" states them (the fight's walk, line check, reposition and unreachable mark; `many`, the sprint steer and its probe; the stuck leg; the shelter pick; the corner and its kinds; survival iteration 4's group steer, retry, leg cut and point-blank re-pin), and they are read here as code, not readings. Two of them bear on this session beyond the fifth chain's: the `pair` flee (two or more within `DANGER_R` and health under `FLEE_HEALTH_PAIR` 60 or any wound count above 0), and the corner's precedence: a cornered Fight runs on the nearest reachable held threat whatever the flee reason, `health` (under `FLEE_HEALTH` 40) and `pair` included, until that zombie is dead, gone or marked, or the nearest held threat is beyond `CORNERED_R` 1.5 (a `walk` corner's end) or a group forms (`GROUP_HOLD_N` 4 held or `GROUP_R_N` 3 within `GROUP_NEAR_R` 3.0, ending a `walk` corner); `flip` names a flee kept because its own walk or leg, sent under `FLIP_MIN_S` 5 s ago, still runs while the Fight would walk (`_kept_flee`, survival iteration 3's rule 1).

**The LLM source** (`testing/agent/base/planner/llm.py` at `cb7d45f7`, its docstring; code, not readings). The goal branch asks its source for a goal only when none runs. `LLMSource.propose(view)` never waits: with no call pending it starts one worker thread running `DEFAULT_CLI` (`claude -p --output-format json --model sonnet --system-prompt SYSTEM --tools "" --setting-sources "" --strict-mcp-config --disable-slash-commands --no-session-persistence`, under Angus's own login, in an empty temporary working directory, `CLAUDECODE` removed from its environment) with the rendered view on stdin, and returns None, so the goal runner holds (`pending`, the decision log's `goal` `{"pending": "llm"}`) and asks again on the next tick with a fresh view. When the worker has ended within `LLM_TIMEOUT_S` 20 s, the CLI's JSON envelope's `result` text is parsed (`first_object`, the first balanced `{...}` decoding as a JSON object), checked by `goals.validate` against `schema/goals.json`, and then, at collection, against the view of the ask that collects it (`place_error`: a `LootBuilding` or `Shelter` naming a building that view does not list, or an `ExploreToward` whose square's centre lies farther than `EXPLORE_MAX_TILES` 60 from that view's `pos`, is `invalid: unknown place`). A valid goal is returned; any failure is recorded and the scripted source's goal returned for that ask (`_stand_in`). Each collected call appends one row to `loop.llm.calls` (`latency_s` from the call's start to its answer, `ok`, the validated `goal`, `error`, `prompt_bytes` the bytes of the system prompt and the stdin prompt together, `model`) and one line to `goals.log`. `SYSTEM`, the system prompt, is rendered once from `goals.json`: the game, the answer contract, one line per goal type with its fields, how a building line reads (`id dist containers/looted/doors flags`), that a building is named only by a listed id and an `ExploreToward` square within 60 tiles of `pos`, and that the user message is the view; it is 1486 bytes (`len(SYSTEM.encode("utf-8"))` at `d2641e2a`). The view (`planner/view.py` at `e07a52cb`) renders one `key: value` line per key, under 2048 bytes: `pos`, `hour`, `health`, `needs`, `needs_wanted`, `load`, `weapon`, `inventory_by_use`, `buildings` (the ten nearest known, each `id dist containers/looted/doors` and `v`, `s` or `-`; `looted` counts the containers a loot could take nothing more from, holding no item of an open use), `zombies_near` (counts within 6, 12 and 25 tiles; no position), `last_goal` (the runner's last row with its `state` and `reason`), `events`, `want_shelter`, `edge` (the scripted source's unexplored edge), `scanned_here`, `stock_uses` and `cooled` (goals that failed having sent nothing, refused for `GOAL_COOL_S` 30 s). The scripted source's order (`goals.py` at `e07a52cb`): `Stock` on a wanted need, `Shelter` when the night wants one, `LootBuilding` the nearest building with a container not looted, `ExploreToward` the view's `edge`, else a scan or a `Rest`, a cooled goal passed over.
