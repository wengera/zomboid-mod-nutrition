## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py` at `4a86c05c`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo` (#4220, #4222). A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall` less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (`DISENGAGE_R` 10 while a threat policy runs, else 6), `inputs.nearest` the nearest held zombie's distance. The why's `flee` field names the rule the branch answered (`unreachable`, `held`, `many`, `unarmed`), `fight` true a fight, `cornered` the zombie a corner runs on, `group` the group read that steered (`hold` or `near`), and `shelter`, `step`, `turn`, `probe`, `stuck`, `ahead`, `ended` and `cut` are the flee's own fields; all are read as values here. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`; the counts "within 1.5 tiles" below are read off that frame's zombie list. `loop.actions` is the planner's 33 sends a2–a34 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's 9 rows; `loop.fights` the six runs of the threat branch. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame the loop had read when it marked the shot and `decision_line` the decision log's line count then; the grab itself comes later, on the worker (§ 6).

The threat rules at the boot (`planner/survive.py` at `c77f69e3` and `planner/policies.py` at `4a86c05c`, their docstrings; code, not readings), as they bear on this session. A fight runs with at most `MAX_FIGHT` 2 held; it walks to a target beyond `FIGHT_REACH` 2.0, reads the map's line to it within reach, repositions once (`REPOSITION_R` 2.5, no nearer than `REPOSITION_MIN` 1.2) when the line reads blocked and marks the target unreachable (`marked`) on a second blocked read, and a marked zombie is skipped for `UNREACHABLE_S` 20 wall seconds; with every held threat marked the branch flees `unreachable`. `many` counts the hold radius's list while a fight runs and the `DANGER_R` count otherwise; a `many` flee beyond `SPRINT_R` 2.0 steers 3 s legs at a sprint while the frame's endurance reads above `SPRINT_END` 0.5, each leg along the away vector from the held threats' centroid turned by `STEER_TURNS` to the first heading whose map line reads clear `AHEAD_R` 9 tiles (`probe` 9.0) or else `STEER_PROBE` 3 tiles; a leg is **stuck** when it ended having moved under `STUCK_MOVE` 0.5 tiles, or while it runs having left the player within 0.5 tiles for `STEP_STALL_S` 1.0 s with a held zombie within `CORNERED_R` 1.5 (R1). The flee's shelter pick (`Flee._pick`) takes a known building's exterior door, read by its own edge, unlocked and unbarricaded, whose approach square lies within `SHELTER_FLEE_R` 25 tiles, no held threat nearer that square than the player is, no known zombie in its corridor and none on the building's squares; its first step walks to the approach square. **The corner** (`_cornered`): armed, with the nearest held threat within 1.5 tiles, it fights the nearest zombie in reach, a `stuck` corner after a stuck leg and a `walk` corner on any other threat tick whose line to the zombie is not read blocked. **Survival iteration 4** (P23-AP): a group, `GROUP_HOLD_N` 4 held or `GROUP_R_N` 3 within `GROUP_NEAR_R` 3.0 tiles, ends a `walk` or `inside` corner and blocks a new one while the last leg is not stuck (the group steer, logged `group`); a `stuck` corner keeps swinging against the group, and with the nearest beyond `POINT_BLANK_R` 1.0 and its stuck read `STUCK_RETRY_S` 2 s old it tries one leg (`group` `retry`); a running steer leg is cut and re-aimed when a held threat comes ahead of it (logged `cut`); the corner follows its Fight's re-pin to the nearest zombie within point blank (fix round 1, R4); and the goal source, after two unmoved stuck goal walks from one square, proposes only a square in the open (`world.unexplored_edge`). A cornered Fight swings at a target within `POINT_BLANK_R` whatever its line reads. `unarmed` (the frame's weapon group null) flees before every other reason.
