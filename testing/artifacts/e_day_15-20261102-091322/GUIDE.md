## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest listed, both read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 357 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a358 in turn (each type and its args checked; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a359 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (52 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e12.kills.per_fight` uses). A fight line is a threat line whose why reads `fight` true, its `target` the pinned zombie; the why's `dist` is that target's distance when the line was chosen, `line` the Fight's line read to it (null beyond `FIGHT_REACH` 2.0, where it is not read), `reposition` the square a blocked line sends a walk to, and `marked` the id the line marked unreachable (`Fight._choose`, `planner/fight.py` L757–L772 at `dfa36e5a`: within reach a blocked line sends one reposition walk at a run, and a second blocked read, or no clear square, marks the target unreachable for `UNREACHABLE_S` 20 wall seconds, `planner/policies.py` L86 at `f70e9392`; code). With every held threat so marked the branch flees `unreachable` (`Threat._unpaired`, `planner/threat.py` L270–L271 at `79e8fea3`; code), and `flip` keeps a running flee walk or steer under `FLIP_MIN_S` 5 s while the fight would walk (L529; code). `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player (PZA/1.4; the schema's note at `eff3bdce`). A square's `building`, `room`, `door` and edge fields are the kept map's square record (the last line naming it); `blockN` reads a step to the north neighbour blocked, `edgeN` a wall, door or window on the north edge, `hopN` that edge hoppable, a low fence (the schema's notes; code); a zombie's square is its `x` and `y` floored. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id. Iteration 15's and iteration 14's rules are described in E14's guide (`testing/artifacts/e_day_14-20261030-235607/GUIDE.md`, How to read it), on the same files at the same commits.
