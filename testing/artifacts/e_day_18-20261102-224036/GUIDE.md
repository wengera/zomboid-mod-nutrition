## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of the state logs in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before; a seq cited from 16231 on is a line of `state.1.log`. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` the zombies the threat branch holds, `inputs.nearest` the nearest listed, each read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour` (`agent.base.replay_decisions.align`: one frame on every line here). A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 357 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a358 in turn (each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a359 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (40 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e15.kills.per_fight` uses). `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player (PZA/1.4), `me.hitReaction` the player's hit reaction, `me.dragDown` the player's `isDeathDragDown()`, and the frame's `action` group {`class`, `n`} the class of the head of the game's timed-action queue and the queue's length. A body part is named by its index `i` in the frame's `body.parts` and by the wound item the game added for it (`item_added` `Base.Wound_Neck_Bite_Male` for part 9, `Base.Wound_LUArm_Bite_Male` for part 4, `Base.Wound_Abdomen_Laceration_Male` for part 7). A square's `building`, `room`, `door`, `window` and edge fields are the kept map's square record (the last map line naming it before the reading cited); a zombie's square is its `x` and `y` floored. A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id.
