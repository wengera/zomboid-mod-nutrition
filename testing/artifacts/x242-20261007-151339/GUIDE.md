How to read it. Every figure is a whole-millisecond total off `getTimestampMs` over its runs (`meta.timer_note`); a sub-block well under 1 ms reads 0 or 1 a run, so only its total over many runs is a cost. The accumulators are `phases.<A|B>.acc.<u:admin|u:bob>.text`, `"<set>|<key>=<ms>/<runs>"` joined by `;`: the nine steps, `__run` (`NutritionRevamp.server.minute.run`) and `__work` (`P.work`), and the sub-blocks `nutrients/…`, `metabolism/…`, `effects/…`, `strength/…` whose sites are the instrument files' `SBA("…")` lines. `phases.<A|B>.ring.rows` is one row per real player's run: user, ms, the bench minute counter, and the Metabolism, Nutrients and Effects day closes inside the run. `phases.<A|B>.hist.text` is each player's run-ms histogram, buckets 0..30.
