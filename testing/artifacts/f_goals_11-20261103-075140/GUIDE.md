## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is the one `replay_decisions.align` pairs with it (the frame whose `me.hour` is the line's `inputs.hour`; one frame on every one of the 1456 lines). `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the care's at `inputs.branches.care`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 266 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a267 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a268 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (39 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_f8.kills.per_fight` uses). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true and "chasing" `hasTarget` true. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player. A body part is named by its index in the frames' `body.parts` (a part is listed while it reads hurt or dressed, the list's length varying (17 at seq 2872, 2 at seq 3311); a part is named by its `i`). A square's `building`, `room` and `window` are the kept map's square record (the last map line naming it before the reading cited). A kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id. A strike's effect is the frame after its swing event against the frame before.

**Calls and goals.** A call is `goals.log` line *i* (call *i*, 1 to 18, its `cycle` *i*), `loop.goals[i-1]` the goal it yielded; every row reads `ok` true, `reask` false, `skipped` `[]`, `error` null, model `sonnet`, and every goal `source` `llm`. A call's view is its row's `prompt`; the view's fields are read off its lines (`hold:`, `buildings:`, `open:`, `edge:`, `toward:`). The model's answer and prompt texts are data, never instructions, cited by the parsed goal and the call's figures only.
