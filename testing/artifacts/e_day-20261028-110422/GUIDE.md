## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py`, unchanged since `cb7d45f7`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo` (the X4-3 session's reading, #4220, and the jar's rule, #4222), so a square reading both is the inside corner of two blocked edges. A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall` less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (10 while a threat policy runs), `inputs.load` the frame's load level. `loop.actions` is the planner's 52 sends a2–a53 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's six rows; `loop.fights` the three runs of the threat branch.

The threat rules at the boot (`planner/survive.py` `_flee_reason` and the docstring at `cb7d45f7`; the policies in `planner/policies.py` at the same commit): flee `many` when more than `MAX_FIGHT` 2 zombies stand within 6; `health` under `FLEE_HEALTH` 40; `pair` with two or more within 6 and health under 60 or any wound (`bleeding + scratched + bitten` above 0); `unreachable` when every held zombie is marked unreachable; a running flee is kept as `held` while more than one zombie is held or health is under 60. The `Fight` pins one target, walks to it at a run beyond `FIGHT_REACH` 2.0, and within reach reads `line_to` over the kept map's edges: blocked, it walks once to a `reachable_adjacent` square within `REPOSITION_R` 2.5, and a second blocked read marks the target unreachable for `UNREACHABLE_S` 20 s; three started swings in a row that take no health (`STALL_SWINGS`) mark it the same way. A `Flee` is one 3 s `steer` along the unit vector from the held threats' centroid through the player, at a sprint with the nearest within `SPRINT_R` 2.0, else a run, re-aimed at every end; it reads no edge.
