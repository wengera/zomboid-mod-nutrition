## How to read it

A frame is `state.log`'s positional line in the schema's `frame` order (`testing/agent/base/pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. The kept map is `World.apply` (`testing/agent/base/world.py` at `607dca06`) over every line up to the seq named; a square record's `blockN` and `blockW` are its own north and west edges' `isBlockedTo` (#4220, #4222). A line reading is this write-up's replay of `policies.line_to` at `145868e2` on the World as the planner held it at a tick (every line whose `wallMs` is at or before the tick's `wall`). A **loop second** is a frame's `wallMs` less `loop.t_loop0_ms`, over 1000, or a `decision.log` line's `wall` less the same base: a difference on this host's one epoch clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n* (`gameMin`, `branch`, `inputs` with each branch's `why` under `inputs.branches`, `action`, `sent`, `goal`); `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.held` within the hold radius (`DISENGAGE_R` 10 while a threat policy runs, else 6), `inputs.nearest` the nearest held zombie's distance. The why's `flee` field names the rule the branch answered (`held_inside`, `many`, `held`, `pair`, `unreachable`), `fight` true a fight, `cornered` the zombie a corner runs on, and `shelter`, `step`, `ended`, `turn`, `probe`, `stuck` and `ahead` are the flee's own fields; all are read as values here. `loop.actions` is the planner's 40 sends a2–a41 with their ends; `loop.goals` the goal runner's eleven rows; `loop.fights` the four runs of the threat branch. A shot is `shots/shots.jsonl`'s line *n*, cited as shot *n*: `seq` the latest frame at the grab and `decision_line` the decision log's line count then.

The threat rules at the boot (`planner/survive.py` and `planner/policies.py` docstrings at `145868e2`; code, not readings): `_threat_plan` makes the branch's one decision in this order: the door fight (only for a chosen shelter whose close ended inside); **the corner** (armed, the nearest held zombie within `CORNERED_R` 1.5 and either the flee's last steer leg ended having moved under `STUCK_MOVE` 0.5 tiles or the character on a room square: a `Fight` on the nearest zombie in reach, whatever the count); **the inside hold** (the character on a room square, every held zombie's `line_to` `False`, no shelter or exit step running, no breach); **the breach** (a hold breaks with a held zombie within `HOLD_BREAK_R` 3.0, or the nearest closing on `CLOSING_TICKS` 3 ticks in a row within 6); else Plan 21b's hysteresis (`_flee_reason`): flee `many` when more than `MAX_FIGHT` 2 zombies are counted, the count being the held list (within 10) while a fight runs and the `DANGER_R` count otherwise; `health` under `FLEE_HEALTH` 40; `pair` with two or more within 6 and health under 60 or any wound; `unreachable` when no held zombie is a reachable target; a running flee is kept as `held` while more than one zombie is held or health is under 60, and with one held at health 60 or over it reads none, so the branch fights. Each change of policy makes a new `Fight` or a new `Flee`. The `Fight` walks at a run to the centre of its pinned target's square beyond `FIGHT_REACH` 2.0; within reach a blocked line sends one walk to the centre of a `reachable_adjacent` square within `REPOSITION_R` 2.5 and no nearer than `REPOSITION_MIN` 1.2 (P23-AJ 5), and a second blocked read marks the target unreachable for `UNREACHABLE_S` 20 s. A `Flee` off a room square first picks a shelter (`_pick`: of the known buildings with no zombie on their squares, the exterior door the map reads neither locked nor barricaded whose approach square lies within `SHELTER_FLEE_R` 25, no nearer any held zombie than the player, with none in its corridor) and runs `night.shelter_plan`'s steps at a run, `approach` (a walk to the centre of the door's approach square), `open` (skipped when the door reads open), `enter` (a walk to the centre of the building's room square nearest the door) and `close`; a step that ends `done` advances, a superseded one is sent again, and one that fails for a reason in `SHUN_REASONS` (`stuck` among them) moves to the next door or, with none, shuns the building and drops the plan; with no plan, it steers 3 s legs along the away vector weighted by 1/distance, turned by `STEER_TURNS` (0, ±30, ±60, ±90 degrees) to the first heading whose map line reads clear `AHEAD_R` 9 and `STEER_PROBE` 3 tiles ahead with no held zombie ahead (`probe` 9.0), a sprint with the nearest within `SPRINT_R` 2.0. On a room square the Flee walks out through an opening (`exit_plan`), else adopts the building, else holds (`trapped`), and never steers. `night.exterior_doors` counts a door square as exterior when one of its four orthogonal neighbours is a mapped, non-solid square with no room; it reads no edge between them.
