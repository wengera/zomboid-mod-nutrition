## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6, `inputs.nearest` the nearest listed, both read by the planner off its own frame on that tick. A tick's frame is the latest frame whose `wallMs` is at or before the line's `wall`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names; on such a line the why's other fields are the running action's, written at its send. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 344 `decision.log` lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a345 in turn (each type checked; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a346 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (34 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_e11.kills.per_fight` uses); a **fight-line run** is a run of consecutive threat lines whose why reads `fight` true. A part is a frame's `body.parts` entry by its index `i`; `infected` is the part's `isInfectedWound()` (the schema's note, `PZAgent_Schema.lua` at `eff3bdce`). `body.armPain` is the summed `getPain()` of the six arm parts and `body.strain` each arm's largest `getStiffness()` (the schema's notes; code). A zombie's `health` and `onFloor` are the frame's listing of it; a kill is read two ways that agree here, the act line's `result.targetDead` and the `zombie_dead` event naming the id (no death event came).
