## How to read it

The schedule is `b_actions.py`'s module docstring: `hello`; `spawn` (5 `Base.WaterBottle` and 5 `Base.Apple` by RCON `additem`, `readings.spawned[*].confirmed` true, so every eat and drink had an item when loot found none); then five repetitions `rep1`–`rep5` (`phase_walls`: rep1 63.696–80.541 s, rep2 to 98.142, rep3 to 116.388, rep4 to 135.14, rep5 to 170.332). A repetition is `scan 15` → the pick → `open` (only when a door or window lies on the line to the pick) → `goto` beside the pick → `loot x y food` → `loot x y water` → the eat reset → `eat <fullType>` → `drink` → `goto` back to the spawn (`readings.spawn_xy` 10997.5, 9699.5).

- **A row:** `readings.reps[i]` holds `rep`, `scan[*]` (`r`, `scanId`, `parts`, `partsSeen`, `squares`, `scanMs`, `scanTicks`, `missing`), `pick` (`x`, `y`, `z`, `dist`, `tier`, `items`), `door`, `steps[*]`, `eat_pick` (`fullType`, `from`, `added`), `server_eat` (`before`, `after[]`, `moved`, `resets`, `client_before`, `client_after`), `notes`, `completed`.
- **A step:** `steps[*]` `goal`, `args`, `seq` (the command's cmdSeq), `state` (done | failed | refused | timeout | skipped), `detail` (the Lua's own detail for done and failed), `ack` (the client's ack line), `ms` (this process's wall ms from the send to the driver reading the end, at `POLL_S` 0.1), `endSeq`/`endWallMs` (the first frame carrying the end), `gameMin` and `worldAge` (that frame's game clock). Beside `ms` this guide gives the client-clock span `endWallMs − ack.wallMs`, both stamped by `PZA.now()`, and the step's game time as the `worldAge` change from the last frame before the ack (read off `state.log`) to `steps[*].worldAge`, in game minutes; `worldAge` is read as hours (inference: over the run `gameMin` 562 → 591 while `worldAge` 2.3820 → 2.8508, 28.1 game minutes).
- **The pick:** `pick_container` takes the non-empty-`containers` square of the scan not picked by an earlier repetition with the best `tier` (one point for any item, one for a fullType matching `world.FOOD_RX`, one for `world.WATER_RX`), nearest the player within the tier; `beside` takes the pick's 8-neighbour whose `solid` is not true, nearest the player.
- **The grade:** `grades.gate_b` (`testing/agent/grades.py` lines 195–231): `completed` counts rows whose `completed` is true (every step done), pass at `COMPLETE_MIN` 4; `reasons_in_set` checks every `failed` step's `detail` against `agent/reasons.py`'s `REASONS`; `eats_seen_by_server` counts rows with an `after` reading whose hunger is below or calories above `before`, re-derived from the readings and not the driver's `moved`, pass at `SEQUENCES` 5.

### 7. The NO-GO: the failing gate and its causes

The failing gate is `completed`; the failing goals are `loot` (9 of 10 failed) and one `goto`. The goal state machines reported what happened: no failure is a Lua fault, every detail is in the closed set, and the eats and drinks ran 10 of 10. The two causes, checked against the files:

**Cause 1 — the fixture and the pick (`loot … no match`, repetitions 1–4).** The containers the pick reached held one Food item (the oatmeal) and no item with a fluid container. The loot's filters are the item's class (`food`: `isA(it, "Food")`, `PZAgent_Act.lua` line 347) and a fluid container (`water`: `getFluidContainer` non-nil, line 349); the picks were (10998, 9698), (10997, 9696), (10998, 9696), (10996, 9696) — the oatmeal's square, then shelves, a counter with a dish cloth and a dresser — chosen nearest-first because the pick's regex tier matched nothing. The controller's reading needs one correction: the house within r=15 does hold water-filter matches, `Base.DrinkingGlass` at (10999, 9696) (repetition 5's pick, never reached; cause 2) and `Base.Bleach` ×2 at (11000, 9701) (never picked), and a second Food-class item, `Base.Dogfood` at (11000, 9696) (never picked; inedible, `CantEat`). So "one food item and no water-like item" holds for the four containers looted and for the driver's `FOOD_RX`/`WATER_RX`, not for the house. It changes nothing for the gate: **no container square in reach holds both a Food-class item and a fluid-container item** (the four candidate squares above each hold one kind), and a row completes only when `loot … food` and `loot … water` both end `done` on its one pick, so on this fixture `completed` reads 0 whatever the pick — and with pick exclusion only two squares could ever pass `water`. The defect is in the **measure on this fixture** (the step order demands two loots from one container; agent0's kitchen holds no clean water in a container and no edible food beside a drinkable one), helped by the **pick** (a fullType regex that names none of the house's items).

**Cause 2 — the neighbour rule (`goto … stuck` and both `loot … not adjacent`, repetition 5).** Confirmed. The pick (10999, 9696) is a counter; `beside` chose (10998, 9697), sent as `goto 10998.5 9697.5`.
