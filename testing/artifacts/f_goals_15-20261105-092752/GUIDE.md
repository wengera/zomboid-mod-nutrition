## How to read it

A frame is a positional line of `state.log` in the schema's `frame` order (`pza1.py`), its delta groups merged from the frame before. A **loop second** is a difference of two `wallMs` (or of two `decision.log` `wall` values) on this host's one clock. A planner tick is one `decision.log` line, cited as line *n*; its frame is `agent.base.replay_decisions.align`'s, the frame whose `me.hour` equals the line's `inputs.hour` (one frame on every one of the 1680 lines). `inputs.threats` counts the zombies within `DANGER_R` 6 and `inputs.nearest` the nearest held; the threat branch's why sits at `inputs.branches.threat`, the goal branch's at `inputs.branches.goal`, the care branch's at `inputs.branches.care`. A line reading `holding` sent nothing and holds the running action it names. A send is matched to its `loop.actions` row by order: the 250 lines with `sent` true are the rows a2–a251 in turn (`replay_decisions.recorded_aids`, offset 1, each type checked against the act lines; the `equip` a1 is the kit's, the `stop` a252 the loop's). `loop.fights[k]` is fight *k* (31 rows, threat episodes, *k* from 0, the index `grade_f11.kills.per_fight` and `hits.by_fight` use; fight 30 has no end, the stop coming inside it); fight *k*'s lines are those whose `wall` falls between its `startS` and its end's `endS` from `loop.startPyMs`. A call is `goals.log` line *i*; call *i* is the goal row `loop.goals[i-1]` (28 of each, in order). A zombie's record is {`id`, `x`, `y`, `dist`, `hasTarget`, `crawler`, `health` (the client's copy), `onFloor`}; "read fallen" is `onFloor` true, "standing" `onFloor` null, "chasing" `hasTarget` true and "idle" `hasTarget` false. `me.dir` is the frame's facing as one of eight compass points (world x east, y south), and a zombie's **bearing** the compass point of the line from the character to it on the same frame. `me.attackers` is the frame's count of the zombies attacking the player; "read attacking" is a frame reading it 1 or more. A **hit** (ruling P28-M) is a run of frames reading `me.hitReaction` non-empty; a **wound** a rise of `body.scratched`, `body.bitten` or `body.bleeding`; `body.armPain` is the arms' summed pain (#4370's sum, the pain the melee log names). A **kill** is a `zombie_dead` event. The frame's `act` group is {aid, state, step}: `act.step` is the reflex's current step (PZA/1.8).

**A melee's steps** (the reflex's header in `PZAgent_StanceMelee.lua` at `967c7076`, code not readings): `stand`, `face` (a tick before the press), `swing`, `back` (after a strike that left the target standing), `shove`, `knock_wait` (after a knock-down), `floor` (the floor swing at a fallen target), `stomp_walk`, and `hit`, the step the reflex takes on any tick its own read of the player's hit reaction is non-empty (`reflex`'s first match, counted once a reaction in `hits`), read at the client's frame rate, not the frames' 10 Hz. A **swing's distance** is the progress line's `d` at its `swing` step's first line (the live `DistToProper` read, #4621); what a swing **did** is the next progress or done line's `hp` against the swing line's (lowered: below it; killed: `end` `dead`, `kill` true); a **knock-down** is the next line's `knockdowns` above the step's. A ladder strike's distance is its decision line's `dist`. The melee's **pain** is the `melee` {mode, pain} log on its send line, the frame's `body.armPain`; `STRAIN_HARD` is 20 (`policies.py`).
