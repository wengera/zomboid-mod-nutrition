## 1. What this repo is

This repository is an agent-facing reference for modding Project Zomboid Build 42; its first consumer is a realism nutrition mod — nutrients tracked mod-side beyond the vanilla macros, a rebalance of the vanilla food items, multiplayer first — whose tree is `mod/NutritionRevamp/` here.
[docs/areas/](docs/areas/) is the nutrition lens, [docs/platform/](docs/platform/) general modding knowledge, [docs/facts/](docs/facts/) the measured vanilla food and nutrition mechanics, [docs/reference/](docs/reference/) the claims register and its archive, the science register and the datasets, tools, experiments, jar notes, harness commands, wall map and artifacts register, and [docs/process/lessons.md](docs/process/lessons.md) the process pitfalls.
Every claim on a page carries a tag naming its row in [the register](docs/reference/claims.tsv), and the row carries the grade and the pointer to its evidence; [README.md](README.md) is the map and the tag grammar, [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md) the charter.
The pages state what the game does and what a mod can and cannot change; the mod's design is not in this tree, and a root file names pages, never claims.

## 4. Extending the reference

Why each rule exists: [process/lessons#register-and-deltas](docs/process/lessons.md#register-and-deltas).

- A claim is minted in the commit that lands its evidence, never later, and a row is never minted before its owner sentence.
- **A new measurement:** the run's JSON is copied byte-identical to `testing/artifacts/<run-id>/` with its reading guide `GUIDE.md`, a row in the artifacts register and its keys in `docs/reference/do-not-cite.csv` (empty allowed; a listed key is never cited); the same commit adds the register rows and the tagged owner sentences, green under the checker. Or the artifact lands in its own controller commit (its row, do-not-cite rows and re-anchor) and its rows follow at the task's mint in the same plan. The artifact-row commit re-runs the full checker and re-anchors every `repo:docs/reference/artifacts.md:` pointer its row shifts; an implementer who may not edit the register stages the artifact files and hands the commit to the controller.
- **Ids:** the next unused id across the register and its archive; the checker fails a duplicate or a gap; a parallel wave reserves an id block per task in its ledger before dispatch, unless its writers never touch the register and the controller mints serially from the delta files at the wave's close.
- **Settling an open row:** it keeps its id and goes `open -> settled` with its pointer and bound filled; the owner sentence takes the settled form, its line leaves `docs/areas/open-questions.md`, and its rows in `docs/reference/experiments.md` and the wall map's experiment table are marked `run <run-id>`; a reading that comes back unmeasured or trivial stays `open` with the run named in `bound`.
- **Superseding:** the row keeps its id and goes `superseded` with `successor`, and every page tagging it moves to the successor in the same commit; while live, its `repo:` pointers are checked like any row's (a moved line re-anchored, a removed one pinned to its last-holding commit); `claims_delta.py archive-superseded` moves superseded rows to `claims-archive.tsv`, where their evidence stays as written.
- **Retiring:** a row that is true but no longer worth a page sentence goes through the delta's `retire` op with a `reason` (its pointers pinned at HEAD, the reason and date appended to its bound, the row moved to the archive), only when no dial src, kernel comment, area rule, skill, guide or open-questions line cites it, it is not an artifact's only reading, and a reviewer agreed; a page tag naming an archived id fails the checker; run `python tools/reference_gen.py cited-by --write` after any retire.
- **A science number:** a value the mod ships rests on a `settled` row of `docs/reference/science.tsv`; a writer files rows in a part file the controller applies with `tools/science_delta.py`, and every settled row's citation is resolved against its record before minting ([reference/science](docs/reference/science.md)).
- **Who edits the register:** page writers never do; a new or changed claim is a delta file the controller applies with `tools/claims_delta.py`, and only the controller runs `claims_check.py --fix-tags`, once per wave close; the committer runs the checker `--staged` (§ 3), and an SDD reviewer re-runs it on the review package.
- **Deltas:** a `supersede` names its successor in the `successor` cell, and the add row may sit anywhere; a `status` row writes only `status` and `bound`, so a pass that changes a claim, pointer, grade or kind is `claims_delta.py apply` AND the controller's fill step, which copies only non-empty claim and pointer cells, and a verified apply re-reads one corrected claim before committing. A `status` row's bound starts with the register's current bound byte for byte, copied from `claims.tsv` as UTF-8 and never through a re-encoding console, and the controller diffs that prefix before the apply.
- **A trial apply** runs on a `git archive` copy of the tree with the repository's objects as alternates, never in the worktree against a scratch register; an apply that supersedes a row the guide cites runs the full pytest before its commit and re-points the guide in that commit.
- **Provisional tags:** the apply tool rewrites plain ones only; the controller rewrites a suffixed `[T4.1/M/n=1]` or `#T4.1` form by regex over the touched pages (`experiments.md` included), never the register or `docs/superpowers/`, matching `T<n>.<m>` before a `/` anywhere (combined brackets included), its `[##` cleanup matching tag brackets only.
- **A live row's text:** every number sits at a path its pointer names, unrounded beyond the sample; a mechanism read off the mod's own code goes in the bound as inference, never in the claim; a pointer never names a `do-not-cite` key, a parent of one or a verdict-level copy of one, and the reviewer checks wildcard keys and parents by hand, since the checker matches neither.
- **Live against study:** a live reading graded against the replay of its own ring shows the mechanics hold live; agreement with a study is the offline oracle's, and every report keeps the two apart; and a live row that names a study says in its bound that the match is by construction wherever a constant was fitted to it; wall-clock epoch-ms leaves are restricted by one wildcard sentence in the run's `GUIDE.md`, with `wildcard` on the pattern's line, never enumerated in `do-not-cite.csv`.
- **Rows on rules:** an added `rule` row rests on register rows (a C pointer copied from one, the bound `inference; a reading of <ids>`, the page line carrying the resting row's tag beside its own; a rule resting only on M rows takes a `repo:` pointer to the line carrying its clause); a `C/inference` row whose evidence is the mod's own line may own an area sentence on what a mod can do when the mod's line is its worked example; a rule copied from outside a page's `## Rules` is copied byte-identically and diffed by hand at review, since the checker's `rules-dup` reads `## Rules` lines only. A rule line edit re-syncs the skills that quote it in the same commit (the checker fails otherwise); a skill's quoted rule keeps the row's numbers, and the skill's own text carries none.
- **Pointers:** a line that only moved is re-anchored, a rename or moved call on a row whose claim holds is requoted live, and only a removed or rewritten line is pinned (`repo:<path>@<commit or tag>:<line> "<quote>"`, resolved through `git show`) to the last commit holding it; a task that changes a quoted line before the task rewriting the claim pins it, and the rewrite removes the pin; a line appended in place to keep pointers still is split onto its own line, re-anchored, at the plan's close cleanup. A `source` cell is harvest provenance, never renumbered when its doc is rewritten; a `docs/superpowers/` path in it resolves at the tag `program-record-v19`.

## 5. Harness rules

The instrument is [platform/harness](docs/platform/harness.md) (its [`## Procedure`](docs/platform/harness.md#procedure)); the command table is [reference/harness-commands](docs/reference/harness-commands.md); the incidents are [process/lessons#live-runs](docs/process/lessons.md#live-runs).

- **Booting:** `python testing/pzt doctor` before every boot; one live game session at a time, repository-wide; never `-safemode`; boot only with at least 12 GB of host memory free, read and ledgered before the boot, and close the host's other memory users first for a two-client session; split a driver longer than about 15 minutes of wall time into sessions, each with its own driver. A live boot never overlaps an offline edit under `mod/`; a live task waits for the tree to be quiescent, and an offline `mod/` task waits for the session to end.
- **Waiting:** a live driver runs in a foreground Bash call that waits on it with a polling loop, never `run_in_background`; a session longer than the 10-minute cap is launched once, detached, and polled across foreground calls, and the controller makes that launch itself after Angus approves it; a client never joins or relaunches under a near-stopped clock.
- **What boots:** a release profile boots the staged copy (`tools/release_pack.py stage`), never `mod/`; `pzt run`, `provision` and `scenario` lint every `path=` mod folder and stop on an ERROR before seeding; the experiment mods (`testing/experiments/TKX_*`) install only through test profiles, never into the fixture; an acceptance profile for the mod sets `Nutrition = false`; a sleep reading needs a profile setting `[server]` `SleepAllowed` and `SleepNeeded` true (the fixture's are false; the keys reach the ini at seed); a two-client run is one session, `pzt run` attaching the profile's `clients` one at a time, each ready before the next.
- **Clients:** a raising probe is gated on the server side with its profile's `[client] timeout` set low, `pzt run` is expected to fail and the grade comes off the artifact, since a release client answers through a raise and the `-debug` client parks ([platform/lua-platform#debug-break](docs/platform/lua-platform.md#debug-break)); a test client opens no helper dialog (`pzt` writes the Survival Guide and tutorial options false at every prepare and blanks the welcome message at seed; the info window and debug console stay as the fixture saved them); a stray `ProjectZomboid64.exe` predating a session is Angus's own client, never killed (`doctor` checks game Java only).
- **Harness changes:** a change (a new `TK.register`, a changed reply) lands before the run that uses it, in its own commit, with its comment block, `python tools/luabalance.py <lua files>` green (HEAD copies first, then the working tree) and `python tools/bus_inventory.py` regenerating the command table, and the next acceptance run is its smoke test; a harness Lua edit appends new sites at the end of its file, and one that shifts lines re-anchors every `repo:` pointer into that file in the same commit (rule 3b fails otherwise); a commit that changes the table's count line updates `#2808`'s claim and quote and `#1235`'s quote in the same commit (rule 3b fails otherwise), the one register edit a harness implementer may make. Kahlua rules for every harness and experiment Lua file: no `goto`, no `%d` on floats, no `#` on Java lists, and no `%g`/`%G` of a value that can be tiny (`tostring` instead; `kahlua_lint`'s `format-g` rule).
- **Setters:** `lua.setpath` (both sides) is the drivers' only setter of a plain Lua field reached from `_G` (`globalmoddata.setpath` assigns into a global modData table), `record.set` (server) the setter of a scalar in the mod's in-memory record by `lua.read`'s grammar, reaching the bracketed keys a dotted path cannot, and `lua.call` (both sides) the only caller of a plain Lua function, with up to four scalar arguments; there is no chunk runner.
- **Writing a driver:** it imports `testing/experiments/xlib.py` and starts its own lib from the latest one; its offline tests pass before its first boot, every grade has an offline case that fails it, and its pre-boot checks run offline against the real staged copy and a real run layout; it refuses any argument it does not define, tested statically and never by invoking it with a bad argument, and an implementer invokes a driver only to boot it; its run prefix and profile constants name its own file and profile, checked before its commit; a defect found in one copy is grepped across every unrun copy before the next boot.
- **Fake bus:** it answers the handler's real reply shapes, read off its `@reply` line or a recorded artifact, the empty-collection shapes included (`players` with nobody online is `{}`); a falsifier is also checked at the live fixture's own values.
- **Reads and events:** every set or setpath event is placed at the drain its read brackets (a before-snapshot equal to the drain's age counts as after); a store read precedes its write by at most one slow minute when the legacy mirror is on; each client read pairs with a server read in the same wall second, except a static field's pair, which may span up to 10 s (`PAIR_SLACK_S`) when two equal server reads bracket it; a setting is restored from the value read before setting it, never a reply's echo, and a check comparing two clocks names each one's unit and source; a server-log pattern excludes the driver's own probe names; a cadence gate is on writes per game minute, counter deltas, the mean dtM and a ceiling, never each minute's dtM.
- **Game state:** a character carrying a filled container logs the engine's auto-drinks off the ring's `fluids.autoDrop` as `tools/replay_live.py`'s `autodrop` events; a started timed action stops through a harness command that calls `ISTimedActionQueue.clear`, or the driver waits for the server's moving or exercise flag to clear before an idle window [lessons#rules]; a night driver polls `acute.winSleptH`, never `acute.sleptH`; a container filled before a drink is checked unsealed in the item scripts.
- **Predictions:** a pre-run prediction replays the live first sight as the server will see it, a fresh record's seed plate included, its stand-in starting from the fixture's own creation body; a prediction about a vanilla value is read off the jar or the data before the run.
- **Freezing:** a driver is never edited after its run (a post-run edit is a skew note); a grade or analysis script is frozen once its output is staged, committed or cited, a later defect fixed in a new regrade script with a new derived artifact (an edit before any output is a pre-run edit named in its commit); a grade or replay script broken at HEAD is never patched but re-run on a `git archive` of the build its artifact names, named in its guide; a broken HEAD replay test on an old ring is restored through a named `REPLAY_ONLY` overlay (composed in `PRE_*` bundles, replay host only, never shipped or read by the guide) proved exact against the last build holding the rule, or frozen on a `git archive` of the last build whose pins it held.
- **Replays:** a multi-day guide or study replay asserts its character stays alive and hydrated unless its series names the death, and a line ends at a death; an old live run's points are drawn only where the HEAD replay of its own inputs matches each drawn quantity at a tolerance with a stated source; a replay grade of a quantity that decays since an eat logs the eat's landing age or grades to the band its minute spans.
- **What is a run:** an aborted boot that took no reading is not one (its driver runs unchanged, its run folder is left, and its auto-copied `testing/artifacts/` folder is never staged and stays untracked until Angus moves or deletes it); a driver that crashes before any reading is not one either, and the one fix is allowed before the real run, named in the guide; a live implementer stopped after its sessions ended is not a re-run (a fresh agent checks its runs byte-identical against `testing/runs/` and writes them up offline, with no boot); a trivial, unmeasured or falsified reading is written as such, never re-run for a prettier number.
- **Artifacts:** a run's files are committed byte-identical under `testing/artifacts/<run-id>/` with its row in [reference/artifacts](docs/reference/artifacts.md); when the server log is a run's only evidence of an event, its `server-stdout.log` is committed byte-identical and cited by line; fixture caches (`testing/fixtures/default/cache/`) and `testing/runs/` are per-machine and gitignored. The controller ticks each session off the run sheet's session table in the ledger before dispatching the write-up.

- **Re-reviews:** a controller fix after a FAIL takes a scoped re-review, except a pointer-, comment- or tag-only edit the full checker and its gate verify, or the reviewer's own scratch-verified text applied verbatim with its probes landed as tests and diffed by the controller; a wording change to a claim always takes one; a small, spot-checked mapping-curator fix takes none when the close review re-samples the data; a deferred wording re-review is named in the ledger and passed item by item by the next whole-delta review before the mint, or, past the mint, by the close review before the push.
- **Fixes:** a verbatim fix names its target by file:line and old text, and the spot-check greps that the old text is gone; a checker miss is reproduced on a minimal case before a brief names its cause; a pending item closed by a no-change ruling writes its reason where the item lives (the row's bound, the open-questions settling cell, the close report), never only in the ledger; a commit named in a page, row or brief takes its plan label from `git log -1 --format=%s <sha>`.

- **Git:** commits are pathspec commits (`git commit -m "…" -- <paths>`), never `--amend`, with no Claude attribution and a succinct subject; implementers never push, and the controller pushes at a plan's close; a subagent never runs `git stash`, `checkout --`, `reset`, `restore`, `add -N`, `add -f` or any index-wide command in the shared worktree, and retries once after two seconds on `.git/index.lock`.
- **Scratch copies:** a mutation runs on a scratch copy (`git archive HEAD | tar -x -C <scratch>`), never on a tracked file restored afterwards; a copy that needs history is a standalone clone (`git clone --no-hardlinks`, origin removed) or an archive with the repository's objects as alternates, never a shell with `GIT_DIR` or any `GIT_*` variable aimed at the real `.git`; a test that runs git in a temporary directory takes `tools/tests/gitenv.scrubbed_env`; a subagent never installs a package into the host Python, naming it in its report instead.
- **Test runs:** the controller reads pytest's summary line and exit code from a saved output file, never a filtered `tail` or `grep` of a piped run; it reads the checker's findings count before committing, never through a grep in a chained command, and stops on a non-zero count; it runs a test in its own call and never chains a commit after it.
- **Unreleased:** no migration, store version, legacy-field drop or compatibility fallback for an older save is written, and any found is removed; a corrupt file heals to a fresh record; the rule lapses at Angus's Workshop upload, not at packaging. A mod never wraps a timed action's `new`; wrap `serverStart` and `serverStop` [lessons#rules]; Plan 19's hedonics rulings (T5-1 to T8-1) live in the hedonics rows' notes of `NR_Data_Dials.lua` and #4156-#4168's bounds, so a change to one is a dial tune or a ruling, never a code edit.
- **Refactor:** until the first release, every task that touches code refactors heavily as it goes (structure, names, files and modules, performance, abstraction, quality), judged by long-term maintainability; a brief asks for it and a review judges structure and performance beside correctness, under the guards: the golden re-recorded under a named change, pointers re-anchored or pinned in the same commit, Rule 6, and a bench before and after any hot-path change; a rename sweep touches present-tense text only.

- **Fits:** a fit carries stability checks in its oracle registry, scored like any oracle, that a flat or degenerate read fails (a response to a ±2 % input; a slow reading such as a fasting rise), and a fitted exponent or scale at or near a bound is reported before acceptance; its reviewer probes it for degeneracy (a cliff, a flat read, a constant at its bound) with each constant at ×0.95 and ×1.05 and a meal-size sweep, and a low loss is not acceptance; a weight fitted to one system's studies never drives a quantity another system reads, which reads the physical quantity at its own scale; the committed artifact is the fit's `--compact` projection (the chosen point, every oracle's value and miss, the per-parameter profile, the grid spec, the candidate count and the full `fit.json`'s sha256), the full file staying in `testing/runs/`; a named non-reproduction is pinned at its reading and asserted still outside its band.
